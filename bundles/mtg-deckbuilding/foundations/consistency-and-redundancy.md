# Definition

**Consistency** is how reliably the deck executes its plan. **Redundancy** is playing multiple cards (or tutors/cantrips) that fill the same role so variance does not strand you.

Curve thinking and source counts are consistency tools: enough cheap plays and enough colored sources so “on-plan” hands appear often.[^wiz-curve][^karsten-sources]

# Guidelines

* In 60-card Constructed, play **four** of cards central to the plan; flex one- and two-ofs only when roles are interchangeable.
* In singleton formats, redundancy means **role density** (many ramp pieces, many draw engines), not four copies.
* Cantrips, scry, and tutors raise consistency but cost slots and mana—budget them like any other role.
* Do not confuse “powerful singleton” with “reliable”: a one-of bomb that wins alone still needs enablers and a keepable mana base.

# Format notes

* **Limited**: Redundancy comes from commons/uncommons that share a role (combat tricks, 2-drops).
* **Commander**: Tutors and dense categories replace 4-ofs; see [Category Budgets](/commander/category-budgets.md).
