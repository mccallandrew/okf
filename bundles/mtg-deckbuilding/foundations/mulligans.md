# Definition

A **mulligan** is shipping an opening hand that cannot execute the plan. Good mulligan philosophy is deck-specific: aggro keeps low curves with lands; control keeps interaction plus enough lands; combo keeps enablers.

Mana bases are built assuming reasonable keeps—source counts and land totals presuppose mulligan rules for the format.[^karsten-sources][^wiz-mana]

# Guidelines

* Define a keep checklist: lands in range, early play (if required), and a path to the plan within a few draws.
* Avoid “hope” keeps that need a specific topdeck unless the deck is built for that (e.g. cantrip-dense).
* On the play vs draw, thresholds change—beatdown keeps may be looser on the play; control may demand interaction on the draw.[^flores-beatdown]
* Build the deck so **most** seven- and six-card hands are functional; if you mulligan constantly, fix the mana or curve.

# Format notes

* **Commander**: Free mulligan / London rules and higher starting life change keep ranges; still punish no-ramp no-land hands.
* **Limited**: Hands without two-drops or with uncastable splashes are frequent ships.
