# Definition

A player (or deck) has **inevitability** when, from the current position or on turn one of a matchup, they will win a sufficiently long game.[^flores-beatdown]

If you have inevitability, you want to survive and stabilize. If you lack it, you must win as the beatdown before the long game arrives.

# Guidelines

* Identify what creates inevitability: larger threats, recursive engines, decking, combo locks, or superior card advantage.
* Building “just one more expensive bomb” only helps if you can reach that stage—mana and answers must support it.
* Sideboarding can steal or concede inevitability (hate pieces, faster clocks, extra interaction).
* Mirror matches often hinge on who correctly claims beatdown when inevitability is nearly symmetric.[^flores-beatdown]

# Format notes

* **Commander**: Multiplayer inevitability is social and board-state dependent; infinite combos and mass resource engines change who “wins long.”
* **Limited**: Bombs and card advantage engines often define late-game inevitability.
