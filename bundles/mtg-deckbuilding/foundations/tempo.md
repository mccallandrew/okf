# Definition

**Tempo** is advantage in time and mana: developing your board (or disrupting theirs) so you are ahead on the clock. Spending all available mana productively is a classic tempo heuristic tied to curve construction.[^wiz-curve][^mm-curve]

Tempo decks win by applying a clock while answering only what would stop that clock.

# Guidelines

* Cheap threats plus cheap interaction preserve mana for both pressure and protection.
* A “tempo-positive” answer often costs less than the spell or permanent it answers (or buys a full turn).
* Flooding high on the curve creates negative tempo even with powerful cards.[^wiz-curve]
* When you are the beatdown, prioritize tempo over long-term card advantage.[^flores-beatdown]
