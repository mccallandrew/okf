# Definition

The **mana curve** is how many spells you want to cast at each point on the mana axis—usually organized by the turn you expect to play them, not only by printed mana value.[^wiz-curve]

A healthy curve lets you make land drops and spend mana productively; a clumped high curve leads to do-nothing early turns, while an all-cheap curve can lack late power.[^wiz-mana][^mm-curve]

# Guidelines

* Sort by **when you cast**, not only CMC: a six-mana board wipe may be a turn-six play; a two-mana combat trick is often off-curve.
* Prefer more cheap spells than expensive ones—you draw into expensive cards over time.[^wiz-curve]
* Protect the two-mana slot in most creature decks; avoid flooding four- and five-drops in opening hands.[^wiz-curve]
* Shape the curve to the [game plan](/foundations/game-plan.md): aggro ladders low; control parks answers and few expensive finishers.
