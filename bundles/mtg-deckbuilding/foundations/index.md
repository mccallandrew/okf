# Foundations

Universal deckbuilding and in-game strategy concepts:

* [Game Plan](game-plan.md) - What the deck is trying to do and how it wins.
* [Mana Curve](mana-curve.md) - Distributing spells by mana value to use available mana.
* [Consistency and Redundancy](consistency-and-redundancy.md) - Seeing the pieces you need often enough.
* [Threats and Answers](threats-and-answers.md) - Role density of threats versus interaction.
* [Card Advantage](card-advantage.md) - Generating and denying useful cards over time.
* [Tempo](tempo.md) - Time and mana as a race against the opponent's development.
* [Inevitability](inevitability.md) - Who wins the long game if neither player dies early.
* [Who's the Beatdown](whos-the-beatdown.md) - Assigning beatdown vs control roles in a matchup.
* [Mulligans](mulligans.md) - Opening-hand philosophy tied to the deck's plan.
