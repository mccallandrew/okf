# Definition

**Who’s the beatdown?** is the question of which player must apply pressure and which must defend. Roles are **contextual**—not identical to the archetype names on the decklists.[^flores-beatdown]

Misassigning the role (racing when you should stabilize, or turtling when you must race) is a classic losing pattern.[^flores-beatdown]

# Guidelines

* Heuristics: more damage → likely beatdown; more removal / permission / draw → likely control.[^flores-beatdown]
* The beatdown player prioritizes clock and tempo; the control player prioritizes surviving into [inevitability](/foundations/inevitability.md).
* Similar-deck matchups still force asymmetric roles unless the decks are truly mirrors.
* Sideboarding can reassign which role you can successfully play.[^flores-threat]

# Deckbuilding implications

* Maindeck threat density and answer density should support the roles you expect to take most often.
* Flex slots and the sideboard exist partly to fix role problems against specific decks.
