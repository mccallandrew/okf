# Overview

Companion piece to role theory: the active (often beatdown) deck presents **threats**; the control deck must find **answers** or lose. Framing construction around threat density, answer density, and the mana costs of each side.[^flores-threat]

# Key takeaways

* Aggressive decks win by exhausting answers or racing past them; they lose when forced into answering without enough tools.
* Control wins by answering efficiently and converting card advantage into a late win.
* Cost asymmetries matter: cheap threats vs expensive answers (and vice versa) define matchup texture.
* Sideboards often add answers for problem permanents or plans the maindeck cannot beat.
