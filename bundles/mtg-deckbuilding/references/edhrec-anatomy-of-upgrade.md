# Overview

EDHREC “Superior Numbers” article walking through concrete upgrade swaps: how to add new cards without randomly cutting basics, and how to classify the *kind* of change you are making.[^edhrec-upgrade]

Focuses on iterative tuning of an existing list rather than a first draft.

# Key takeaways

* Do not upgrade by cutting lands by default—pair adds with intentional cuts of comparable roles.
* Prefer **CMC-neutral or CMC-negative** net changes so average mana value does not creep up.
* Swap types include: match desired power level, replace under-performers, address a known weakness, “homework” tests, and chase over-performers.
* Prefer like-for-like cuts (creature for creature, artifact for artifact) when preserving deck balance.
* Different builders update at different cadences; the method matters more than a single “correct” timing.
