# Overview

Wizards primer covering two foundational deckbuilding ideas: the **mana base** (lands and other mana sources) and the **mana curve** (balance of cheap vs expensive spells). Frames land count and curve shape as strategy-dependent rather than fixed mystical numbers.[^wiz-mana]

# Key takeaways

* A deck of only expensive spells has nothing to do early; a deck of only one-drops runs out of power later.
* Aggressive creature decks skew very low; control and midrange keep early defense or cantrips without diluting late-game power.
* Land count adjusts with average mana value, ramp, card draw, and how often you need to hit early drops.
