# Overview

Quantitative look at **Commander** mana curves and how land count and mana rocks trade off against expected mana spent in the early game. Optimizes expected compounded mana over the first several turns under stated assumptions.[^karsten-edh]

Use as a calibration tool, not a mandate for every social deck.

# Key takeaways

* Curve shape and commander mana value strongly affect whether rocks help or hurt expected early development.
* Cheap commanders often want to curve out with spells; denser rock suites matter more for expensive commanders or ramp strategies.
* Community baselines (roughly mid-30s lands plus a ramp package) are a starting point; tune to average mana value and draw density.
