# Overview

Wizards of the Coast feature article on building a **mana curve**: the distribution of spells by the turn you expect to cast them, not merely by printed mana value. Emphasizes more cheap spells than expensive ones, the importance of two-drops, and separating on-curve casts from reactive spells.[^wiz-curve]

# Key takeaways

* Put cards on the curve where you will actually want to cast them.
* You generally need more copies of cheap spells than expensive ones because you draw into expensive cards over time.
* Two-drops are crucial for most decks; overcrowding the four- and five-mana slots slows opening hands.
* Noncreature interaction often sits “below” the creature curve as off-curve casts.
