# Overview

Foundational strategy essay arguing that in most matchups one player must act as **beatdown** and the other as **control**, independent of deck labels. Misassigning the role is a common path to losing.[^flores-beatdown]

# Key takeaways

* Beatdown tries to win as quickly as possible; control weathers pressure and wins with longer-term advantage.
* Relative speed, removal density, and card advantage tools help assign roles.
* Sideboarding can change which role your deck can successfully play.
* Applies inside aggro-vs-aggro and control-vs-control as much as cross-archetype.
