# Overview

Podcast episode (The Command Zone 658) presenting an updated **deckbuilding template**—target counts for lands, ramp, card advantage, targeted disruption, mass disruption, and remaining “plan” cards—so singleton lists reliably draw functional pieces early.[^cz-template]

Treat the numbers as a diagnostic baseline, not a rigid recipe for every archetype.

# Key takeaways

* Common 2025 template targets: roughly **~38 lands**, **~10 ramp**, **~12 card advantage**, **~12 targeted disruption**, **~6 mass disruption**, and **~30 plan cards**.
* Categories exist so you notice accidental shortfalls (e.g. “I thought I had removal”) rather than to forbid archetype deviations.
* Mana curve still matters: density at low-to-mid mana values; commander cost shapes how you spend early turns.
* Plan cards are the strategy—enablers, payoffs, and finishers—not leftovers after staples.
* Tune down (aggro) or up (expensive commanders, landfall) deliberately once you know why you deviate.
