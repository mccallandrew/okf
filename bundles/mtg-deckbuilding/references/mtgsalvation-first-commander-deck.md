# Overview

Step-by-step first-deck guide: pick a commander, build a mana base, then fill creatures, spells, and nonland permanents, finishing with basic lands by color-pip ratio.[^mtgs-first]

Recommends mono- or two-color for a first custom build; notes precon shortcuts for players who prefer not to scratch-build.

# Key takeaways

* Prefer **mono or two colors** for a first build; three-plus colors multiply manabase difficulty.
* Land count is deck-dependent (mid-30s common); include reliable fixing and a few utility lands early.
* Staple rocks (e.g. Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Command Tower) are common teaching inclusions—not universal mandates.
* Every list still needs enough removal, ramp, and card draw even when the theme is tribal or “fun.”
* Finish the manabase by weighting basics to the deck’s colored pip counts.
