# Overview

Draftsim guide by Timothy Zaccagnino compiling popular Commander (EDH) archetypes. Descriptors mix and match within the same decks; the article is not a ranking and not an optimality guide. Example lists highlight what *kinds* of cards to expect in each archetype.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

# Key takeaways

* [Ramp](/archetypes/commander/ramp/) — Accelerate mana early to cast oversized threats ahead of schedule.
* [Aggro](/archetypes/commander/aggro/) — Race opponents to zero life with a dense early curve and combat pressure.
* [Control](/archetypes/commander/control/) — Answer threats at instant speed, generate advantage, and win with inevitability.
* [Typal](/archetypes/commander/typal/) — Bias the creature base toward one type and stack typal payoffs.
* [Tokens/Go-Wide](/archetypes/commander/tokens-go-wide/) — Flood the board with creatures and finish with mass pump or convoke payoffs.
* [+1/+1 Counters](/archetypes/commander/plus-one-counters/) — Build around +1/+1 counters with proliferate, move, and counter payoffs.
* [Cascade / Discover](/archetypes/commander/cascade-discover/) — Cheat on mana by cascading or discovering into free spells.
* [Wheel](/archetypes/commander/wheel/) — Force mass discard-and-draw effects and profit from the refill.
* [Lifegain](/archetypes/commander/lifegain/) — Gain life as a resource and convert it into card advantage or damage.
* [Combo](/archetypes/commander/combo/) — Assemble a discrete combo win condition, often with tutors and protection.
* [Recursion/Graveyard](/archetypes/commander/graveyard/) — Treat the graveyard as a second hand via mill, recursion, and reanimation.
* [Landfall](/archetypes/commander/landfall/) — Lands-matter strategies that use lands beyond tapping for mana.
* [Aristocrats/Sacrifice](/archetypes/commander/aristocrats/) — Sacrifice creatures for value and drain or token loops.
* [Voltron](/archetypes/commander/voltron/) — Stack auras, equipment, and counters on one unstoppable attacker.
* [Group Hug](/archetypes/commander/group-hug/) — Share resources with the table while positioning for a late pivot.
* [Group Slug](/archetypes/commander/group-slug/) — Punish the whole table with symmetrical damage and taxing pain.
* [Chaos](/archetypes/commander/chaos/) — Maximize randomness and splashy effects for wild game states.
* [Blink/Flicker](/archetypes/commander/blink/) — Combine strong ETBs with flicker effects for repeated value.
* [Spellslinger](/archetypes/commander/spellslinger/) — Cast many instants and sorceries with magecraft-style payoffs.
* [Enchantress](/archetypes/commander/enchantress/) — Draw and win through a dense enchantment package.
* [Affinity/Artifacts](/archetypes/commander/artifacts/) — Artifact-matters engines, affinity-style cost reduction, and artifact payoffs.
* [Infect](/archetypes/commander/infect/) — Win with poison counters via infect/toxic creatures and proliferate.
* [Stax](/archetypes/commander/stax/) — Lock opponents under resource denial while advancing a lean win plan.
* [Theft Decks](/archetypes/commander/theft/) — Steal opponents' cards and use their best pieces as your win conditions.
* [Cast From Exile](/archetypes/commander/cast-from-exile/) — Impulse, plot, and exile-cast engines that play from exile.
* [Legends Matter/Historic](/archetypes/commander/legends-matter/) — Legendary- and historic-matters synergies across the 99.
* [Mill](/archetypes/commander/mill/) — Put opponents' libraries to zero while hedging against their graveyards.
