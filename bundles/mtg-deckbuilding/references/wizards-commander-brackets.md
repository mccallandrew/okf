# Overview

Wizards announcement (with the Commander Format Panel) of **Commander Brackets**—a shared language for matching social and competitive expectations, intended to replace vague “power level 1–10” talk. Defines five brackets and a curated **Game Changers** list that warps casual games.[^wiz-brackets]

Rule Zero still applies: pods can negotiate exceptions. The system is a matchmaking aid, not a tournament ruleset.

# Key takeaways

* **Brackets 1–3** are socially focused; **4–5** are high-power / competitive (cEDH mindset).
* **Game Changers** are legal but bracket-gated: none in brackets 1–2, up to three in bracket 3, unrestricted in 4–5.
* Brackets 1–3 generally exclude mass land denial; early two-card infinite combos and dense tutors/extra-turn chaining are restricted by bracket intent.
* Bracket 2 maps roughly to a modern precon; bracket 3 is upgraded beyond precon; bracket 5 is metagame-driven competitive play.
* Honesty and pregame discussion matter more than any calculator—intent defines the experience.
