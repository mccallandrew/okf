# Overview

MTGGoldfish Commander Quickie arguing that a **deckbuilding checklist**—minimum counts for mana, draw, interaction, and closers—catches incomplete lists before playtesting, then is tuned per strategy and budget.[^goldfish-checklist]

Aimed at casual / semi-competitive decks that close around turns 8–12, not turn-3 cEDH shells.

# Key takeaways

* Treat the checklist as a diagnostic floor, not a rigid recipe; combine with playtesting and cards you enjoy.
* Budget baseline: **~50 mana** (often **37 lands + 13 ramp**), **≥10** card draw, **≥6** targeted removal, **~3** board wipes, **~2** recursion, **~2** flexible tutors, **≥1** graveyard hate, **≥1** surprise “I win” closer.
* Ramp should be sized to what you ramp *into* (engine CMC), not generic “more rocks.”
* Tutors raise consistency and let you trim weaker redundant copies; budget lists need cheap tutors; unlocked budgets can run many more.
* Under-manning lands to jam “fun” cards is the most common failure mode the checklist exists to catch.
