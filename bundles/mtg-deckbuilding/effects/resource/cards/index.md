# Cards

Oracle tags under **Cards** (`resource/cards`).

* [Cards](overview.md) - Card-flow and card-advantage patterns that are not pure draw.
* [Cantrip](cantrip.md) - Cards that let you draw a card as they resolve or enter the battlefield.
* [Card Advantage](card-advantage.md) - Things that give you access to more cards. (The technical meaning of card advantage can be much broader than this, but this is what we're using here.)
* [Catalog](catalog.md) - Draw two cards, then discard a card.
* [Compulsive Research](compulsive-research.md) - Cards with a function of "draw N cards, then discard 2 unless you discard [type]"
* [Consult](consult.md) - Dig through your library until you find a card meeting some criteria.
* [Consult-Cast](consult-cast.md) - Consult effects that let you cast the card you find.
* [Drawback](drawback.md) - Cards that have some kind of disadvantage.
* [Future Sight Engine](future-sight-engine.md) - Cards that reveal the top card of your library and conditionally play it. See also [precognition engine](/tags/card/precognition-engine) for a more recent variant in this vein.
* [Greed Ability](greed-ability.md) - Pay life and mana to draw cards.
* [High Flying](high-flying.md) - Flying creatures that can only block other flying creatures. See also [gives high flying](gives-high-flying).
