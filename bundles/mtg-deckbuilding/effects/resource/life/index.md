# Life

Oracle tags under **Life** (`resource/life`).

* [Life](overview.md) - Gaining or paying life as a resource.
* [Alternate-Cost-Gain-Life](alternate-cost-gain-life.md) - Cards that let you give your opponents life to play them for less or no mana
* [Alternate-Cost-Life](alternate-cost-life.md) - Cards that let you pay life to play them for less or no mana
* [Blood Artist Ability](blood-artist-ability.md) - Cards that have abilities along the lines of: "Whenever creature dies target/each opponent loses 1 life (and you gain 1 life)."
* [Boltland](boltland.md) - Lands you can pay 3 life/take 3 damage to have them enter untapped.
* [Drain Life](drain-life.md) - Hurt your opponent and gain life to match.
* [Fog](fog.md) - Effects that can prevent all or most of the damage from an entire combat, similar to the card Fog. See also [pseudo-fog](https://tagger.scryfall.com/tags/card/pseudo-fog).
* [Fog-Selective](fog-selective.md) - Fog effects where the damage prevention is or has the potential to be asymmetrical in some way, usually by exempting a subset of attackers/defenders.
* [Gainland](gainland.md) - Lands that have you gain life when they enter the battlefield.
* [Gains Lifelink](gains-lifelink.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gains lifelink.
* [Gives Extort](gives-extort.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives extort.
* [Gives Lifelink](gives-lifelink.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives lifelink.
* [Gives Lifelink Noncreature](gives-lifelink-noncreature.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives lifelink noncreature.
* [Life Payment](life-payment.md) - Cards that cost life to use; not necessarily with the "pay" keyword.
* [Lifegain](lifegain.md) - Cards that cause you to gain life.
* [Old Lifelink](old-lifelink.md) - Before lifelink was keyworded in Magic 2010, it was a triggered ability.
* [Painland](painland.md) - Lands that deal damage to the user upon tapping them for mana. For the original cycle of painlands, see [cycle-painland](cycle-painland) or [is:painland](https://scryfall.com/search?q=is%3Apainland).
* [Phyrexian Mana](phyrexian-mana.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: phyrexian mana.
* [Phyrexian Mana Ability](phyrexian-mana-ability.md) - Cards whose activated abilities cost phyrexian mana
* [Repeatable Food](repeatable-food.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: repeatable food.
* [Repeatable Lifegain](repeatable-lifegain.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: repeatable lifegain.
* [Shockland](shockland.md) - Lands which enter tapped unless you pay two life.
* [Soul Warden Ability](soul-warden-ability.md) - Creatures entering the battlefield causes you to gain N life.
