# Permanent Effects

Effects that create, remove, or change permanents on the battlefield.

Child categories and any mid-level tags:

* [Permanent Effects](overview.md) - Effects that create, remove, or change permanents on the battlefield.
* [Create](create/) - Creating tokens or new permanents.
* [Destroy](destroy/) - Destroy-mode answers (when not filed under interaction/removal).
* [Sacrifice](sacrifice/) - Sacrifice outlets and sacrifice payoffs.
* [Blink](blink/) - Short exile-and-return (flicker) for ETB reuse. Not haven-style long storage and not banish.
* [Transform](transform/) - Transform and face-up/face-down style effects.
* [Modify](modify/) - Counters, type/text/color changes, animate, and residual granted abilities/stats. Prefer routing keyword grants by role (combat, protection, spell) before landing here; gains vs gives share a leaf.
* [Control](control/) - Changing or stealing control of permanents.
* [Tap](tap/) - Tapping, untapping, freeze, and lockdown.
