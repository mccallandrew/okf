# Modify

Oracle tags under **Modify** (`permanent/modify`).

* [Modify](overview.md) - Counters, type/text/color changes, animate, and residual granted abilities/stats. Prefer routing keyword grants by role (combat, protection, spell) before landing here; gains vs gives share a leaf.
* [Alternative Crewing](alternative-crewing.md) - Vehicles with crew that also have alternative ways for self-animation. For vehicles without crew see [crewless vehicle](crewless-vehicle).
* [Animate](animate.md) - Effects that turn things into creatures.
* [Animate Artifact](animate-artifact.md) - Cards that can turn other noncreature artifacts into creatures. See also [animate vehicle](/tags/card/animate-vehicle).
* [Animate Battle](animate-battle.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: animate battle.
* [Animate Enchantment](animate-enchantment.md) - Cards that can turn other noncreature enchantments into creatures.
* [Animate Instant](animate-instant.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: animate instant.
* [Animate Land](animate-land.md) - Cards that can turn other noncreature lands into creatures.
* [Animate Planeswalker](animate-planeswalker.md) - Effects that turn planeswalkers into creatures.
* [Animate Self](animate-self.md) - Noncreatures that can become creatures. For creatures that can stop being creatures, see [deanimate self](deanimate-self).
* [Animate Sorcery](animate-sorcery.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: animate sorcery.
* [Animate Token](animate-token.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: animate token.
* [Animate Vehicle](animate-vehicle.md) - Cards that can turn other vehicles into creatures (other than by crewing them), permanently or temporarily. See also [animate artifact](/tags/card/animate-artifact).
* [Any Zone Color Change](any-zone-color-change.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: any zone color change.
* [Any Zone Type Change](any-zone-type-change.md) - These cards change types in every and any zone.
* [Armoring](armoring.md) - A toughness-pumping ability. See also [firebreathing] in red and [shade pump] in black.
* [Artifactify](artifactify.md) - Effects that turn things into artifacts.
* [Auraify](auraify.md) - Effects that turn things into auras.
* [Auto Equip](auto-equip.md) - Equipments that attach themselves to other creatures as they enter the battlefield.
* [Awaken](awaken.md) - Collection tag used to apply the various tags that comprise the keyword Awaken. Should be 1-1 with kw:awaken.
* [Becomes Changeling](becomes-changeling.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: becomes changeling.
* [Color Change](color-change.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: color change.
