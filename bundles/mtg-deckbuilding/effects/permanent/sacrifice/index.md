# Sacrifice

Oracle tags under **Sacrifice** (`permanent/sacrifice`).

* [Sacrifice](overview.md) - Sacrifice outlets and sacrifice payoffs.
* [Alternate-Cost-Sacrifice](alternate-cost-sacrifice.md) - Cards that let you sacrifice permanents to play them for free/a reduced amount of mana
* [Bombard](bombard.md) - Sacrifice something else to deal N damage. See also [fling](/tags/card/fling), [bombard-self](bombard-self).
* [Buttfling](buttfling.md) - Fling, but using toughness for damage.
* [Devour](devour.md) - Before a creature enters the battlefield, the player may sacrifice a number of permanents and the creature will enter with +1/+1 counters on it in relation to the number of permanents sacrificed.
* [Emerge](emerge.md) - Creatures that let you sacrifice something to cast them at a cost reduced by the mana cost of the creature sacrificed
* [Emerge-From-Artifact](emerge-from-artifact.md) - Cards where you can sacrifice an artifact and subtract its cost from the mana you pay
* [Emerge-From-Creature](emerge-from-creature.md) - Cards where you can sacrifice a creature and subtract its cost from the mana you pay
* [Free Sacrifice Outlet](free-sacrifice-outlet.md) - Ways to sacrifice a permanent repeatedly that require no additional cost.
* [Gives Casualty](gives-casualty.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives casualty.
* [Opponent Sacrifice Matters](opponent-sacrifice-matters.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: opponent sacrifice matters.
* [Plunder](plunder.md) - Sacrifice something else to draw cards.
* [Repeatable Plunder](repeatable-plunder.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: repeatable plunder.
* [Repeatable Sacrifice Outlet](repeatable-sacrifice-outlet.md) - Repeatedly sacrifice stuff.
* [Sacrifice Matters](sacrifice-matters.md) - Cards that give you some kind of benefit for sacrificing stuff, or for making your opponent sacrifice stuff.
* [Sacrifice Outlet](sacrifice-outlet.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: sacrifice outlet.
* [Sacrifice Outlet-Artifact](sacrifice-outlet-artifact.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: sacrifice outlet-artifact.
* [Sacrifice Outlet-Creature](sacrifice-outlet-creature.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: sacrifice outlet-creature.
* [Sacrifice Outlet-Enchantment](sacrifice-outlet-enchantment.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: sacrifice outlet-enchantment.
* [Sacrifice Outlet-Land](sacrifice-outlet-land.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: sacrifice outlet-land.
* [Sacrifice Outlet-Nonland](sacrifice-outlet-nonland.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: sacrifice outlet-nonland.
* [Sacrifice Outlet-Permanent](sacrifice-outlet-permanent.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: sacrifice outlet-permanent.
* [Sacrifice Outlet-Planeswalker](sacrifice-outlet-planeswalker.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: sacrifice outlet-planeswalker.
* [Sacrifice Outlet-Token](sacrifice-outlet-token.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: sacrifice outlet-token.
* [Your Sacrifice Matters](your-sacrifice-matters.md) - Effects that reward you for sacrificing your own permanents. Contrast [opponent sacrifice matters](opponent-sacrifice-matters).
