# Tax

Oracle tags under **Tax** (`interaction/tax`).

* [Tax](overview.md) - Cost-increasing and stax-adjacent taxes.
* [Cast Tax](cast-tax.md) - When a spell is cast, something happens unless the player casting the spell pays a cost, usually mana.
* [Opaline Effect](opaline-effect.md) - Whenever your stuff becomes the target of a spell and/or ability, you draw a card.
* [Rhystic](rhystic.md) - Effects that opponents can buy off if they pay mana.
* [Tax](tax.md) - Extract resources from your opponents when they try to do stuff, either by making them spend extra resources or by generating them for yourself.
* [Toll](toll.md) - Generate resources from your opponent's actions without them being able to pay it off.
