# Removal

Oracle tags under **Removal** (`interaction/removal`).

* [Removal](overview.md) - Getting permanents off the board (including banish/o-ring holds and removal-tuck).
* [Abrade](abrade.md) - Modal instant or sorcery spells that offer the option to either deal damage to creature(s) or remove artifact(s).
* [Abyss](abyss.md) - Recurring effects that make players sacrifice, destroy or otherwise remove permanents, usually creatures, every turn.
* [Arena Effect](arena-effect.md) - You and an opponent pick a creature you own, then they fight! (Or kiss, in the tunnel of love)
* [Banish](banish.md) - Exile things as long as this permanent remains.
* [Banish-Graveyard](banish-graveyard.md) - Exile one or more cards from graveyards as long as this remains in play. Often used as roundabout recursion.
* [Banish-Hand](banish-hand.md) - Exile one or more cards from a player's hand as long as this remains in play.
* [Burn Any](burn-any.md) - Cards that burn any target: players, creatures, battles, or planeswalkers.
* [Burn Creature](burn-creature.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: burn creature.
* [Burn Planeswalker](burn-planeswalker.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: burn planeswalker.
* [Burn-Self](burn-self.md) - Creatures deal damage to themselves.
* [Disenchant/Naturalize](disenchant-naturalize.md) - Cards that remove artifacts and/or enchantments.
* [Doom Blade](doom-blade.md) - 2 MV "Destroy target creature, unless it's X"
* [Drain Creature](drain-creature.md) - Hurt a creature and gain life to match.
* [Man-O'-War](man-o-war.md) - Creatures that can bounce your opponent's stuff when they enter the battlefield.
* [Marauder](marauder.md) - Enters and causes all players to sacrifice a creature.
* [Multi Removal](multi-removal.md) - Removal for more than one, but less than all. See also [spot removal](spot-removal) and [sweeper](sweeper).
* [Mutual Sacrifice](mutual-sacrifice.md) - Everyone sacrifices something.
* [No Mercy](no-mercy.md) - Cards that destroy creatures that damage you.
* [Outnumber](outnumber.md) - Deal damage to a creature based on your creature count.
* [Pacifism](pacifism.md) - A form of removal that prevents a creature from attacking, and usually also from blocking and/or activating its abilities.
* [Removal](removal.md) - Get things off the table.
* [Removal-Artifact](removal-artifact.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-artifact.
* [Removal-Aura](removal-aura.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-aura.

* [Removal-Burn](removal-burn.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-burn.
* [Removal-Creature](removal-creature.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-creature.
* [Removal-Destroy](removal-destroy.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-destroy.
* [Removal-Enchantment](removal-enchantment.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-enchantment.
* [Removal-Equipment](removal-equipment.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-equipment.
* [Removal-Exile](removal-exile.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-exile.
* [Removal-Fight](removal-fight.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-fight.
* [Removal-Land](removal-land.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-land.
* [Removal-Noncreature](removal-noncreature.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-noncreature.
* [Removal-Nonenchantment](removal-nonenchantment.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-nonenchantment.
* [Removal-Nonland](removal-nonland.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-nonland.

* [Removal-Planeswalker](removal-planeswalker.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-planeswalker.
* [Removal-Sacrifice](removal-sacrifice.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-sacrifice.
* [Removal-Spacecraft](removal-spacecraft.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-spacecraft.
* [Removal-Token](removal-token.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-token.
* [Removal-Toughness](removal-toughness.md) - Removal that can kill creatures by reducing their toughness to 0 or less.
* [Removal-Tuck](removal-tuck.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-tuck.
* [Removal-Vehicle](removal-vehicle.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: removal-vehicle.
* [Repeatable Removal](repeatable-removal.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: repeatable removal.
* [Spot Removal](spot-removal.md) - Removal for one target specifically. See also [sweeper](sweeper) and [multi removal](multi-removal).
* [Sunder](sunder.md) - Remove everything attached to a permanent while possibly leaving the permanent behind. (Some of these taggings consider the permanent might be indestructible.)
* [Sweeper](sweeper.md) - _o\ Destroy all the things! See also [spot removal](spot-removal) and [multi removal](multi-removal).
* [Sweeper-One-Sided](sweeper-one-sided.md) - Sweepers that are, or have high potential to be, one-sided.
