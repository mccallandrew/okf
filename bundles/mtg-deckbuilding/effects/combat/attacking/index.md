# Attacking

Oracle tags under **Attacking** (`combat/attacking`).

* [Attacking](overview.md) - Attack triggers and attacking-matters payoffs.
* [Affinity For Attacking](affinity-for-attacking.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: affinity for attacking.
* [Attack Trigger](attack-trigger.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: attack trigger.
* [Attacking Matters](attacking-matters.md) - Cards that care about you attacking.
* [Attacking Matters-Any](attacking-matters-any.md) - Cards that care about anyone attacking.
* [Attacking Matters-Self](attacking-matters-self.md) - Cards that care about themselves attacking.
* [Attacking Opponents Matters](attacking-opponents-matters.md) - Cards that synergize with any attacker, as long as they're attacking an opponent of the card's controller.
* [Battalion](battalion.md) - Attacking with at least two other creatures matters.
* [Battle Cry](battle-cry.md) - Whenever this attacks, each other attacking creature gets +1/+0 until end of turn.
* [Curiosity](curiosity.md) - Draw cards when dealing damage to an opponent
* [Curiosity-Like](curiosity-like.md) - Cards that can give you some form of card advantage upon dealing damage, usually combat damage, to a player.
* [Drawlink](drawlink.md) - Effects that cause combat damage dealt to draw that many cards.
* [Gains Battle Cry](gains-battle-cry.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gains battle cry.
* [Gives Drawlink](gives-drawlink.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives drawlink.
* [Gives Firebending](gives-firebending.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives firebending.
* [Omnivore Ability](omnivore-ability.md) - A creature's combat damage effectively hits multiple opponents.
* [Quietus Effect](quietus-effect.md) - Get hit and lose a sizeable fraction of your life.
* [Raid](raid.md) - Cards that have an upside if you attacked this turn.
* [Renown](renown.md) - Collection tag used to apply the various tags that comprise the keyword Renown. Should be 1:1 with kw:renown.
* [Saboteur](saboteur.md) - Effects that trigger when you deal damage to an opponent.
* [Slith Ability](slith-ability.md) - Whenever a creature deals combat damage to a player, it gets a +1/+1 counter.
* [Titan Trigger](titan-trigger.md) - An ability that triggers on both entering the battlefield and attacking, similar to the Titan cycle from M11
