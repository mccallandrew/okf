# Library

Oracle tags under **Library** (`zone/library`).

* [Library](overview.md) - Mill, scry, surveil, peek, hand→library outlets (tuck-outlet), and other library manipulation. Battlefield→library removal/utility is zone/battlefield-to-library.
* [Bottom Deck Manipulation](bottom-deck-manipulation.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: bottom deck manipulation.
* [Brainstorm](brainstorm.md) - Draw a number of cards, then put cards from your hand on top of your library in any order.
* [Fateseal](fateseal.md) - The ability to 'scry' another player's library instead of your own. Variants of this ability may allow you to choose between your own library and that of another player.
* [Grind](grind.md) - Mill cards until N land cards are milled.
* [Ingest](ingest.md) - Permanents that exile one or more cards from the top of an opponent's library upon dealing combat damage.
* [Library Manipulation](library-manipulation.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: library manipulation.
* [Loot To Library](loot-to-library.md) - Draw, then shuffle cards into your library or put them on the bottom of it. Contrast [brainstorm](brainstorm).
* [Mill](mill.md) - A keyword action used in Magic to describe the action of a player taking cards from the top of their library and putting them into their graveyard.
* [Mill-Any](mill-any.md) - Cards that let you choose a library to mill. Could be yours, could be theirs. It could also be an indirect choice, e.g. milling the controller of a targeted permanent.
* [Mill-Each](mill-each.md) - Cards that mill each player simultaneously, putting cards from top of their library to their graveyards.
* [Mill-Exile](mill-exile.md) - Exile cards from the top of a library.
* [Mill-Opponent](mill-opponent.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: mill-opponent.
* [Mill-Self](mill-self.md) - Cards that put cards from top of your own library to the graveyard. Check the tag "mill" for more.
* [Peek](peek.md) - Cards that give you access to hidden information (Yours or other players)
* [Peek-Face-Down](peek-face-down.md) - Cards that allow you to look at face-down creatures you don't control.
* [Peek-Hand](peek-hand.md) - Take a quick look at a players hand
* [Peek-Library](peek-library.md) - Look at some number of cards from the top of libraries
* [Precognition](precognition.md) - Cards that let you freely look at the top card of your library. You know, others don't.
* [Restock-To-Bottom](restock-to-bottom.md) - Put cards from your graveyard on the bottom of your library.
* [Restock-To-Top](restock-to-top.md) - Put cards from your graveyard on top of your library.
* [Rummage To Library](rummage-to-library.md) - Rummage with the discarded cards tucked in your library.
* [Scry](scry.md) - See also [scry-like](scry-like) for effects that are like scry, but don't use the keyword.
* [Scry Like](scry-like.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: scry like.
* [Soothsaying](soothsaying.md) - Look at the top N cards of a library and rearrange them.
* [Surveil](surveil.md) - Cards that let you look at the top # cards of your library and put some into the graveyard.
* [Take The Initiative](take-the-initiative.md) - Cards that take the initiative, allowing you to enter Undercity and venture further into it on future turns.
* [Thoughtseize](thoughtseize.md) - Opponent reveals their hand, and you choose a card for them to discard.
* [Top Deck Manipulation](top-deck-manipulation.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: top deck manipulation.
* [Tuck-Outlet](tuck-outlet.md) - Cards that let you put cards from your hand into your library.
* [Tutor-To-Top](tutor-to-top.md) - Cards that tutor something and put it on the top of the library.
