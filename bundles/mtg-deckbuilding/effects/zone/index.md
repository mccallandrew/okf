# Zone Effects

Effects defined by moving or manipulating cards across zones.

Child categories and any mid-level tags:

* [Zone Effects](overview.md) - Effects defined by moving or manipulating cards across zones.
* [Draw](draw/) - Drawing cards from the library.
* [Search](search/) - Library search (tutors) and wishes. Typal tutor filters stay Related, not separate leaves.
* [Graveyard To Hand](graveyard-to-hand/) - Returning cards from graveyard to hand (not cast-from-graveyard permission).
* [Graveyard To Battlefield](graveyard-to-battlefield/) - Reanimating or cheating permanents from graveyard to battlefield.
* [Exile](exile/) - Exile-centric movement and payoffs—friendly delayed holds (haven/purgatory), imprint/reference exile, and unexile. Hostile o-ring holds are interaction/removal (banish); short flicker loops are permanent/blink.
* [Hand](hand/) - Discard, hand disruption, and discard outlets.
* [Library](library/) - Mill, scry, surveil, peek, hand→library outlets (tuck-outlet), and other library manipulation. Battlefield→library removal/utility is zone/battlefield-to-library.
* [Battlefield To Hand](battlefield-to-hand/) - Bounce and similar returns to hand. Not tuck (library).
* [Battlefield To Library](battlefield-to-library/) - Tuck—put a spell or permanent into its owner's library. Distinct from bounce.
* [Cheat Into Play](cheat-into-play/) - Put permanents onto the battlefield without casting (sneak, Show and Tell, and similar). Not cost-reduction and not cast-from-nonhand.
