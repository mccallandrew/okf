# Cheat Into Play

Oracle tags under **Cheat Into Play** (`zone/cheat-into-play`).

* [Cheat Into Play](overview.md) - Put permanents onto the battlefield without casting (sneak, Show and Tell, and similar). Not cost-reduction and not cast-from-nonhand.
* [Consult-Onto-Battlefield](consult-onto-battlefield.md) - Consult effects that put the card you find onto the battlefield.
* [Impulse-Onto-Battlefield](impulse-onto-battlefield.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: impulse-onto-battlefield.
* [Show And Tell](show-and-tell.md) - Each player puts stuff from their hand onto the battlefield.
* [Sneak](sneak.md) - Put a permanent from your hand onto the battlefield. (With or without a requirement to sacrifice it later.) Not related to the Sneak keyword first seen in TMT.
* [Sneak From Command Zone](sneak-from-command-zone.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: sneak from command zone.
