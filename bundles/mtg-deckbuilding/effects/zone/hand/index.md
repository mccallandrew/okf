* [Discard To Exile](discard-to-exile.md) - Put cards from the opponent's hand into exile, either permanently or by [banishing](banish-hand) them.
* [Discard To Library](discard-to-library.md) - Put cards from the opponent's hand back into their library.
* [Discard-Symmetrical](discard-symmetrical.md) - Force each player to discard cards without drawing back up. See also [wheel-symmetrical](wheel-symmetrical).
* [Free Discard Outlet](free-discard-outlet.md) - Ways to discard repeatedly that require no additional cost or numerical limit.
* [Hand Disruption](hand-disruption.md) - Mess with your opponent's hand.
* [Random Discard](random-discard.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: random discard.
* [Specter Ability](specter-ability.md) - Damaging the opponent strips cards out of their hand.
* [Torment](torment.md) - A stock punisher effect where you force an opponent to choose between discarding, sacrificing, or losing life.
