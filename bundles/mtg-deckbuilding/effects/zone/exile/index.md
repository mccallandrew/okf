# Exile

Oracle tags under **Exile** (`zone/exile`).

* [Exile](overview.md) - Exile-centric movement and payoffs—friendly delayed holds (haven/purgatory), imprint/reference exile, and unexile. Hostile o-ring holds are interaction/removal (banish); short flicker loops are permanent/blink.
* [Haven](haven.md) - Exile your permanents to return them at much later point, essentially storing them somewhere safe. Not to be confused with [flicker](/tags/card/flicker) or [banish](banish).
* [Imprint](imprint.md) - Cards that exile other cards (or themselves) to use as reference marker for another effect.
* [Imprinted Card Types Matter](imprinted-card-types-matter.md) - Cards that care about the card types of other cards that they've exiled with a linked ability.
* [Purgatory](purgatory.md) - When your permanents die, they're stored in a holding area where they can come back after a delay.
