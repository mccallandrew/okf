# Draw

Oracle tags under **Draw** (`zone/draw`).

* [Draw](overview.md) - Drawing cards from the library.
* [Burst Draw](burst-draw.md) - Card draw effects that can net you a bunch of cards at once. As a rule of thumb, to qualify for this tag, the card should let you draw at least three cards at a time and come out ahead by at least +1.
* [Draw](draw.md) - Spells and abilities that textually draw you cards. See also [pure draw](pure-draw).
* [Draw Engine](draw-engine.md) - Card draw effects that can net you additional cards repeatedly over time. Contrast [Burst Draw](burst-draw), which nets you multiple cards right away.
* [Enchantment Engine](enchantment-engine.md) - Whenever you cast an enchantment spell, draw a card.
* [Loot To Exile](loot-to-exile.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: loot to exile.
* [Pure Draw](pure-draw.md) - Spells and abilities that let you draw (a) card(s) without discarding other cards or sacrificing other permanents.
* [Sift](sift.md) - Draw three cards, then discard a card. Not to be confused with [compulsive research](compulsive-research) or [probe](probe).
