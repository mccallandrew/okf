# Recast

Oracle tags under **Recast** (`spell/recast`).

* [Recast](overview.md) - Casting again via zone-recast keywords (flashback-like).
* [Auto Buyback](auto-buyback.md) - Derived from the buyback keyword, this ability will automatically return a card to your hand after casting it usually after a condition is met.
* [Gives Escape](gives-escape.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives escape.
* [Gives Flashback](gives-flashback.md) - Gives the textual flashback effect. For cards that let you cast others out of the graveyard in general, check [gives castable from graveyard](gives-castable-from-graveyard).
* [Gives Harmonize](gives-harmonize.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives harmonize.
* [Gives Jump-Start](gives-jump-start.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives jump-start.
* [Gives Mayhem](gives-mayhem.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives mayhem.
* [Gives Rebound](gives-rebound.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives rebound.
