# Counter

Oracle tags under **Counter** (`spell/counter`).

* [Counter](overview.md) - Counterspells and stack denial.
* [Banish-Spell](banish-spell.md) - Exile a spell on the stack, and bring it back when this permanent goes away.
* [Counterspell](counterspell.md) - Spells that counter stuff. See child tags for variants on the behaviour; notably [counterspell-soft](/tags/card/counterspell-soft) which gives the opponent a buyout cost.
* [Counterspell-Ability](counterspell-ability.md) - Cards that counter activated/triggered abilities. NOT for abilities that counter things.
* [Counterspell-Artifact](counterspell-artifact.md) - Counterspells that call out artifacts specifically.
* [Counterspell-Aura](counterspell-aura.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: counterspell-aura.
* [Counterspell-Automatic](counterspell-automatic.md) - Cards that will automatically try to counter spells.
* [Counterspell-Battle](counterspell-battle.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: counterspell-battle.
* [Counterspell-Bounce](counterspell-bounce.md) - Counterspells that instead "counter" by bouncing the spell.
* [Counterspell-Creature](counterspell-creature.md) - Counterspells that call out creatures specifically.
* [Counterspell-Enchantment](counterspell-enchantment.md) - Counterspells that call out enchantments specifically.
* [Counterspell-Exile](counterspell-exile.md) - For counterspelling that exiles the spell.
* [Counterspell-Free](counterspell-free.md) - Counterspells that can be cast right now with no mana cost (even if they might incur some alternate/later cost).
* [Counterspell-Instant](counterspell-instant.md) - Counterspells that call out instants specifically.
* [Counterspell-Loyalty-Ability](counterspell-loyalty-ability.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: counterspell-loyalty-ability.
* [Counterspell-Noncreature](counterspell-noncreature.md) - Counterspells that call out noncreatures specifically.
* [Counterspell-Planeswalker](counterspell-planeswalker.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: counterspell-planeswalker.
* [Counterspell-Reusable](counterspell-reusable.md) - Counterspell spells and abilities you can use again and again.
* [Counterspell-Sacrifice](counterspell-sacrifice.md) - Counterspell abilities that require sacrifice as a cost.
* [Counterspell-Soft](counterspell-soft.md) - A "soft counterspell" gives the other player a chance to buy off the counter, such as with a mana payment.
* [Counterspell-Sorcery](counterspell-sorcery.md) - Counterspells that call out sorceries specifically.
* [Counterspell-Sweeper](counterspell-sweeper.md) - Counter ALL the things!
* [Counterspell-Tuck](counterspell-tuck.md) - Counter a spell by putting it into its owner's library.
* [Mana Leak Series](mana-leak-series.md) - Soft counterspells with mana value N that cost more than N to pay off
