# Spell Effects

Effects about casting, copying, recasting, costing, or countering spells.

Child categories and any mid-level tags:

* [Spell Effects](overview.md) - Effects about casting, copying, recasting, costing, or countering spells.
* [Cast](cast/) - Casting spells as part of resolution or from unusual timing.
* [Cast From Nonhand](cast-from-nonhand/) - Permission to cast or play cards from exile, graveyard, library, or other nonhand zones. Distinct from zone returns (recursion) and from flashback-style recast keywords when the deckbuilding job is castability.
* [Copy](copy/) - Copying spells or abilities.
* [Recast](recast/) - Casting again via zone-recast keywords (flashback-like).
* [Cost Reduction](cost-reduction/) - Reducing spell costs (cheaper than MV, discounts).
* [Cost Bypass](cost-bypass/) - Bypassing mana costs—free spells, static alternate costs that ignore MV. Put-into-play without casting is zone/cheat-into-play.
* [Counter](counter/) - Counterspells and stack denial.
