# Cast

Oracle tags under **Cast** (`spell/cast`).

* [Cast](overview.md) - Casting spells as part of resolution or from unusual timing.
* [Addendum](addendum.md) - Instant-speed cards that do something extra if you played them during your main phase.
* [Cast On Resolution](cast-on-resolution.md) - Spells and abilities that let you cast spells as part of their resolution.
* [Demilich Effect](demilich-effect.md) - Exiles cards from graveyard, but lets you cast copies of them. Essentially (usually) one-time recursion.
* [Expertise](expertise.md) - Effects that allow you to cast a card with a given mana value or less from your hand without paying its mana cost, usually in association with another effect.
* [Gives Adventure](gives-adventure.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives adventure.
* [Gives Cascade](gives-cascade.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives cascade.
* [Gives Foretell](gives-foretell.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives foretell.
* [Gives Madness](gives-madness.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives madness.
* [Gives Miracle](gives-miracle.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives miracle.
* [Gives Plot](gives-plot.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives plot.
* [Gives Ripple](gives-ripple.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives ripple.
* [Gives Suspend](gives-suspend.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives suspend.
* [Gives Warp](gives-warp.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives warp.
* [Madness](madness.md) - The specific keyword, madness (Used for applying specific subtags)
