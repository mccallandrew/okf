# Cost Bypass

Oracle tags under **Cost Bypass** (`spell/cost-bypass`).

* [Cost Bypass](overview.md) - Bypassing mana costs—free spells, static alternate costs that ignore MV. Put-into-play without casting is zone/cheat-into-play.
* [Cost Ignorer](cost-ignorer.md) - Cards that allow you to bypass mana costs. This could be by making the spell free or by setting a static cost that's independent of the original.
