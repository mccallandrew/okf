# Modal

Oracle tags under **Modal** (`synergy/modal`).

* [Modal](overview.md) - Modal spells and choices.
* [Charm](charm.md) - Modal spells where you pick one option out of three
* [Command](command.md) - Modal spells where you pick two options out of four
* [Confluence](confluence.md) - Modal spells where you pick three options out of three, with the ability to choose a mode multiple times
* [Modal](modal.md) - Spells which give you your choice from two or more distinct functions.
* [Modal Inverse Choices](modal-inverse-choices.md) - Spells where the modal options are: X, Mirror of X
* [Siege (modal)](siege-modal.md) - Named ongoing modal effect. Chosen when entering and tracked by players.
