# Triggered

Oracle tags under **Triggered** (`synergy/triggered`).

* [Triggered](overview.md) - Triggered abilities and enter/fall-style triggers.
* [Artifactfall](artifactfall.md) - Cards that care about an artifact entering the battlefield.
* [Berserk](berserk.md) - A creature gets a power boost, but will die this turn.
* [Block Trigger](block-trigger.md) - Effects that trigger on a creature blocking or becoming blocked.
* [Cast Trigger](cast-trigger.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: cast trigger.
* [Cast Trigger-Other](cast-trigger-other.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: cast trigger-other.
* [Cast Trigger-Self](cast-trigger-self.md) - Cards that have a trigger when they are cast
* [Cast Trigger-You](cast-trigger-you.md) - Whenever you cast X, do something.
* [Combat-Neutral Damage Trigger](combat-neutral-damage-trigger.md) - Creature abilities that trigger upon dealing any amount of damage, no matter if it was done in combat or not. Compare and contrast [saboteur](saboteur).
* [Creaturefall](creaturefall.md) - Cards that care about a creature entering the battlefield. Also known as Alliance in New Capenna.
* [Death Trigger](death-trigger.md) - Abilities that trigger from permanents being sent to a graveyard. See also [leaves trigger](leaves-battlefield-trigger).
* [Death Trigger Opponent](death-trigger-opponent.md) - Cards that trigger when an opponent's permanent is sent to a graveyard.
* [Death Trigger-Self](death-trigger-self.md) - Permanents with abilities that trigger when they die.
* [Delayed Cantrip](delayed-cantrip.md) - Draw a card next turn.
* [Delayed Trigger](delayed-trigger.md) - Create a triggered ability that may trigger later. Similar to a [reflexive trigger](reflexive-trigger).
* [Earthbend](earthbend.md) - Collection tag used to apply the various tags that comprise the keyword Earthbend. Should be 1:1 with kw:earthbend.
* [Enchantmentfall](enchantmentfall.md) - Cards that care about enchantments entering the batllefield.
* [Enters-And-Leaves-Trigger-Self](enters-and-leaves-trigger-self.md) - Permanents that have an effect that triggers when the permanent enters the battlefield or leaves the battlefield to any zone. Compare [life-and-death trigger](life-and-death-trigger-self).
* [Gains Vanishing](gains-vanishing.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gains vanishing.
* [Gives Evoke](gives-evoke.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives evoke.
* [Gives Persist](gives-persist.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives persist.
* [Gives Prowess](gives-prowess.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives prowess.
* [Gives Renown](gives-renown.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: gives renown.
* [Hunger](hunger.md) - Creatures dying to this one make this one grow stronger. Coined by Maro: http://markrosewater.tumblr.com/post/178364542148
* [Hunger Reanimation](hunger-reanimation.md) - Creatures this one kills come back into play under your control.
* [Hunger Trigger](hunger-trigger.md) - Abilities that trigger off other creatures dying to this one. Named after the more-specific Hunger ability.
* [Impact Effect](impact-effect.md) - Effects that have your creatures do ETB damage or life loss.
* [Intervening If Clause](intervening-if-clause.md) - At/When/Whenever [event], if [clause], [effect].
* [Leaves Battlefield Trigger](leaves-battlefield-trigger.md) - Cards that trigger on something leaving the battlefield. See also [death trigger](death-trigger).
* [Leaves Body Behind](leaves-body-behind.md) - Creatures that create one or more creature bodies when they die.
* [Leaves Graveyard Trigger](leaves-graveyard-trigger.md) - Cards that have triggers whenever a card (or cards) leave your graveyard
* [Leaves Trigger-Self](leaves-trigger-self.md) - Permanents with abilities that trigger when they leave the battlefield.
* [Legendfall](legendfall.md) - Cards that care about legendaries entering the battlefield.
* [Life-And-Death-Trigger-Self](life-and-death-trigger-self.md) - Permanents that have an effect that triggers when they enter the battlefield and when they die. See also [enters-and-leaves triggers](enters-and-leaves-trigger-self).
* [Lose Trigger](lose-trigger.md) - Cards that trigger when an opponent loses the game (as opposed a replacement effect for yourself).
* [Magecraft](magecraft.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: magecraft.
* [Mana Gorger](mana-gorger.md) - Cards that grow bigger when players cast spells.
* [Old Damage Deathtouch](old-damage-deathtouch.md) - Before the keyword was made evergreen in Magic 2010, deathtouch was sometimes a triggered ability based on damage. See also [old blocking deathtouch](old-blocking-deathtouch).
* [Permanentfall](permanentfall.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: permanentfall.
* [Persist](persist.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: persist.
* [Planeswalkerfall](planeswalkerfall.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: planeswalkerfall.
* [Reflexive Trigger](reflexive-trigger.md) - An ability that triggers based on actions taken earlier during a spell or ability's resolution.
* [Snowfall](snowfall.md) - Cards that care about snow permanents entering the battlefield, not the weather condition.
* [Splits On Death](splits-on-death.md) - Cards with abilities that create multiple creature tokens when a creature dies.
* [State Trigger](state-trigger.md) - Cards that use a state trigger, which is a particular and rare kind of trigger. A common one is "When you control no [X]s, sacrifice this creature".
* [Substance](substance.md) - Substance is an obsolete static ability that has no effect at all. It existed briefly only as a technical detail to support implementing a few cards in Magic Online's Mirage release.
* [Thingfall](thingfall.md) - Abilities that trigger when something (other than itself) enters the battlefield
* [Tokenfall](tokenfall.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: tokenfall.
* [Trigger From Exile](trigger-from-exile.md) - Abilities that trigger from inside exile.
* [Trigger From Graveyard](trigger-from-graveyard.md) - Abilities that trigger from inside the graveyard.
* [Triggered Ability](triggered-ability.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: triggered ability.
* [Triggers At Cleanup Step](triggers-at-cleanup-step.md) - Cards that trigger at the cleanup step, rather than what would usually be the end step.
* [Turn-Face-Up-Trigger](turn-face-up-trigger.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: turn-face-up-trigger.
* [Turn-Face-Up-Trigger-Self](turn-face-up-trigger-self.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: turn-face-up-trigger-self.
* [Unblocked Trigger](unblocked-trigger.md) - Scryfall oracle tag: unblocked trigger.
