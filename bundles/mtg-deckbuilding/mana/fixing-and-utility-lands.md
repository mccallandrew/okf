# Definition

**Fixing** lands produce multiple colors or find the color you need. **Utility lands** add a spell-like effect at the cost of sometimes entering tapped, costing life, or not making colored mana.

Good mana bases maximize untapped colored sources for early spells while still enabling the splash or utility the plan needs.[^wiz-mana][^karsten-sources]

# Guidelines

* Prioritize **untapped** sources for early pips; put tapped duals later in the curve or in decks that can afford them.
* Fetch lands improve consistency and thin the deck but cost life and slots—budget life against aggro. Even slow Onslaught-style fetches into budget duals smooth a base and leave an upgrade path.[^341-build]
* Multicolored lands do for mana what multicolored spells do for [pip distribution](/mana/pip-distribution.md)—one slot serving multiple colors.[^341-build]
* Bias extra sources toward **casting the commander** and any color-intensive payoff you expect on a specific turn.[^341-build]
* MDFC spell-lands can count partially toward land totals; treat them as flexible but not full lands in every draw.
* Utility lands that make only colorless can strand color-intensive hands—limit them in greedy bases.
* In Limited, prioritize basics and easy duals; splash only with enough sources and fixing.
