# Overview

[EDHREC](https://edhrec.com) aggregates public Commander lists to show high-synergy and high-inclusion cards for a given commander, theme, or color identity. Also surfaces combos (via Commander Spellbook) and similar commanders.

# When to use it

* Seed a first pass around a commander or theme.
* Discover staples and “new cards” packages without inventing the wheel.
* Cross-check your list against community baselines—then cut what does not serve *your* plan.
