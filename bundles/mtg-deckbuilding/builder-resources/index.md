# Builder Resources

External tools and community sites for deckbuilding:

* [Scryfall](scryfall.md) - Card search, legality, and gatherer-quality lookup.
* [EDHREC](edhrec.md) - Commander popularity and synergy data.
* [Archidekt](archidekt.md) - Commander (and other) list builder.
* [Moxfield](moxfield.md) - Multi-format deck builder and sharing.
* [Commander Spellbook](commander-spellbook.md) - Combo database for Commander.
* [MTGGoldfish](mtggoldfish.md) - Metagame, prices, and decklists.
* [MTGTop8](mtgtop8.md) - Tournament results aggregation.
* [17Lands](seventeen-lands.md) - Limited Premier Draft data.
* [Limited Resources](limited-resources.md) - Limited strategy podcast and site.
* [Official Rules and Formats](wizards-rules-hub.md) - Wizards rules and format construction hub.
