# Overview

[MTGGoldfish](https://www.mtggoldfish.com) aggregates decklists, metagame percentages, and price data for Standard, Modern, Pioneer, Legacy, Pauper, and more.

# When to use it

* See what is popular before you brew or sideboard.
* Compare stock lists and sideboard guides for a known archetype.
* Budget-check a brew (prices change; treat as a snapshot).
