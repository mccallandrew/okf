# Definition

A **local meta** is the recurring set of decks, power bands, and play patterns at your tables. Commander rarely has a single global metagame the way Modern does; tuning against regular opponents is the practical substitute.[^341-build]

# Guidelines

* After real games, note which strategies punish you (e.g. explosive token boards) and which [packages](/commander/packages.md) failed to answer them.[^341-build]
* Prefer **package-level** or like-for-like swaps that shore up those matches while staying true to [deck vision](/commander/deck-vision.md)—do not plan for every possible opponent.[^341-build]
* Example: swap a storm finisher that only pings for a sweeper/token maker that also leaves blockers against a ramp-token neighbor.[^341-build]
* Revisit [brackets](/commander/power-level-and-brackets.md) with the table; dominating a claimed band is a meta signal, not just variance.[^edhrec-power]
* Multiplayer threat reading still applies when adapting packages—answer the resources that will win, not only the flashiest board.[^edhrec-threat]

# Living list

Deckbuilding is rarely finished: play patterns, hidden gems, and local pressure keep packages in motion.[^341-build] See [Testing and Iteration](/process/testing-and-iteration.md).
