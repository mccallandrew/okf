# Definition

**Synergy** cards are above-rate only in your strategy (tribal payoffs, combo pieces). **Goodstuff** cards are powerful in most decks (efficient ramp, draw, removal).

Healthy Commander lists usually blend both: enough synergy to make the commander matter, enough goodstuff to function when synergies miss.[^flores-threat]

EDHREC’s synergy score measures how *unique* an include is for a commander—not how powerful it is. Colloquial “synergy” means thesis coherence; **exploitation** means artificially extracting outsized value and often explains why a themed deck still belongs in a higher [bracket](/commander/power-level-and-brackets.md).[^edhrec-synergy]

# Guidelines

* Focus on one **core strategy**; subthemes should feed that strategy rather than dilute it.[^draftsim-tips]
* If the deck only works with three specific cards, add redundancy or tutors—or cut the fragile theme.
* Prefer multi-role cards that advance the plan *and* fill a budget category (ramp, draw, interaction).[^draftsim-tips]
* [EDHREC](/resources/edhrec.md) high-inclusion cards are often goodstuff; theme pages highlight synergy—use both.
* Cut “win-more” synergies before cutting interaction and draw when the deck underperforms.
* Do not equate “on-theme” with low power—combos and stax packages can be highly synergistic *and* exploitative.[^edhrec-synergy]
* Combo density is a power-level choice—see [Commander Spellbook](/resources/commander-spellbook.md) and [brackets](/commander/power-level-and-brackets.md).
