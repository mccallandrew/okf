# Definition

**Ramp** decks invest early turns in extra mana (lands, rocks, dorks) to cast high-impact spells before a fair curve would allow.[^wiz-mana]

Common in Constructed big-mana strategies and most Commander lists.

# List shape

* Dense early ramp; high average mana value payoffs.
* Interaction to survive the window before the big spell resolves.
* Land counts that support both ramp and flood management.[^karsten-edh]

# Failure modes

* All ramp, no payoff (or all payoff, no ramp).
* Dying to aggro during the setup turns—need defense or a faster plan.[^flores-beatdown]
* Over-ramping into tapped mana and still missing colors.
