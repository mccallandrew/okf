# Definition

**Combo** decks win by assembling a specific set of cards or game state (infinite loop, lethal storm, hard lock) rather than fair attrition. Speed and consistency compete with protection.[^flores-threat]

# List shape

* Redundant combo pieces, tutors, and cantrips for consistency.
* Protection (silence effects, counters, discard) and/or a backup fair plan.
* Mana acceleration when the combo is expensive.

# Failure modes

* Drawing the wrong half; insufficient redundancy.
* Opponents’ hate cards (graveyard, storm, specific permanents) without answers.
* Goldfish turn is fast but real-game turn collapses to interaction—know when you must race as beatdown.[^flores-beatdown]
