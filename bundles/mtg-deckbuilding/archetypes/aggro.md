# Definition

**Aggro** decks win by dealing damage quickly with a dense early curve. They usually take the beatdown role and accept worse long games.[^flores-beatdown][^wiz-mana]

# List shape

* Heavy one- and two-drops; few cards above four mana.[^wiz-curve]
* High threat count; interaction is cheap and often doubles as damage (burn, combat tricks).
* Lower land counts than control, still enough to curve out.[^wiz-mana]

# Failure modes

* Flooding on lands or expensive spells.
* Opponents stabilize with lifegain or board wipes while you lack reach.
* Misassigned control role in a mirror—you needed to race harder.
