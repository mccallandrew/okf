# Role

Wheel decks are interesting in that there are multiple angles to key on, but they each surround players discarding and drawing new handfuls of cards. That means, card draw payoffs, discard payoffs, draw hate and graveyard strategies all have a chance in the spotlight. Windfall, Reforge the Soul, and Wheel of Fate are typical wheel effects.

This particular wheel list by Xero uses Xyris, the Writhing Storm as a consistent payoff and enabler as long as you trigger its saboteur effect.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

Illustrative list from the Draftsim EDH archetypes survey—not a claim of optimality.
