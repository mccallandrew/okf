# Definition

Wheel decks are interesting in that there are multiple angles to key on, but they each surround players discarding and drawing new handfuls of cards. That means, card draw payoffs, discard payoffs, draw hate and graveyard strategies all have a chance in the spotlight. Windfall, Reforge the Soul, and Wheel of Fate are typical wheel effects.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

# How it wins

Force mass discard-and-draw effects and profit from the refill.

# Typical packages

This particular wheel list by Xero uses Xyris, the Writhing Storm as a consistent payoff and enabler as long as you trigger its saboteur effect.

# Example decks

* [Xyris Wheel (Xero)](xyris-writhing-storm.md) — Wheel strategy with Xyris as consistent saboteur payoff and enabler.

# Failure modes

Unfocused theme dilution; missing interaction or mana; polarizing plans without table buy-in.
