# Definition

In Commander, **archetype** labels describe how a deck tends to win or what engine it biases toward—aggro, aristocrats, group slug, wheels, and many others. Labels are nebulous and non-exclusive: the same 99 often combines several descriptors.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

This hub mirrors a Draftsim survey of widely played themes. It is not exhaustive, not ranked, and not a claim about what is optimal. Example decks exist to show card *kinds*, including precon and community lists with imperfect mana bases.

# How to use this hub

1. Pick a primary plan under [Archetypes](/archetypes/commander/) that matches your commander’s desires.
2. Read the archetype `overview` for win patterns, packages, and failure modes.
3. Open the linked **Example Deck** concepts for a full illustrative list.
4. Still hit [category budgets](/commander/category-budgets.md) and [brackets](/commander/power-level-and-brackets.md)—theme does not replace a functional skeleton.
