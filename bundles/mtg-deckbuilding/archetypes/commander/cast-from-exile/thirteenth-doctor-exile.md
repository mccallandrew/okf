# Role

Some use the term paradox for the mechanic of the same name introduced in _Doctor Who_. While the mechanic itself is pretty general, there now exist quite a few cards that reward players for casting spells from zones other than their hands, usually from exile.

These make for very popular cards, with some notable commanders being Prosper, Tome-Bound, Faldorn, Dread Wolf Herald, and Vega, the Watcher. The _Paradox Power_ precon is a perfect showcase of just how many different mechanics in Magic work with this type of strategy.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

Illustrative list from the Draftsim EDH archetypes survey—not a claim of optimality.
