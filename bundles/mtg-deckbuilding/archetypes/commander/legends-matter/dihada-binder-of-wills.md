# Role

Legendary-matters decks are easier to build than they’ve ever been. Legendary spells and payoffs are a key part of so many new sets that you can build legends-matter decks across a variety of different color pairs without much overlap.

The strategy often boils down to “legendary good-stuff,” though you might incorporate a historic theme into these decks since legendary cards are indeed historic. The Dihada, Binder of Wills precon has a fair amount of fluff and filler, but it’s easily customizable and, with this great Mardu commander at the helm, highlights exactly how a legends-matter deck tends to look.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

Illustrative list from the Draftsim EDH archetypes survey—not a claim of optimality.
