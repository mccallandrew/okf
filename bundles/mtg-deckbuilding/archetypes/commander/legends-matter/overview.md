# Definition

Legendary-matters decks are easier to build than they’ve ever been. Legendary spells and payoffs are a key part of so many new sets that you can build legends-matter decks across a variety of different color pairs without much overlap.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

# How it wins

Legendary- and historic-matters synergies across the 99.

# Typical packages

The strategy often boils down to “legendary good-stuff,” though you might incorporate a historic theme into these decks since legendary cards are indeed historic. The Dihada, Binder of Wills precon has a fair amount of fluff and filler, but it’s easily customizable and, with this great Mardu commander at the helm, highlights exactly how a legends-matter deck tends to look.

# Example decks

* [Dihada Legends Matter](dihada-binder-of-wills.md) — Legendary- and historic-matters synergies under Dihada.

# Failure modes

Unfocused theme dilution; missing interaction or mana; polarizing plans without table buy-in.
