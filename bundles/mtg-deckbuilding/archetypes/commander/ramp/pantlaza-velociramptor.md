# Role

Starting off with some of the broader macro-archetypes, ramp decks look to accelerate mana in the first few turns and drop haymakers and finishers way ahead of schedule.

While most green decks run some number of Rampant Growth effects, ramp decks usually play a large quantity of mana dorks like Birds of Paradise to kick off as soon as possible. They often forego mid-game 4-drops and 5-drops to load up on early ramp spells and late-game closers.[^draftsim-edh-arch]
