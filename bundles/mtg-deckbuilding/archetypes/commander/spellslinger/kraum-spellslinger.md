# Role

Spellslinger decks usually feature a low creature count in favor of instants and sorceries, sometimes just non-creature spells in general. This is an A+B archetype that combines spells with spell payoffs.

Your Ponders and Brainstorms are already solid in most decks, but they’re even better when they’re triggering cards like Guttersnipe or Archmage Emeritus.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

Illustrative list from the Draftsim EDH archetypes survey—not a claim of optimality.
