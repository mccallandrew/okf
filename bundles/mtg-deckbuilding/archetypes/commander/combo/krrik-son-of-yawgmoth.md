# Role

K'rrik, Son of Yawgmoth aims to pull off one specific combo and that is to pull Chainer, Dementia Master, Gray Merchant of Asphodel, and Dimir House Guard from the graveyard and sacrifice the Gray Merchant repeatedly. Since we're in the correct EDH bracket for two card combos, Sanguine Bond and Exquisite Blood are also viable enchantments to search for and win with.

I borrowed nearly this entire decklist from our budget cEDH listing and it stands up to represent the combo decks possible. These decks often have several ways to tutor for and dig for the exact cards to win with, and the fewer the cards, the easier it is to pull off.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

Illustrative list from the Draftsim EDH archetypes survey—not a claim of optimality.
