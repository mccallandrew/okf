# Definition

K'rrik, Son of Yawgmoth aims to pull off one specific combo and that is to pull Chainer, Dementia Master, Gray Merchant of Asphodel, and Dimir House Guard from the graveyard and sacrifice the Gray Merchant repeatedly. Since we're in the correct EDH bracket for two card combos, Sanguine Bond and Exquisite Blood are also viable enchantments to search for and win with.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

# How it wins

I borrowed nearly this entire decklist from our budget cEDH listing and it stands up to represent the combo decks possible. These decks often have several ways to tutor for and dig for the exact cards to win with, and the fewer the cards, the easier it is to pull off.

Not all of those combos are legal in commander, but they're a great starting point if you want to use a combo to win. Combos are especially good to end the game against stax, lifegain, or any decks that seek to make the game last a long time.

You can't leave this section without hearing about the cEDH card in Thassa's Oracle. Lots of combo decks find ways to empty their library, then drop Thoracle and win on the spot.
