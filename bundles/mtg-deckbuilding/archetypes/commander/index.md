# Commander Archetypes

* [Overview](overview.md) - Popular EDH archetype descriptors with illustrative example lists.


# Macro roles

* [Ramp](ramp/) - Accelerate mana early to cast oversized threats ahead of schedule.
* [Aggro](aggro/) - Race opponents to zero life with a dense early curve and combat pressure.
* [Control](control/) - Answer threats at instant speed, generate advantage, and win with inevitability.
* [Combo](combo/) - Assemble a discrete combo win condition, often with tutors and protection.


# Board & combat themes

* [Typal](typal/) - Bias the creature base toward one type and stack typal payoffs.
* [Tokens/Go-Wide](tokens-go-wide/) - Flood the board with creatures and finish with mass pump or convoke payoffs.
* [+1/+1 Counters](plus-one-counters/) - Build around +1/+1 counters with proliferate, move, and counter payoffs.
* [Voltron](voltron/) - Stack auras, equipment, and counters on one unstoppable attacker.
* [Infect](infect/) - Win with poison counters via infect/toxic creatures and proliferate.


# Engines

* [Cascade / Discover](cascade-discover/) - Cheat on mana by cascading or discovering into free spells.
* [Wheel](wheel/) - Force mass discard-and-draw effects and profit from the refill.
* [Lifegain](lifegain/) - Gain life as a resource and convert it into card advantage or damage.
* [Landfall](landfall/) - Lands-matter strategies that use lands beyond tapping for mana.
* [Aristocrats/Sacrifice](aristocrats/) - Sacrifice creatures for value and drain or token loops.
* [Blink/Flicker](blink/) - Combine strong ETBs with flicker effects for repeated value.
* [Spellslinger](spellslinger/) - Cast many instants and sorceries with magecraft-style payoffs.
* [Enchantress](enchantress/) - Draw and win through a dense enchantment package.
* [Affinity/Artifacts](artifacts/) - Artifact-matters engines, affinity-style cost reduction, and artifact payoffs.
* [Cast From Exile](cast-from-exile/) - Impulse, plot, and exile-cast engines that play from exile.
* [Legends Matter/Historic](legends-matter/) - Legendary- and historic-matters synergies across the 99.
* [Recursion/Graveyard](graveyard/) - Treat the graveyard as a second hand via mill, recursion, and reanimation.


# Politics & denial

* [Group Hug](group-hug/) - Share resources with the table while positioning for a late pivot.
* [Group Slug](group-slug/) - Punish the whole table with symmetrical damage and taxing pain.
* [Chaos](chaos/) - Maximize randomness and splashy effects for wild game states.
* [Stax](stax/) - Lock opponents under resource denial while advancing a lean win plan.
* [Theft Decks](theft/) - Steal opponents' cards and use their best pieces as your win conditions.


# Alternate win

* [Mill](mill/) - Put opponents' libraries to zero while hedging against their graveyards.
