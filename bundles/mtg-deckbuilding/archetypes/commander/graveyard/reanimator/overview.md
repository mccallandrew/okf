# Definition

Reanimator packages fill the graveyard on purpose, then return high-impact creatures or permanents at a discount.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

A sub-strategy under [Recursion/Graveyard](/archetypes/commander/graveyard/overview.md); lists often mix these modes.

# How it wins

Resolve a reanimation spell or effect targeting a piled graveyard payoff.

# Typical packages

Entomb/Buried Alive effects, reanimation sorceries and auras, and durable threats worth returning.

# Failure modes

Graveyard hate blanks the plan; over-milling without outlets; splitting focus across too many graveyard modes without enough payoffs.
