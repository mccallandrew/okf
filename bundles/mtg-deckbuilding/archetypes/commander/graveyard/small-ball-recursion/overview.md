# Definition

Small-ball recursion repeatedly returns incremental value creatures and spells instead of relying on a single haymaker reanimation.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

A sub-strategy under [Recursion/Graveyard](/archetypes/commander/graveyard/overview.md); lists often mix these modes.

# How it wins

Outgrind the table through persistent recursive value.

# Typical packages

Eternal Witness–style effects, persist/undying-style loops, and cheap recursion engines.

# Failure modes

Graveyard hate blanks the plan; over-milling without outlets; splitting focus across too many graveyard modes without enough payoffs.
