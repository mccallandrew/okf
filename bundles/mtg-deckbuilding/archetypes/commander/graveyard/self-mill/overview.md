# Definition

Self-mill decks treat milling themselves as card selection and fuel for recursion, delirium-style thresholds, or reanimator piles.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

A sub-strategy under [Recursion/Graveyard](/archetypes/commander/graveyard/overview.md); lists often mix these modes.

# How it wins

Convert a stocked graveyard into advantage or a reanimation turn.

# Typical packages

Mill creatures and instants, recursion outlets, and payoffs that care about graveyard size or contents.

# Failure modes

Graveyard hate blanks the plan; over-milling without outlets; splitting focus across too many graveyard modes without enough payoffs.
