# Role

Yidris, Maelstrom Wielder is a rare 4-color commander that excludes white, and relies on trample plus support from other cards that make cascade easier to achieve like Flamekin Herald, Bigger on the Inside and Maelstrom Wanderer (a close runner up as the cascade commander).

Black is important to setup cards like The Infamous Cruelclaw, Felix Five-Boots and Prosper, Tome-Bound that synergize nicely. Discover is close enough to cascade that Dinosaur Egg and Hit the Mother Lode help this deck do it's thing.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

Illustrative list from the Draftsim EDH archetypes survey—not a claim of optimality.
