# Role

Control is a reactive macro-archetype that wants to operate at instant speed and respond to what your opponents are doing. Control decks are usually much lighter on wincons and board presence, with more emphasis on interaction through counterspells, board wipes, and flash threats.

Control decks often have a combo element to them, biding time with interaction until the combo pieces can be assembled. This Nymris, Oona's Trickster decklist has a theme of casting spells during opponents’ turns, and it features almost exclusively instant-speed plays.[^draftsim-edh-arch]
