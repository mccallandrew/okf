# Definition

Control is a reactive macro-archetype that wants to operate at instant speed and respond to what your opponents are doing. Control decks are usually much lighter on wincons and board presence, with more emphasis on interaction through counterspells, board wipes, and flash threats.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

# How it wins

Answer threats at instant speed, generate advantage, and win with inevitability.

# Typical packages

Control decks often have a combo element to them, biding time with interaction until the combo pieces can be assembled. This Nymris, Oona's Trickster decklist has a theme of casting spells during opponents’ turns, and it features almost exclusively instant-speed plays.

# Example decks

* [Nymris, Oona's Trickster Control](nymris-oonas-trickster.md) — Flash-heavy Dimir control illustrating instant-speed answers and late finishers.

# Failure modes

Unfocused theme dilution; missing interaction or mana; polarizing plans without table buy-in.
