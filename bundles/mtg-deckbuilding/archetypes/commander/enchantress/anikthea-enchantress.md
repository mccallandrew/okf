# Role

Enchantress is all about enchantments. What a twist!

These decks have a much higher-than-usual enchantment count, using cards like Argothian Enchantress and Enchantress's Presence as payoffs.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

Illustrative list from the Draftsim EDH archetypes survey—not a claim of optimality.
