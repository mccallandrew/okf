# Definition

A Voltron strategy focuses all its attention on building up one unstoppable threat and having that be your main wincon. Voltron strategies often rely on hexproof or indestructible creatures to ward off interaction, and they use equipment, auras, and/or +1/+1 counters to enhance that threat. These decks can be soft to certain kinds of removal and often draw the attention of opponents since they often play the role of the aggressor.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

# How it wins

Stack auras, equipment, and counters on one unstoppable attacker.
