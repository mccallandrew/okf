# Role

Everyone’s fave. Infect refers to any deck that’s trying to win with poison as an alternate wincon.

While many players associate infect with Blightsteel Colossus, Tainted Strike, or Triumph of the Hordes, those are cards that win independently of an actual infect strategy, and they’ve kind of sullied the strategy as a whole for many players.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

Illustrative list from the Draftsim EDH archetypes survey—not a claim of optimality.
