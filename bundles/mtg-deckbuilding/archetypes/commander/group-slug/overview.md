# Definition

Group Slug players just want everyone to have a bad time. The antithesis of group hug, group slug players seek pain. This strategy attacks players’ resources, which often includes the group slug player themselves. Sulfuric Vortex and Painful Quandary are examples of group slug cards that sap players of their resources, whether that’s life, board presence, or cards in hand.[^draftsim-edh-arch]

# How it wins

Punish the whole table with symmetrical damage and taxing pain.

# Typical packages

I’ve pulled a Group Slug decklist from fellow Draftsim writer A.L. Walser that exemplifies this playstyle perfectly. The group slug commander’s mean, the cards are mean, and the entire premise of the deck is making sure players are dying, even if that means taking damage from your own cards.
