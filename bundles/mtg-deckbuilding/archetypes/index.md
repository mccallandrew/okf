# Archetypes

Macro deck roles and how they shape construction:

* [Aggro](aggro.md) - Low curve, high threat density, race to zero.
* [Midrange](midrange.md) - Efficient value threats and flexible interaction.
* [Control](control.md) - Answers first, win later with inevitability.
* [Combo](combo.md) - Assembling a discrete win condition under disruption.
* [Tempo](tempo.md) - Cheap threats plus interaction that preserves the clock.
* [Ramp](ramp.md) - Accelerate into oversized threats or finishers.
