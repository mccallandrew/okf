# Definition

**Control** decks prioritize answers and card advantage, then win with a small number of finishers once the board is stable. They usually claim [inevitability](/foundations/inevitability.md).[^flores-beatdown][^flores-threat]

# List shape

* High density of interaction; low threat count.
* Higher land counts and often expensive sweepers or card-draw spells.[^wiz-mana]
* Sideboards full of hate for combo and problematic permanents.

# Failure modes

* Insufficient early answers against hyper-aggro.
* Drawing too many win conditions and not enough interaction (or the reverse).
* Playing like the beatdown when you should be stabilizing.
