# Definition

**Midrange** sits between aggro and control: sturdy threats that trade up, plus enough interaction to win ugly games. Role assignment is matchup-dependent—sometimes beatdown, sometimes control.[^flores-beatdown]

# List shape

* Curve centered on two- to four-drops with a few expensive haymakers.[^wiz-curve]
* Mix of threats and answers; card advantage engines matter.[^flores-threat]
* Flexible mana bases for splash colors and utility lands.

# Failure modes

* Being too slow against aggro and too fair against combo/control.
* Clunky curve (too many fours) without enough early defense.
* Sideboarding into a pile with no clear plan.
