# Definition

**Sideboarding** swaps cards between games to improve a matchup: add answers to problem threats, increase the clock, or change which role you can play.[^flores-beatdown][^flores-threat]

# Guidelines

* Enter game two with an explicit plan: “I am beatdown / control” and which cards enable that.[^flores-beatdown]
* **Hate cards** answer a strategy (graveyard, combo, artifacts); **plan cards** reinforce your own axis.
* Do not sideboard into a pile with no win condition—every cut needs a kept threat or finisher.
* Numbers matter: boarding in two soft answers may be worse than one hard answer plus a threat.
* Practice configurations; write a sideboard guide for expected decks in Constructed.

# Format notes

* **Limited / Sealed**: Sideboards are the unused pool—board in bombs and answers, board out clunky off-color cards.
* **Commander**: Usually no sideboard; “boarding” is deck choice and politics—see power-level alignment instead.
