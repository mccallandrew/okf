# Definition

A **Limited curve** targets enough early creatures to contest the board and enough midrange threats to close, usually with **16–18 lands** in a 40-card deck.[^wiz-mana][^wiz-curve]

# Guidelines

* Aim for a healthy count of two- and three-drops; avoid hands full of five-drops.[^wiz-curve]
* Rough creature counts often land in the mid-to-high teens depending on format speed and removal density.
* Combat tricks and removal sit off the creature curve but still need mana—do not cut all early plays for “bombs.”
* Aggressive pools skew lower; controlling pools need early defense (blockers, removal) even if the wincon is late.
