# Definition

**Sealed** gives a fixed pool (typically six packs). Building means choosing the strongest two-color (sometimes three) core with a real curve and enough interaction—not maximizing rare count alone.[^flores-threat]

# Guidelines

* Lay out creatures by mana value; prefer the pair with the smoothest curve and removal.[^wiz-curve]
* Splash bombs only with fixing and a clear reason.[^wiz-mana]
* Sideboard is the rest of the pool—board in hate and bombs for known opponents.
* Accept that Sealed pools are inconsistent; prioritize playables over speculative synergies.
