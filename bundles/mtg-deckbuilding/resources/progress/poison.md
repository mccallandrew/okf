# Definition

**Poison** is an alternate loss condition: usually ten poison counters. Infect, toxic, and poisonous are **production patterns** that advance this clock—not separate win conditions. Dual-homed as counters under [Poison Counters](/resources/counter/poison-counters.md).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Build production (combat infect/toxic, direct poison) plus finishers (proliferate)—see [Proliferate And Multiplication](/resources/counter/proliferate-and-multiplication.md).
* Mixing poison with a life-damage plan without shared pieces often strands half the deck.
