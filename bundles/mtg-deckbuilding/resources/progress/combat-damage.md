# Definition

**Combat damage** is damage assigned in combat. As progress it is the red-zone clock: attackers connecting for lethal, often aided by evasion, extra combats, or go-wide.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Bodies and keywords matter—see [Combat Bodies](/resources/permanent/creature/combat-bodies.md), [Combat Effects](/effects/combat/).
* Trample and unblockable convert board into reliable progress; fogs reset a combat step’s progress.
* Infect/toxic on combat dealers convert hits into [poison](/resources/progress/poison.md) instead of (or in addition to) life damage.
* Commander combat damage also ticks the [commander damage](/resources/progress/commander-damage.md) clock in EDH.
