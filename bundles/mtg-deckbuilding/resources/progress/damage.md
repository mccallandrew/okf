# Definition

**Damage** to players (and sometimes planeswalkers) is progress toward reducing [life totals](/resources/life/life-total.md) to zero. It is not spent like mana; it is a race. Split for deckbuilding into [combat damage](/resources/progress/combat-damage.md) and [noncombat damage](/resources/progress/noncombat-damage.md).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Count *effective* damage after lifegain, fogs, and prevention—see [Fog And Prevention](/resources/life/fog-and-prevention.md).
* Multiplayer: spreading damage can leave everyone alive; focus fire or have a table-wide finisher ([Burn Player-Each](/effects/combat/damage/burn-player-each.md) and similar).
* Infect combat damage is poison progress, not life damage—see [Poison](/resources/progress/poison.md).
* Effect hub: [Combat → Damage](/effects/combat/damage/), [Burn](/effects/combat/damage/burn.md), [Burn Player](/effects/combat/damage/burn-player.md). Drain that also gains life: [Drain](/resources/life/drain.md).
