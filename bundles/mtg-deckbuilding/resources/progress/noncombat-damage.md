# Definition

**Noncombat damage** is damage (or life loss paired with burn-like finishes) from spells and abilities outside the combat damage step—classic Burn, pings, and many drains.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Burn finishes through fogs that only stop combat; life gain still races you—see [Life Total](/resources/life/life-total.md).
* Chip damage sets up lethal; redundant reach spells close games.
* Effect tags: [Burn](/effects/combat/damage/burn.md), [Burn Player](/effects/combat/damage/burn-player.md); life-linked: [Drain Life](/effects/resource/life/drain-life.md), [Drain](/resources/life/drain.md).
