# Definition

**Mill to deck** treats [library depth](/resources/card/library/library-depth.md) as a clock: when a player would draw from an empty library, they lose. Opponent mill is a progress plan; self-mill is usually setup for [graveyard](/resources/card/graveyard/) or [alternate wins](/resources/progress/alternate-win-conditions.md) (e.g. Laboratory Maniac lines).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Dedicated mill needs volume and a finish; incidental mill rarely wins alone outside combo.
* Commander’s large libraries slow pure mill—prefer combo finishes or alt-wins.
* Card-zone framing of milled cards: [Milled Cards](/resources/card/library/milled-cards.md). Effect tags: [Mill](/effects/zone/library/mill.md), [Mill-Opponent](/effects/zone/library/mill-opponent.md), [Mill-Self](/effects/zone/library/mill-self.md).
