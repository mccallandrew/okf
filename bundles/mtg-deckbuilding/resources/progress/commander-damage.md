# Definition

**Commander damage** is a **Commander/EDH-format-specific** progress clock: 21 combat damage from a single commander to a player causes that player to lose, tracked per commander pairing. It races beside life totals and is **not** reduced by lifegain.

Also framed under [Life → Commander Damage](/resources/life/commander-damage.md) (buffer/race beside life). The command-zone privilege that makes the commander available is [Commander Zone](/resources/privilege/commander-zone.md).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Voltron and aggressive commanders treat 21 as the real close; pillows, fogs, and chump blocks buy time.
* Multiplayer: watch for stealth 21 from one player while the table races life totals.
* Does not apply in formats without the commander damage rule—do not import this clock into 60-card constructed plans.
* Construction hubs: [Commander](/commander/), [Commander Overview](/formats/commander-overview.md).
