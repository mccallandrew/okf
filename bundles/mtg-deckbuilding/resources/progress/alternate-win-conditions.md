# Definition

**Alternate win conditions** are card-driven wins (or forced losses) outside the default life/poison/(EDH) commander-damage clocks—Approach of the Second Sun–style, empty-library win replacements, and similar. The resource is meeting the card’s stated condition.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Build the enabler package (casts, mill, specific permanents) as a real plan, not a singleton pet win.
* Not a catalog of every AWC—use effect inventory and Scryfall.
* Effect hubs: [Alternate Win Condition](/effects/game/win-condition/alternate-win-condition.md), [Alternate Loss Condition](/effects/game/win-condition/alternate-loss-condition.md), [Win Condition](/effects/game/win-condition/).
* Self-mill AWC lines pair with [Mill To Deck](/resources/progress/mill-to-deck.md).
