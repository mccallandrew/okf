# Definition

**Kingmaking** is when a player who cannot win (or will not) decides which other player does—via attack direction, a wipe, a counter, or a broken deal. It is a failure mode of the social resource: deals and cooperation outlive a fair race.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Minimize forced kingmake spots: avoid “I can only interact with one player’s win” as your only line; keep multi-target answers and own clocks.
* After [archenemy](/resources/social/archenemy.md) falls, survivors often kingmake—plan finishes that do not rely on a third player’s charity.
* Playgroup norms differ; some tables treat kingmaking as soft-fail etiquette, others as fair politics—tune via [Local Meta](/commander/local-meta.md).
* Decks that farm [table favor](/resources/social/table-favor.md) without a wincon are especially likely to hand someone else the game.
