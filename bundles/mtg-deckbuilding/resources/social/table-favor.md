# Definition

**Table favor** is value you offer others—shared draw, [giving life](/resources/life/giving-life.md), goad targets, joint ramp—to buy attacks elsewhere, answers on a rival, or a pass. It is how soft [political capital](/resources/social/political-capital.md) is topped up with hard cards.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Prefer *controllable* gifts over fully symmetrical “everyone draws forever”; the latter feeds the strongest deck—see [Group Hug](/archetypes/commander/group-hug/overview.md).
* Favor without a pivot or wincon is charity; package politics with a close.
* [Giving life](/resources/life/giving-life.md), Humble Defector–style deals, and goad ([Hate-Goad](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-goad.md)) are common favor engines.
* Opposite posture: [Group Slug](/archetypes/commander/group-slug/overview.md) and [Tax](/effects/interaction/tax/) spend favor to punish the table instead.
