# Definition

A **social** resource is soft multiplayer standing: goodwill, deals, how threatening you look, and whether the table gangs up on you. It is **not** CR-tracked like mana or the [monarch](/resources/privilege/monarch.md), but pods spend and race it constantly—especially in [Commander](/commander/).

Social capital is negotiated and reputation-backed. **Board state and specific cards can still spend it for you** (or force others to spend answers on you). A free-draw engine, stax piece, or open combo line is not “just politics”; it is hard game state that converts into [card-driven heat](/resources/social/card-driven-heat.md).

# Deckbuilding notes

* This branch is **Commander/multiplayer-first**. In duels and most 1v1 formats it is mostly null.
* Soft does not mean optional: decks that ignore heat lose political capital on every splashy permanent; decks that farm favor without a wincon lose anyway.
* Do not confuse social standing with a privilege you *hold*—the monarch is a rules designation; “don’t attack me” is a deal.
* Point archetypes at Group Hug / Group Slug / Stax / Chaos for packages; this taxonomy names the resource those plans spend and earn.
