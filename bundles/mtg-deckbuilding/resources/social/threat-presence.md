# Definition

**Threat presence** is how much attention, combat, and answers the table spends on you. It is a soft score: high presence means you are the problem; low presence means others race elsewhere. Visible [card](/resources/card/) and [mana](/resources/mana/) engines drive it more than life totals.[^edhrec-threat]

# Deckbuilding notes

* Construction that reliably outpaces the table *is* threat presence—others should (and will) answer it first; see [Threats and Answers](/foundations/threats-and-answers.md).
* Soften presence with split wins, politics, or [table favor](/resources/social/table-favor.md); harden it when you are ready to close.
* [Card-driven heat](/resources/social/card-driven-heat.md) spikes presence on a single permanent; sandbagging and sequencing manage the curve of attention.
* Distinct from being the beatdown in a duel—multiplayer presence is relative to three boards, not one.
