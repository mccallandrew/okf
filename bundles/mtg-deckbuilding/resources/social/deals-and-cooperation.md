# Definition

**Deals and cooperation** are temporary table agreements—“I won’t touch you if…”, shared focus on a runaway player, attack redirection. They are enforceable only by [political capital](/resources/social/political-capital.md) and future reputation, not by the rules.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Build levers that make deals *credible*: goad, pillowfort, targeted help, or a visible reason the other player benefits—see [Table Favor](/resources/social/table-favor.md) and [Pillowfort](/effects/interaction/pillowfort/).
* Breaking deals can win a game and lose a playgroup; track local norms in [Local Meta](/commander/local-meta.md).
* Contested privileges ([monarch](/resources/privilege/monarch.md), [initiative](/resources/privilege/initiative.md)) often become deal subjects (“you keep the crown; I won’t swing”).
* Cooperation against [archenemy](/resources/social/archenemy.md) pressure is still a deal—exit conditions matter before the last opponent dies.
