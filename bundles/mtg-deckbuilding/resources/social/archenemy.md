# Definition

**Archenemy** here means the social state where the rest of the pod cooperates against you (1v3), not the casual Archenemy *product* format with scheme cards. It is [threat presence](/resources/social/threat-presence.md) maxed out: deals form without you, and every answer points your way.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Plans that win through the table (stax locks, open infinites, mass denial) should expect archenemy and pack [interaction](/effects/interaction/), protection, or a fast close.
* Surviving archenemy is a resource race: life, fog, pillowfort, and stack defense matter more when three players spend answers on you.
* Becoming archenemy can be correct if you are actually winning; becoming it early on empty boards is usually [card-driven heat](/resources/social/card-driven-heat.md) without payoff.
* When the archenemy falls, leftover cooperation often collapses into [kingmaking](/resources/social/kingmaking.md) among the survivors.
