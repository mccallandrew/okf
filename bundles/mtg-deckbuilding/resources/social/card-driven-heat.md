# Definition

**Card-driven heat** is when hard game pieces convert into social cost: free-draw engines, [tax](/effects/interaction/tax/)/stax, infinite setups, mass land denial, and “must answer or lose” permanents. Soft standing is still the ledger—but **cards put the target on you**.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Classic heat magnets: unpaid-draw enchantments, extra-turn chains, hard stax, tutor + combo lines left open on board.
* Sequence heat behind a close or behind politics; casting the magnet with no follow-up spends [political capital](/resources/social/political-capital.md) for free.
* Protection and recursion on heat pieces invite more hate; plan answers for the answers ([Removal](/effects/interaction/removal/), artifact/enchantment hate).
* Construction implication: every high-heat include raises expected [threat presence](/resources/social/threat-presence.md)—budget interaction and win speed accordingly.[^edhrec-threat]
