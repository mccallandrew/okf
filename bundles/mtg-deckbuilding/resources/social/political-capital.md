# Definition

**Political capital** is the soft currency of table standing—goodwill, trust, and the sense that you are not the problem yet. You spend it when you ask for a pass, redirect an attack, or break a deal; you earn it by looking fair, keeping agreements, and not racing ahead of the pod.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Invisible until spent: low capital means every ask fails and every engine draws hate.
* [Card-driven heat](/resources/social/card-driven-heat.md) burns capital even if you never speak—hard boards spend soft standing for you.
* Bracket and power honesty before the game ([Power Level and Brackets](/commander/power-level-and-brackets.md)) is pre-game capital; misrepresentation spends it before turn one.
* Distinct from [Privilege](/resources/privilege/) designations you *hold*—capital is reputation, not a CR status.
