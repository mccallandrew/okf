# Definition

Your **life total** is the primary numeric buffer against a damage-based loss. It is also the pool that [life payments](/resources/life/life-payment.md) and many manabase costs spend. In multiplayer it is a political resource—looking healthy or vulnerable changes who gets attacked.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Starting life differs by format (20, 25, 40, etc.); the same payment package plays differently at each total.
* Racing plans care about *effective* life after burns, commander damage, and poison—not the printed starting number alone.
* Lifegain raises the buffer; it does not win unless payoffs say so—see [Lifegain Payoffs](/resources/life/lifegain-payoffs.md).
* Opposite race resource: [Damage](/resources/progress/damage.md).
