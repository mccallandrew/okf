# Definition

**Lifelink** converts damage you deal into [lifegain](/resources/life/lifegain.md). It ties the life resource to combat (or other damage sources) and scales with power and extra combats.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Best on evasive or large bodies; useless when you cannot connect.
* Granting lifelink to the team is a payoff for go-wide; voltron stacks it on one attacker.
