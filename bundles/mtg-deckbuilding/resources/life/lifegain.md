# Definition

**Lifegain** increases your [life total](/resources/life/life-total.md). As a resource it matters when it enables payments, races burn, or feeds [lifegain payoffs](/resources/life/lifegain-payoffs.md)—raw padding alone is often the weakest card in the deck.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Incidental gain (gainlands, combat lifelink) supports payment and survival; dedicated gain needs payoffs or a real race problem.
* [Repeatable Lifegain](/resources/life/repeatable-lifegain.md) and [Food](/resources/permanent/token/food.md) are engine-shaped; one-shot gain is usually a combat trick or sideboard vs Burn.
