# Definition

**Fog and prevention** stop damage that would lower your [life total](/resources/life/life-total.md). They buy turns against aggro without increasing life—preservation rather than gain.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Best as reactive answers in midrange/control; dead when opponents win without combat damage.
* Selective fogs can push your attack while stopping theirs.
* Effect tags: [Fog](/effects/resource/life/fog.md), [Fog-Selective](/effects/resource/life/fog-selective.md); broader prevention may sit under combat/interaction tags.
