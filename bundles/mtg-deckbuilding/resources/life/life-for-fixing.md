# Definition

**Life for fixing** is life spent on the manabase—shocklands, painlands, boltlands, and similar—so you get [colored mana](/resources/mana/colored-mana.md) on time. Life is traded for mana reliability.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Early shocks are correct when the alternative is missing spells; late shocks are often unnecessary.
* Painlands tax every activation—count against long games and mirror burns.
* Construction context: [Fixing and Utility Lands](/mana/fixing-and-utility-lands.md), [Colored Sources](/mana/colored-sources.md).
* Effect tags: [Shockland](/effects/resource/life/shockland.md), [Painland](/effects/resource/life/painland.md), [Boltland](/effects/resource/life/boltland.md), [Gainland](/effects/resource/life/gainland.md) (life *gain* on ETB—opposite trade).
