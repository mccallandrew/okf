# Definition

**Giving life** raises an opponent’s life total—alternate costs, group hug, or incidental healing. You spend *their* buffer as a resource when the trade (cheaper spell, political deal) is worth it.

# Deckbuilding notes

* [Alternate-Cost-Gain-Life](/effects/resource/life/alternate-cost-gain-life.md) cheapens your spells by padding opponents—fine if you win the same turn or race differently (poison, combo).
* In Commander, giving life can redirect aggro; in duels it is usually a real cost.
* Opposite of [Drain](/resources/life/drain.md).
