# Definition

**Life** is both a win/loss threshold and a spendable currency. Decks race [life totals](/resources/life/life-total.md), weaponize [lifegain](/resources/life/lifegain.md), pay life for cards and mana ([life payment](/resources/life/life-payment.md), [Phyrexian mana](/resources/mana/phyrexian-mana.md)), drain opponents, or preserve life with fogs and lifelink.

This branch is **not**:

* **[Damage](/resources/progress/damage.md)** / **[Poison](/resources/progress/poison.md)** as win *progress*—those live under [Progress](/resources/progress/); life is the buffer those clocks spend down.
* **[Life Effects](/effects/resource/life/)** — oracle-tag inventory of effects that gain, pay, drain, or prevent (this tree links *to* those tags).
* Format starting-life rules essays—only the resource implications appear here.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Separate **buffer** (survive the race) from **fuel** (payments, Phyrexian, shocklands) from **engine** (lifegain payoffs, extort, soul sisters).
* High starting life (Commander) makes payment lines and lifegain engines more attractive; low life (Burn, poison) punishes them.
* [Hate-Lifegain](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-lifegain.md) and [Hate-Life-Payment](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-life-payment.md) punish greedy life strategies.
* Giving opponents life (alternate costs) is sometimes correct—treat their life as negotiable, not sacred.
