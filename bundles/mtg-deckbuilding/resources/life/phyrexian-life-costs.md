# Definition

**Phyrexian life costs** are the life half of [Phyrexian mana](/resources/mana/phyrexian-mana.md): each Phyrexian symbol may be paid with two life instead of one mana of that color. Life becomes splash and off-color casting power.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Strong when life is plentiful (Commander, post-stabilization); weak under Burn and poison races.
* Stacks with other [life payments](/resources/life/life-payment.md)—budget the total.
* Deep mana framing: [Phyrexian Mana](/resources/mana/phyrexian-mana.md). Effect tags: [Phyrexian Mana](/effects/resource/life/phyrexian-mana.md), [Phyrexian Mana Ability](/effects/resource/life/phyrexian-mana-ability.md).
