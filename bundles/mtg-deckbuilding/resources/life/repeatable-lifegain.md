# Definition

**Repeatable lifegain** is life produced every turn or on a common trigger—soul sisters, lifelink attackers, Food engines, extort. It is the production half of a lifegain resource engine.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Prefer triggers you already want to fire (creature ETBs, attacks) over tapping down for 1 life.
* [Food](/resources/permanent/token/food.md) stores gain on the board until spent—see [Repeatable Food](/effects/resource/life/repeatable-food.md).
