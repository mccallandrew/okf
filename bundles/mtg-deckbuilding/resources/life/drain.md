# Definition

**Drain** links opponent life loss to your gain (or parallel loss/gain)—Extort, Blood Artist, Drain Life. It attacks their [life total](/resources/life/life-total.md) / [damage progress](/resources/progress/damage.md) while padding yours.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Aristocrats and spell-extort shells turn every death or spell into a drain tick.
* In multiplayer, “each opponent” drains scale harder than single-target.
