# Definition

**Life payment** treats life as currency: paying life for spells and abilities, alternate costs, and many “as an additional cost” lines. Closely related: [Phyrexian mana](/resources/mana/phyrexian-mana.md) and [life for fixing](/resources/life/life-for-fixing.md) on lands.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Aggressive decks spend life for tempo; control spends it for cards ([Life For Cards](/effects/resource/cards/life-for-cards.md)) when the race allows.
* Track cumulative payments—death by a thousand cuts is real against Burn.
