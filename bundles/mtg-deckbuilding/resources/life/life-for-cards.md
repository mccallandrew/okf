# Definition

**Life for cards** converts life into [card](/resources/card/) access—Necropotence-style and greed abilities. Life becomes the payment for [card advantage](/resources/card/card-advantage.md).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Best when you are ahead on board or at high life; suicidal under aggro.
* Instant-speed life payments and end-step draws change how much you can safely spend.
* Effect tags: [Life For Cards](/effects/resource/cards/life-for-cards.md), [Greed Ability](/effects/resource/cards/greed-ability.md); also [Life Payment](/effects/resource/life/life-payment.md).
