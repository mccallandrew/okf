# Definition

**Lifegain payoffs** turn life gained into another resource—draw, tokens, drains, or pumps. They make lifegain an engine instead of a buffer.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Count how often you gain life per turn; payoffs need a reliable trigger rate (soul sisters, lifelink army, Food loops).
* Aegis-style and “whenever you gain life” cards define the shell; filler gain spells without triggers are traps.
* Overlap with [Drain](/resources/life/drain.md) when gain and opponent loss are linked.
* Search effect inventory under [Lifegain](/effects/resource/life/lifegain.md) and related triggers; pair with [Repeatable Lifegain](/effects/resource/life/repeatable-lifegain.md).
