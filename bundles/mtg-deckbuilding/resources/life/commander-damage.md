# Definition

**Commander damage** is a **Commander/EDH-format-specific** loss condition: 21 combat damage from a single commander. It races beside [life total](/resources/life/life-total.md) and is tracked per commander pairing. Lifegain does **not** erase commander damage already dealt.

Full progress-clock framing (how to race and finish) lives under [Progress → Commander Damage](/resources/progress/commander-damage.md). This leaf keeps the life-adjacent view: a second clock next to your life buffer.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Voltron and aggressive commanders treat 21 as the real clock; pillows and fogs buy time.
* Multiplayer: distribute awareness so no single commander sneaks 21 while you race life totals.
* Lifegain still matters against non-commander damage and burn; it does not unwind commander damage already marked.
* Does not apply outside Commander-rules formats.
