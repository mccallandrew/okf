# Definition

The **command zone** privilege is always-on access to cast your commander (subject to timing, color identity, and **commander tax**). It is the defining resource of Commander deckbuilding: a card that begins outside your library and returns to the zone when it would go to hand, library, graveyard, or exile (with exceptions).

This is the most important format privilege in this taxonomy. Construction methodology lives under [Commander](/commander/); this leaf names the *in-game resource*—reliable access to a specific legendary spell/permanent.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Tax accumulates: ramp, cost reduction, and protection keep the commander castable—see [Category Budgets](/commander/category-budgets.md), [Commander Selection](/commander/commander-selection.md).
* Build *around* the commander without a single point of failure—redundancy if the commander is answered repeatedly.
* Partner/background and companion-like extras are related zone privileges; keep the commander itself primary.
* [Commander damage](/resources/life/commander-damage.md) is a progress clock from the commander as a permanent, not the zone privilege itself.
* Format hub: [Commander Overview](/formats/commander-overview.md). Cast-from-elsewhere patterns: [Cast From Nonhand](/effects/spell/cast-from-nonhand/).
