# Definition

A **privilege** is a permission or designation a player **holds**—not a permanent they cast, and not a normal card in hand. Privileges reshape what you may do each turn or what ongoing status you enjoy.

The most important format privilege in this bundle is **[Commander Zone](/resources/privilege/commander-zone.md)** access: the always-available commander (and tax) that defines Commander deckbuilding.

Rough groups (flat files; grouping is prose only):

* **Turn permissions** — [land drop](/resources/privilege/land-drop.md), [extra turn](/resources/privilege/extra-turn.md)
* **Contested / cumulative designations** — [monarch](/resources/privilege/monarch.md), [initiative](/resources/privilege/initiative.md), [the Ring](/resources/privilege/the-ring.md), [city's blessing](/resources/privilege/citys-blessing.md), [day and night](/resources/privilege/day-and-night.md)

# Deckbuilding notes

* Privileges are often **contested** (monarch, initiative) or **global** (day/night)—build to take, keep, or punish them.
* Do not confuse a privilege with the permanent that grants it; the Ring-tempts effect is an effect, the Ring status is the privilege.
* Effect otags are sparse for many designations—link hubs that exist and use Scryfall text search when tagging is thin.
* Attractions and similar Un-products are out of scope for this branch.
