# Definition

The **monarch** is a contested player designation: the monarch draws a card at the beginning of their end step, and combat damage to the monarch can transfer the crown. It is recurring [card advantage](/resources/card/card-advantage.md) tied to a fightable status.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Decks that want the monarch need ways to *take* it (combat, effects) and *keep* it (pillowfort, politics, flying).
* Go-wide and evasion make stealing the crown easy; pillowfort and fog make keeping it easier.
* Few dedicated oracle tags—search Scryfall for “the monarch” / “monarch”; pair with combat and pillowfort packages under [Interaction](/effects/interaction/).
