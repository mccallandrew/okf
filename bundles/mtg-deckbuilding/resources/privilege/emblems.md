# Definition

An **emblem** is an ongoing effect owned by a player that is not a permanent and is typically almost impossible to remove. Emblems are sticky privileges—often ultimates or delayed win conditions that outlast board wipes.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Planeswalker ultimates and similar emblem creators are “must answer before the emblem” threats.
* Once the emblem exists, interaction shifts to racing or alternate win denial—not removing the emblem.
* Sparse otags—search Scryfall for emblem; planeswalker framing: [Planeswalkers](/resources/permanent/planeswalker/planeswalkers.md), [Loyalty](/resources/counter/loyalty.md).
