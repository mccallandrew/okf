# Definition

**The Ring** is a cumulative privilege: whenever you are tempted, you advance Ring bonuses and choose a Ring-bearer. The status tracks how many times you have been tempted; the bearer is a designation on a creature you control.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Tempt density (many “the Ring tempts you” triggers) is the engine; a protected bearer converts the combat bonuses.
* Unlike monarch/initiative, opponents do not normally “steal” your Ring progress—only race their own temptations if they have them.
