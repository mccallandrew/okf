# Definition

The **initiative** is a contested designation: the player with the initiative ventures into the dungeon (on upkeep, once they have it), and combat damage can transfer it. It packages [dungeon and venture](/resources/privilege/dungeon-and-venture.md) progress behind a fightable status—like the [monarch](/resources/privilege/monarch.md) for dungeon payoffs.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Initiative decks want to take it early, keep it, and finish Undercity (or the chosen dungeon) for the big reward.
* Effect tag: [Take The Initiative](/effects/zone/library/take-the-initiative.md).
* Cross-build with combat pressure and venture payoffs; without dungeon completion the initiative is just incremental ventures.
