# Definition

**City's blessing** is a player designation gained via ascend (typically when you control ten or more permanents). Once you have it, you keep it for the rest of the game—an unlocked privilege that turns on ascend payoffs.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Build to hit ten permanents reliably (tokens, cheap rocks, go-wide) then cash permanent ascend upsides.
* Unlike monarch, it is not contested—race the threshold, then play the upgraded text.
* Few otags—search Scryfall for ascend / “city’s blessing”; board density links: [Go Wide](/resources/permanent/go-wide.md), [Board Presence](/resources/permanent/board-presence.md).
