# Definition

**Rad counters** are counters on players that cause radiation-style mill and life loss over time. As a privilege-shaped resource they are a designation-like pressure you pile on opponents (or accept on yourself for payoffs). They are **dual-homed**: also documented as [Rad Counters](/resources/counter/rad-counters.md) under Counter.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Rad decks want ways to give opponents rad and to proliferate; self-rad needs mill/recursion payoffs.
* Proliferate scales rad like poison/energy—see [Proliferate And Multiplication](/resources/counter/proliferate-and-multiplication.md).
* Effect otags are thin—search Scryfall for rad counters; compare [Poison](/resources/progress/poison.md) as another player-counter clock.
