# Definition

A **land drop** is the once-per-turn (by default) permission to *play* a land from hand. Extra land drops are a privilege that accelerate [lands as mana](/resources/mana/lands-as-mana.md) without putting lands into play via ramp spells.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Distinct from [land ramp](/resources/mana/land-ramp.md) that *puts* lands onto the battlefield—both raise land count, different rules text and sequencing.
* Extra drops shine with landfall and full hands; dead when you are flooded with no plays.
* Effect tag: [Play Additional Land](/effects/resource/mana/play-additional-land.md). Construction: [Land Counts](/mana/land-counts.md).
