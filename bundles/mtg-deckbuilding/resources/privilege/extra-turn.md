# Definition

An **extra turn** is permission to take another turn after the current one. It multiplies [land drops](/resources/privilege/land-drop.md), draws, combat, and untaps—extreme tempo compressed into one card.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Best when you convert the turn immediately (kill, lock, or irreversible advantage); soft extra turns invite interaction.
* Chains and copies escalate quickly in multiplayer—expect politics and [Prevent Extra Turns](/effects/interaction/hate/prevent-extra-turns.md) hate.
