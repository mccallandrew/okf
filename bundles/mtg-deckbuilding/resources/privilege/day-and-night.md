# Definition

**Day and night** is a shared designation on the game: once day/night begins, it flips based on who cast spells on prior turns. Daybound/nightbound permanents and payoffs treat the current status as a resource you try to lock or flip.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Decks that care want to *control spell cadence* (cast or not cast) to keep the preferred face.
* Multiplayer makes night/day politics weird—track who cast what each turn.
* Sparse otags—search Scryfall for daybound, nightbound, day/night; permanents that transform are under [Transform](/effects/permanent/transform/).
