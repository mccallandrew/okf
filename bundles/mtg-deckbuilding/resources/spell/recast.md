# Definition

**Recast** is getting another cast from a card that already resolved or sits in a zone with a cast-again keyword (flashback, escape, jump-start, rebound, and similar). Extra cast events from the same card.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Flashback piles raise effective spell density without raising deck count.
* Distinct from returning to hand then casting again (recursion)—recast skips the hand.
