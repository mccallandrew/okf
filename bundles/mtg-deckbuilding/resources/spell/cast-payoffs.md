# Definition

**Cast payoffs** convert [cast events](/resources/spell/cast-events.md) into other resources—counters, damage, card draw, tokens. Magecraft and prowess are the common shapes.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Instant/sorcery density for magecraft; any-spell density for many prowess-like triggers.
* Noncreature spells often matter more than creatures for prowess-style pumps.
* Effect tags: [Magecraft](/effects/synergy/triggered/magecraft.md), [Gives Prowess](/effects/synergy/triggered/gives-prowess.md), [Cast Trigger You](/effects/synergy/triggered/cast-trigger-you.md).
