# Definition

A **cast event** is one successful casting of a spell. Storm, magecraft, prowess, and many “whenever you cast” triggers count cast events—not cards drawn or permanents entered.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Cheap spells, cantrips, and rituals increase cast density; expensive bombs do not.
* Casting from nonhand zones still counts as casting when the rules say so—see [Cast From Nonhand](/resources/spell/cast-from-nonhand.md).
