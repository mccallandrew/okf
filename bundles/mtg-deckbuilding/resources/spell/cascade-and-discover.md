# Definition

**Cascade and discover** (and cousins) cast or play extra cards from the library when you cast a spell—bonus cast events and card access chained off one spell.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Mana-value sequencing and deck construction (hit rate) define the resource quality.
* Giving cascade to cheap spells is a classic broken pattern—see [Gives Cascade](/effects/spell/cast/gives-cascade.md).
