# Definition

**Copied spells** multiply what a single card does on the stack—Fork effects, storm copies, and “copy target spell.” Copies are spell resources that often did not consume an extra card from hand.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Know whether the copy is cast (counts for storm/magecraft) or put on the stack.
* Copying opponents’ spells is theft-adjacent value—see also [Control](/effects/permanent/control/) for permanents.
* Effect hubs: [Copy](/effects/spell/copy/), [Copy-Spell](/effects/spell/copy/copy-spell.md)—do not list every `copy-*` subtype.
