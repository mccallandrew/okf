# Definition

**Instant speed** is the privilege to cast on the stack during almost any priority window. Instants and flash (and similar) turn mana and cards into reactive answers and end-step value.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Instant-speed interaction and draw change mulligans and mana holding patterns.
* Sorcery-speed combo needs protection or empty-stack windows; instant-speed combo is harder to punish.
* Flash on permanents blurs spell timing with board development—still a timing resource when casting.
