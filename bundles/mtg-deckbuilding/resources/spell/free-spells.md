# Definition

**Free spells** (and near-free alternate costs) are cast events that spend little or no mana—cost bypass, suspend payoffs, cascade freecasts, and similar. They spike [spell count](/resources/spell/spell-count.md) and tempo.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Overlap with [Cost Bypass](/effects/spell/cost-bypass/) and [Alternative Costs](/resources/mana/alternative-costs.md) / [Cost Reduction](/resources/mana/cost-reduction.md).
* “Free” often has a hidden cost (life, exile, board)—budget that resource too.
* Effect hubs: [Cost Bypass](/effects/spell/cost-bypass/), [Cost Ignorer](/effects/spell/cost-bypass/cost-ignorer.md).
