# Definition

**Storm** turns [spell count](/resources/spell/spell-count.md) into a pile of copies when a storm spell is cast. The resource is a high cast count *the same turn* before the storm payoff.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Rituals, cantrips, and free spells are storm fuel; the storm card is the sink.
* Storm copies are put on the stack—they are not cast (usually), so they may not grow magecraft the same way—read the payoff.
* Effect tags: [Gives Storm](/effects/spell/copy/gives-storm.md); hate: [Hate-Storm](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-storm.md).
