# Definition

**Stun counters** keep permanents from untapping (or similar stall text). Used offensively on opposing threats, they are a soft-removal counter resource; on your own permanents they are usually a drawback.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Tap/stun packages lock blockers and activated abilities without exiling.
* Removing stun counters (including as [Counter Fuel-Stun](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-fuel-stun.md)) can be upside for your side or release an opponent’s permanent.
* Often paired with tap effects under [Permanent → Tap](/effects/permanent/tap/).
