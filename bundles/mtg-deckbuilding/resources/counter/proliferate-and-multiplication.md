# Definition

**Proliferate and multiplication** increase counters you already have—proliferate chooses kinds among permanents/players, while doublers and “additional counter” replacements amplify placement. They turn a thin counter presence into a thick resource.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Best when you already sprinkle counters widely (+1/+1, planeswalkers, energy, poison, saga lore).
* Poison and experience proliferate as hard as +1/+1—know which clocks you accelerate.
* Pair with enablers that place the first counter; proliferate does nothing from zero.
* Few dedicated otags may appear under modify/matters; search Scryfall for proliferate and “double counters” alongside [Counters Matter](/effects/permanent/modify/counters-matter.md).
