# Definition

**Oil counters** are a storage/matters counter type strongly associated with Phyrexian designs—placed on permanents and spent or counted for abilities.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Build as a package: oil producers, oil spenders, and [Oil Counters Matter](/effects/permanent/modify/oil-counters-matter.md) payoffs.
* Proliferate and counter movement tricks apply as with charge.
* Effect tags: [Oil Counters Matter](/effects/permanent/modify/oil-counters-matter.md), [Counter Fuel-Oil](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-fuel-oil.md).
