# Definition

**Lore counters** track Saga chapters. The count is a clock toward later chapters and sacrifice—accelerating lore speeds the story; removing lore can stall or reset value.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Read-ahead and lore manipulation change when payoffs land; proliferate can leap chapters.
* Saga-matters decks treat the graveyard/recursion of Sagas as part of the same package.
* Effect tags: [Counter Fuel-Lore](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-fuel-lore.md); enchantment/Saga lines under [Enchantment](/resources/permanent/enchantment/).
