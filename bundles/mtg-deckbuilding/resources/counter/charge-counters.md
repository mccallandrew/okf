# Definition

**Charge counters** are the classic storage counter: artifacts and other permanents accumulate them to unlock abilities or serve as [counter fuel](/resources/counter/counter-fuel.md). Functionally similar storage appears as oil, lore, and time counters on other card types.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Charge-heavy artifacts want proliferate and counter placement; empty batteries are dead draws.
* Sagas use [lore counters](/resources/counter/lore-counters.md) on a fixed clock—different pacing than optional charge spend.
* Effect tags: [Counter Fuel-Charge](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-fuel-charge.md); related storage under oil/lore/time fuel tags.
