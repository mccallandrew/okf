# Definition

**Shield counters** prevent damage or destruction (per the counter’s rules text) and are then removed. They are a defensive counter resource—durability stored on the permanent.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Stacking shields on key threats buys turns against removal and combat.
* Proliferate adds shields; effects that remove counters as fuel can strip them—see [Counter Fuel-Shield](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-fuel-shield.md).
* Distinct from indestructible keyword counters, which grant a keyword instead of a one-shot prevention.
