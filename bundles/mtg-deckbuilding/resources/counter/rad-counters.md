# Definition

**Rad counters** are counters on players. They trigger radiation-style mill and life loss, scaling with the count. As a counter resource they sit with [energy](/resources/counter/energy.md) and [poison](/resources/progress/poison.md); as a designation-like pressure they are also under [Privilege → Rad Counters](/resources/privilege/rad-counters.md).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Give rad to opponents to clock libraries/life; self-rad only with mill or recursion payoffs.
* Proliferate multiplies rad—see [Proliferate And Multiplication](/resources/counter/proliferate-and-multiplication.md).
* Sparse otags—search Scryfall for rad counters.
