# Definition

**+1/+1 counters** permanently increase a creature’s power and toughness and are the default counter resource for “counters matter,” adapt/mentor/support lines, and many [counter fuel](/resources/counter/counter-fuel.md) costs.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Go-wide strategies want distributed counters; voltron wants stacks on one body.
* Anthems are not counters—temporary boosts do not proliferate; see [Board Presence](/effects/resource/board-presence/).
* Persist/undying and -1/-1 interactions can fight +1/+1 stacks—track both.
