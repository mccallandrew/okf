# Definition

**Time counters** usually track suspend (and similar delay clocks) or vanishing/fading. Removing time counters can cast suspended spells early; adding them delays or extends permanence.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Suspend decks want ways to remove time counters and to stack suspended threats.
* Vanishing permanents die when the last time counter is removed—proliferate can be upside or downside.
* Effect tags: [Counter Fuel-Time](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-fuel-time.md).
