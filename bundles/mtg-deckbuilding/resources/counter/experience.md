# Definition

**Experience counters** accumulate on a player—often from a commander—and scale payoffs for the rest of the game. They rarely leave once gained, so they are a long-term scaling resource.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Commander experience strategies want early, repeatable experience triggers and payoffs that scale with the count.
* Proliferate accelerates experience; few effects remove it.
* Singleton commanders that care about experience define the package; supporting cards should not dilute the plan.
