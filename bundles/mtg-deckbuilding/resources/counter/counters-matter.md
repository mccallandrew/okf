# Definition

**Counters matter** is the deckbuilding pattern of treating counters as a resource the rest of the deck rewards—extra power, draws, proliferate targets, or unique-counter payoffs. The resource is not one counter type but *having counters in play*.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Pick a primary counter type (+1/+1, energy, oil) and support it; “any counter” payoffs are flexible but weaker without density.
* [Unique Counters Matter](/effects/permanent/modify/unique-counters-matter.md) wants diversity of types—different build than pure +1/+1.
* Preservation and “counters remain” effects protect the resource through blinks and deaths—see [Counter Preservation](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-preservation.md), [Counters Remain](/effects/permanent/modify/counters-remain.md).
