# Definition

**Counter fuel** is spending counters—removing them from permanents or players—to pay for abilities. The counter is both a stored battery and a cost. Types include charge, +1/+1, energy, loyalty, oil, lore, time, shield, and keyword counters.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Ensure production rate exceeds spend rate, or the engine stalls.
* Spending +1/+1 counters shrinks creatures; spending loyalty risks losing the planeswalker; energy spend is on the player and does not shrink the board.
* “Any counter” fuel is flexible; typed fuel demands a matching package.
* Effect inventory: [Counter Fuel](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-fuel.md) and typed children (`counter fuel-pp`, `counter fuel-energy`, `counter fuel-charge`, …)—link the hub, not every subtype.
