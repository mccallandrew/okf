# Definition

**Loyalty** is the counter resource on planeswalkers: it is both hit points against damage and the currency of loyalty abilities (adding or removing loyalty). Killing a walker removes the resource entirely.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Plus abilities grow the resource; minus abilities spend it for impact—sequence toward ultimates only when the deck wants that clock.
* Proliferate and loyalty doublers accelerate walkers; creature pressure and [counterspell-loyalty-ability](/effects/spell/counter/counterspell-loyalty-ability.md) interaction punish them.
* [Counter Fuel-Loyalty](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-fuel-loyalty.md) spends loyalty in nonstandard ways.
