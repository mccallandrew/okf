# Definition

**Energy** is a counter resource on players (not permanents). You gain {E} from effects and spend it on abilities. It is frequently confused with [mana](/resources/mana/) but does not pay mana costs unless a card explicitly converts it.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Energy decks need both producers and sinks in the same package; stranded {E} does nothing.
* Proliferate hits players with energy; opponent energy is rarely your problem.
* Unlike rocks, energy is invisible on board and survives most board wipes.
* Effect tags are sparse by name—see [Counter Fuel-Energy](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-fuel-energy.md) and Scryfall `otag` / oracle searches for energy; contrast [Mana](/resources/mana/).
