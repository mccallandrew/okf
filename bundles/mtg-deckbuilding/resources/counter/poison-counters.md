# Definition

**Poison counters** are player counters that form an alternate loss condition (usually at ten). As a deckbuilding resource they are primarily a [progress](/resources/progress/poison.md) race, listed here because they are counters and proliferate with other counter strategies.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Infect/toxic decks maximize poison production; proliferate finishes low poison counts.
* Mixing poison with +1/+1 proliferate plans can dilute either clock—choose a primary win.
