# Definition

**Keyword counters** are counters named for keywords; a permanent with a flying counter has flying. They are movable ability resources and proliferate targets, distinct from printed keywords.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Useful for spreading evasion/keywords without auras; removal that exiles still answers the creature.
* Do not list every subtype here—use [Keyword Counter](/effects/permanent/modify/keyword-counter.md) and specific tags (`flying counter`, `menace counter`, …) when searching.
* [Counter Fuel-Keyword](/effects/permanent/modify/counter-fuel-keyword.md) spends them as cost.
