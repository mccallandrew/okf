# Definition

**Discard fuel** is hand cards you *want* to discard—loot/rummage fodder, madness enablers, or deliberate [graveyard](/resources/card/graveyard/) setup. The hand card is spent as payment rather than cast.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Decks with heavy loot/rummage need enough disposable cards (lands late, redundant pieces) so filtering does not pitch bombs blindly.
* Free and repeatable [discard outlets](/effects/zone/hand/discard-outlet.md) turn hand into a graveyard engine.
* Opponent discard is denial, not your fuel—see [Discard](/effects/zone/hand/discard.md) / [Hand Disruption](/effects/zone/hand/hand-disruption.md).
