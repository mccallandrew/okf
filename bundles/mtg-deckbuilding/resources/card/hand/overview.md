# Definition

The **hand** is the primary ready-to-use card zone: lands to play, spells to cast, and discards to pay. Hand size, hand quality, and vulnerability to discard all define how valuable the resource is.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Mulligans set opening hand quality; keep rules differ by format.
* Maximum hand size and discard-to-hand-size effects cap accumulation—plan outlets or [discard fuel](/resources/card/hand/discard-fuel.md).
* Opponent-facing discard attacks *their* hand resource; your discard outlets convert hand into [graveyard](/resources/card/graveyard/) fuel.
