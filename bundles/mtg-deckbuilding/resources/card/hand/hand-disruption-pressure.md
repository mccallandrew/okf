# Definition

**Hand disruption pressure** treats the *opponent’s* cards in hand as a resource you attack—discard, peek-and-take, and specter lines. Your gain is virtual [card advantage](/resources/card/card-advantage.md).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Strongest against combo and control hands; weaker when the opponent empties their hand quickly (aggro).
* Random discard vs choice discard changes which cards survive.
* Effect inventory: [Hand Disruption](/effects/zone/hand/hand-disruption.md), [Discard](/effects/zone/hand/discard.md), [Specter Ability](/effects/zone/hand/specter-ability.md).
