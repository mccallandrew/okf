# Definition

**Cards in hand** are the selectable options you can play or cast under normal timing. Each card is a unit of the hand resource until spent, discarded, or exiled.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Count actionable cards (castable this turn or soon), not just raw hand size.
* Bounce and recursion refill hand from board or graveyard; wheels replace the whole set.
* Effect paths that put cards into hand: [Draw](/effects/zone/draw/), [Impulse](/effects/resource/cards/impulse.md), [Regrowth](/effects/zone/graveyard-to-hand/regrowth.md), bounce under [Battlefield To Hand](/effects/zone/battlefield-to-hand/).
