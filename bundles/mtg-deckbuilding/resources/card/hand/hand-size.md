# Definition

**Hand size** is the count of cards in hand. Larger hands mean more options and more [card advantage](/resources/card/card-advantage.md) buffer; they also risk discarding to maximum hand size and present a fatter target for discard spells.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Seven is the usual maximum; effects that raise max hand size or let you skip cleanup discard protect large grips.
* “Draw seven” wheels spike hand size; empty-hand payoffs want the opposite.
* Life-for-cards and greed engines grow hand size at a cost—see [Life For Cards](/effects/resource/cards/life-for-cards.md), [Greed Ability](/effects/resource/cards/greed-ability.md).
