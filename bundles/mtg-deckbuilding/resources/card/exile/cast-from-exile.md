# Definition

**Cast from exile** is ongoing or effect-granted permission to cast exiled cards—broader than a single-turn impulse window. Exile becomes a persistent alternate hand.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Adventure, plot, and similar designs bake cast-from-exile into card text; global grants upgrade any exiled pile.
* Pairs with impulsive mill and discovery-style exile.
