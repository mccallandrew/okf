# Definition

**Exiled cards** you can still use—cast later, return, or reference—are card resources parked outside hand and graveyard. Inaccessible exile (hard removal) is not your resource.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Track which exiled cards are linked to which permissions (impulse pile vs haven vs imprint).
* Graveyard hate that exiles can accidentally fuel escapology or impulsive recursion lines.
