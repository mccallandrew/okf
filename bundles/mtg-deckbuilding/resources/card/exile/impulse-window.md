# Definition

An **impulse window** is short-lived permission to play or cast cards exiled from library (or similar)—classic red impulsive draw. Miss the window and the cards are usually gone.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Sequence mana and threats so the window is usable the turn it opens; long-term impulsive draw relaxes that pressure.
* Distinct from [Impulse](/effects/resource/cards/impulse.md) dig-to-hand (look at N, put in hand)—naming overlap in tags; both are card resources via different zones.
