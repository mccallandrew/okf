# Definition

**Exile** is usually a holding zone outside the game’s main loops—but many strategies treat accessible exile as a card resource: timed impulse windows, haven/purgatory storage, imprint references, and cast-from-exile permissions.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Distinguish *friendly* exile (you will cast or return it) from *hostile* exile (banish/removal)—only the former is your resource.
* Impulse is use-it-or-lose-it; haven delays return; imprint keeps a reference without casting.
