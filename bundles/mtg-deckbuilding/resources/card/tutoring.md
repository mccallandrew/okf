# Definition

**Tutoring** converts the [library](/resources/card/library/) from anonymous depth into a chosen card—usually into [hand](/resources/card/hand/), sometimes to battlefield or top. The resource is *guaranteed access* to a named role (removal, combo piece, land).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Tutors raise consistency and reduce effective deck size; they also shuffle and can be slow.
* Silver-bullet packages need enough tutors to justify the slots; toolbox decks are tutor-shaped.
* Effect inventory lives under [Search](/effects/zone/search/) (tutor tags). Random library→hand is [Seek](/effects/resource/cards/seek.md), not a classic tutor.
* [Gamble](/effects/zone/search/gamble.md)-style tutors trade certainty for speed.
