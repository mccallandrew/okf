# Definition

**Card advantage** as a game resource is being ahead in *usable* cards—extra draws, two-for-ones, or stranded opposing cards. Distinct from a single [card in hand](/resources/card/hand/cards-in-hand.md): it is the running balance of card access across zones.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Fit the plan: cantrips and selection for combo/tempo; reciprocal advantage for control; attrition for midrange; aggro often trades CA for tempo.
* Virtual advantage (discard, hate) denies the opponent’s [hand](/resources/card/hand/) without adding to yours.
* Commander budgets almost always need a dedicated draw/selection package—see [Category Budgets](/commander/category-budgets.md).
