# Definition

**Cast from graveyard** uses yard cards as if they were in hand (flashback, escape, unearth-as-cast, and similar). The graveyard becomes a second hand for those cards.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Cards with built-in cast-from-GY text are private resources; global “cast from GY” effects upgrade the whole yard.
* Timing and cost differ from reanimate-to-battlefield (no casting, different interaction).
