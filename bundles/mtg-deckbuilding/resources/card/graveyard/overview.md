# Definition

The **graveyard** is an alternate hand for the right strategies: reanimate targets, flashback/escape cards, delve/escape fuel, and recursion. Cards arrive via discard, mill, and the natural flow of play.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Build enable + payoff: self-mill/loot without recursion wastes the resource; recursion without density whiffs.
* Graveyard hate (exile, Rest in Peace effects) attacks this resource specifically—plan redundancy or fair lines.
* Distinguish returning to hand ([regrowth](/effects/zone/graveyard-to-hand/regrowth.md)), to battlefield ([reanimate](/effects/zone/graveyard-to-battlefield/)), and casting from GY ([cast-from-graveyard](/resources/card/graveyard/cast-from-graveyard.md)).
