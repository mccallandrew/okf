# Definition

**Graveyard cards** are specific objects you can recur, reanimate, cast, or exile for value. Quality matters: a Titan in yard is a different resource than a cantrip.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Reanimator packages want fat creatures plus enablers; flashback/adventure piles want spells that are good from yard.
* Public information: opponents can hate or play around known yard cards.
* Effect hubs: [Recursion](/effects/zone/graveyard-to-hand/recursion.md), [Regrowth](/effects/zone/graveyard-to-hand/regrowth.md), [Reanimate-Creature](/effects/zone/graveyard-to-hand/reanimate-creature.md) (and battlefield variants).
