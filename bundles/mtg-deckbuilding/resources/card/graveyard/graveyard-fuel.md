# Definition

**Graveyard fuel** is yard cards consumed as payment—delve, escape costs, and [graveyard fuel](/effects/zone/graveyard-to-hand/graveyard-fuel.md) abilities. Those cards leave the yard forever for mana reduction or activation.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Overlap with [Alternative Costs](/resources/mana/alternative-costs.md) when fuel pays for spells.
* Do not exile your only recursion targets for marginal savings.
* Effect tags: [Delve](/effects/zone/graveyard-to-hand/delve.md), [Graveyard Fuel](/effects/zone/graveyard-to-hand/graveyard-fuel.md).
