# Definition

**Cantrips** are cards that draw (or otherwise replace themselves) as they resolve or enter. They convert a [library](/resources/card/library/) card into a new [hand](/resources/card/hand/) card while still performing another job—net-neutral quantity with positive selection.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Raise consistency without eating as much of the “draw slot” as pure dig spells.
* Best when the cantrip’s primary mode is already on-plan (removal, ritual, tutor enabler).
* Density matters in combo and tempo; fair midrange can trip over too many soft cantrips.
* Effect tag: [Cantrip](/effects/resource/cards/cantrip.md). Pure draw without a second job lives under [Draw](/effects/zone/draw/).
