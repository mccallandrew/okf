# Definition

**Card selection** improves which cards you have access to—loot, rummage, impulse, scry/surveil, consult, wheels—often at neutral or negative raw card count. Quality of the [hand](/resources/card/hand/) and [top of library](/resources/card/library/top-of-library.md) is the resource.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Loot (draw then discard) vs rummage (discard then draw) differ when the discarded card must be known first; both feed [graveyard](/resources/card/graveyard/) strategies.
* Impulse digs without keeping every card; mulch/surveil deliberately stock the graveyard.
* Wheels and mini-wheels reset hands—explosive with empty-hand payoffs, disastrous when behind on board.
