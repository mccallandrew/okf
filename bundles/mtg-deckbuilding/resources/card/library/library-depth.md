# Definition

**Library depth** is how many cards remain. It is both untapped [card advantage](/resources/card/card-advantage.md) and a clock—decking loses, and mill strategies spend the opponent’s depth.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Large libraries (Commander) make pure mill harder; small formats make laboratory maniac and mill more relevant.
* Tutors and dig spells “spend” depth for selection; restock effects refill it from the graveyard.
* Effect tags: [Mill](/effects/zone/library/mill.md), [Mill-Self](/effects/zone/library/mill-self.md), [Mill-Opponent](/effects/zone/library/mill-opponent.md).
