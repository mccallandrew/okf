# Definition

The **top of library** is a near-term card resource when you can see, arrange, or play it—scry, surveil, impulse-to-top, future sight / precognition engines, and play-from-top permissions.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Brainstorm-style and scry effects make the top a planned draw; mismanagement strands bombs under lands.
* [Future Sight Engine](/effects/resource/cards/future-sight-engine.md) and [Precognition Engine](/effects/resource/cards/precognition-engine.md) turn the top into a continuous hand-like zone.
* [Play From Top](/effects/spell/cast-from-nonhand/play-from-top.md) / castable-from-library blur library and hand.
