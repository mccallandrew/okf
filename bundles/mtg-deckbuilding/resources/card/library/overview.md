# Definition

The **library** is the bulk of remaining card resources: unknown until drawn, tutored, revealed, or milled. Depth, top-card quality, and tutor density define how the library feeds other zones.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Fair decks treat the library as a draw pile; combo and toolbox decks treat it as a tutor menu.
* Self-mill converts library into [graveyard](/resources/card/graveyard/) on purpose; opponent mill races [library depth](/resources/card/library/library-depth.md) as a clock.
* Top-deck manipulation (scry, brainstorm-style, future sight) makes the next draws a near-term resource.
