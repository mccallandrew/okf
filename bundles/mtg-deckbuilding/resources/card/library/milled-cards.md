# Definition

**Milled cards** leave the library into the [graveyard](/resources/card/graveyard/) (or sometimes exile). For self-mill decks they are *setup*; for mill-to-win decks they are progress against [library depth](/resources/card/library/library-depth.md).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Self-mill plus recursion/reanimate treats mill as card selection into the graveyard.
* Surveil and mulch are selective mill; pure mill is not.
* [Mill-Exile](/effects/zone/library/mill-exile.md) feeds exile strategies instead of the graveyard.
