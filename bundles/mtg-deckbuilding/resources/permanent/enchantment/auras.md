# Definition

**Auras** attach to permanents and modify them. They are voltron and Pacifism-style resources—powerful when the host lives, card-disadvantageous when it dies.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Bestow, totem armor, and aura recursion mitigate two-for-ones.
