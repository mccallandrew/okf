# Definition

**Enchantments** on the battlefield provide continuous effects and trigger enchantress draw. They are high-stickiness board resources when creature sweepers are common.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Global enchantments scale without attacking; auras concentrate risk on one creature.
* Effect tags: [Removal-Enchantment](/effects/interaction/removal/removal-enchantment.md), [Flicker-Enchantment](/effects/permanent/blink/flicker-enchantment.md), [Animate Enchantment](/effects/permanent/modify/animate-enchantment.md).
