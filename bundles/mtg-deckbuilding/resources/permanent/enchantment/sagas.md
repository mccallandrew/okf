# Definition

**Sagas** are enchantments that read chapters as [lore counters](/resources/counter/lore-counters.md) accumulate, then usually sacrifice. They are timed value packages on the board.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Read-ahead and lore manipulation change pacing; proliferate can skip ahead.
* Recursion and blink can replay sagas; plan for the final chapter sacrifice.
