# Definition

**Enchantments** are sticky noncreature permanents—global engines, locks, auras, and sagas. They often dodge creature removal and reward enchantress-style card draw.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Enchantress decks treat enchantments as both engine and card advantage fuel.
* Vulnerable to [Hate-Enchantment](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-enchantment.md) and [Disenchant/Naturalize](/effects/interaction/removal/disenchant-naturalize.md).
* Saga clocks use [lore counters](/resources/counter/lore-counters.md).
