# Definition

A **permanent** on the battlefield is board presence you can attack with, block with, tap, sacrifice, or otherwise use: [creatures](/resources/permanent/creature/), [lands](/resources/permanent/land/), [artifacts](/resources/permanent/artifact/), [enchantments](/resources/permanent/enchantment/), [planeswalkers](/resources/permanent/planeswalker/), [battles](/resources/permanent/battle/), and [tokens](/resources/permanent/token/). Quantity (go-wide) and quality (threat density) both count as resources.

This branch is **not**:

* **[Permanent Effects](/effects/permanent/)** — create/destroy/sacrifice/blink/modify/control/tap *roles* (this tree links *to* those hubs).
* **[Removal](/effects/interaction/removal/)** — answers that remove permanents (link when discussing resilience).
* Cards still in hand/library/graveyard — those are [Card](/resources/card/) resources until they become permanents.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Match permanent types to the plan: creatures for combat, rocks/enchantments for engines, lands for mana and landfall.
* Tokens are permanents too—disposable fuel vs sticky threats is a build choice.
* Sweepers and edicts punish wide boards; hexproof/indestructible and recursion punish spot removal—plan for the expected answers.
* Do not explode every typal or create-* otag into a resource leaf; link [Create](/effects/permanent/create/), [Matters](/effects/synergy/matters/), and type hubs.
