# Definition

**ETB value** treats a permanent as a reusable effect package: the enter trigger is the resource, and blink/bounce/recursion re-buys it.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Blink and flicker multiply ETB—see [Blink](/effects/permanent/blink/), [Flicker](/effects/permanent/blink/flicker.md).
* Haven-style delayed exile is different storage—see [Haven](/resources/card/exile/haven.md).
* Cheat-into-play lines put ETB bodies into play without casting—[Cheat Into Play](/effects/zone/cheat-into-play/).
