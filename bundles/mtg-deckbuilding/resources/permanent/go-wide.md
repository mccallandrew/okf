# Definition

**Go wide** maximizes permanent *count*—token armies, weenie swarms, proliferating boards—then converts quantity via anthems, overrun, convoke, or aristocrats.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Needs payoffs that scale with count; raw 1/1s without anthem or sacrifice outlets are weak.
* Vulnerable to sweepers and edicts—hold recursion, protection, or a second wave.
* [Creature Tokens](/resources/permanent/token/creature-tokens.md) are the usual fuel; [Convoke](/effects/resource/mana/convoke.md) and [Alternative Costs](/resources/mana/alternative-costs.md) spend the army as mana.
