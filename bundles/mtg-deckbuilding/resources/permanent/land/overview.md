# Definition

**Lands** are permanents that usually produce mana and enable land-matters strategies. They are both [mana resources](/resources/mana/lands-as-mana.md) and board pieces that survive many sweepers.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Construction (counts, fixing) lives under [Mana](/mana/); this branch is the in-play land asset.
* Utility lands trade mana efficiency for effects; too many can stumble on color or enter-tapped rates.
* Land destruction and nonbasic hate are real in some metas—see [Hate-Nonbasic-Land](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-nonbasic-land.md), [Removal-Land](/effects/interaction/removal/removal-land.md).
