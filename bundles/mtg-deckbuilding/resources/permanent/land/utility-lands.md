# Definition

**Utility lands** contribute board effects—removal, card selection, manlands, recursion—at the cost of colored density or enter-tapped drawbacks.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Cap utility density so the manabase still casts spells on time.
* Manlands are combat bodies that dodge creature sweepers until activated.
