# Definition

**Sticky permanents** are board resources that survive or return through interaction—indestructible, hexproof/ward, regenerate, death triggers that replace themselves. They keep presence through removal.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Over-investing in sticky threats loses to exile, tuck, and sacrifice effects—diversify answers you fear.
* Enchantments and planeswalkers can be “sticky” by being outside creature removal—until disenchants and walker hate show up.
* Interaction inventory: [Removal](/effects/interaction/removal/), [Sweeper](/effects/interaction/removal/sweeper.md).
