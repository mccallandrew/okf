# Definition

**Battles** sit on the battlefield with defense counters; dealing combat damage to them progresses toward a transformed reward. They package future cards or permanents behind a siege.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Tempo decks may ignore battles; midrange and go-wide decks flip them efficiently.
* Defense counters interact with proliferate and counter removal—see [Counter](/resources/counter/).
