# Definition

**Battles** (typically Sieges) are permanents that convert attack damage into flipping rewards. They are board resources that ask you to siege them—often with your own creatures.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Plan enough attackers to flip on schedule; battles without a board stall.
* Opponents can also siege your battles in some rules contexts—know the format.
* Effect tags: [Removal-Battle](/effects/interaction/removal/removal-battle.md), [Animate Battle](/effects/permanent/modify/animate-battle.md).
