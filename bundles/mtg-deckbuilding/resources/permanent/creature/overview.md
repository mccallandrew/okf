# Definition

**Creatures** are the default combat permanent and a common engine chassis (ETBs, tap abilities, sacrifice). They are both [board presence](/resources/permanent/board-presence.md) and the usual target of removal.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Curve creatures to the plan—see construction [Mana Curve](/foundations/mana-curve.md) and [Mana Curve Construction](/mana/mana-curve-construction.md).
* Tokens vs nontokens: tokens excel at go-wide and fodder; nontokens often carry better stats and ETBs.
* Typal and power-matters packages live under [Matters](/effects/synergy/matters/)—link hubs, do not enumerate creature types here.
