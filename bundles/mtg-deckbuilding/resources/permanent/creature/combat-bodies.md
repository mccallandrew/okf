# Definition

**Combat bodies** are creatures whose main job is the red zone—clocks, chump blockers, and evasive beaters. Utility text is secondary to stats and keywords.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Aggro maximizes early bodies; midrange wants resilient threats; control wants finishers that end the game.
