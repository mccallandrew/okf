# Definition

**Creatures** on the battlefield attack, block, and host abilities. As a resource they are measured by rate (stats for cost), relevance (evasion, removal resistance), and engine text.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Separate beaters, blockers, and utility creatures when budgeting slots.
* Summoning sickness and tap timing matter for dorks and activated engines—see [Mana Dorks](/resources/mana/mana-dorks.md).
* Effect roles: [Create](/effects/permanent/create/), [Sacrifice](/effects/permanent/sacrifice/), [Removal-Creature](/effects/interaction/removal/removal-creature.md), power matters under [Matters](/effects/synergy/matters/).
