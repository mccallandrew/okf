# Definition

**Board presence** is your controlled permanents taken together—how much stuff you have that can attack, block, make mana, or fire engines. It is the umbrella resource that type-specific leaves refine.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Empty board + full hand is a different plan than empty hand + full board; know which resource you are converting.
* Anthems and team pumps scale with presence—see [Anthem](/effects/resource/board-presence/anthem.md).
* Presence without a win condition stalls; presence without interaction dies to sweepers.
