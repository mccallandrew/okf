# Definition

**Planeswalkers** are permanents whose [loyalty](/resources/counter/loyalty.md) is both durability and activation fuel. They provide repeatable advantage and ultimate win conditions.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Protect walkers with blockers and removal; proliferate accelerates them.
* Hate and burn to walkers are common—see [Hate-Planeswalker](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-planeswalker.md), [Removal-Planeswalker](/effects/interaction/removal/removal-planeswalker.md).
