# Definition

**Sacrifice fodder** is board presence you intend to spend—tokens, eggs, persist creatures—feeding [sacrifice outlets](/effects/permanent/sacrifice/sacrifice-outlet.md) and aristocrats drains.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Free and repeatable outlets turn fodder into a continuous engine; scarce outlets strand tokens.
* Prefer fodder that also attacks or blocks when idle.
* Effect hubs: [Sacrifice](/effects/permanent/sacrifice/), [Sacrifice Outlet](/effects/permanent/sacrifice/sacrifice-outlet.md), [Sacrifice Matters](/effects/permanent/sacrifice/sacrifice-matters.md), [Free Sacrifice Outlet](/effects/permanent/sacrifice/free-sacrifice-outlet.md).
