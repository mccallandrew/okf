# Definition

**Equipment** is an artifact resource that concentrates value on a creature. The board resource is the attached package—vulnerable to creature removal unless you can re-equip.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Voltron wants cheap equip costs and protection; go-wide wants living weapons or token makers.
