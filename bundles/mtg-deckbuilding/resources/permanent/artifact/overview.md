# Definition

**Artifacts** are permanents that are often colorless and synergistic with affinity, improvise, and artifact-matters payoffs. They include [mana rocks](/resources/mana/mana-rocks.md), equipment, vehicles, and engines.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Artifact decks want density plus payoffs ([Artifact Matters](/effects/synergy/matters/artifact-matters.md)); splash rocks without payoffs are just mana.
* Vulnerable to [Hate-Artifact](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-artifact.md) and [Disenchant/Naturalize](/effects/interaction/removal/disenchant-naturalize.md).
* Many tokens are artifacts (Treasure, Clue, Food)—see [Token](/resources/permanent/token/).
