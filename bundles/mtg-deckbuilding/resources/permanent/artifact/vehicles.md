# Definition

**Vehicles** are artifact permanents that become creatures via crew or other animation. They need crew fodder or alternate crewing to be combat resources.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Crew cost vs body quality defines the rate; tokens make crewing easy.
* Effect tags: [Animate Vehicle](/effects/permanent/modify/animate-vehicle.md), [Alternative Crewing](/effects/permanent/modify/alternative-crewing.md), [Removal-Vehicle](/effects/interaction/removal/removal-vehicle.md).
