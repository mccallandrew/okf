# Definition

**Tokens** are permanents created by effects rather than cast as cards. Many are purpose-built resources—[Treasure](/resources/mana/treasure.md), [Clue](/resources/permanent/token/clue.md), [Food](/resources/life/lifegain.md)—while creature tokens fuel [go-wide](/resources/permanent/go-wide.md) and [sacrifice](/resources/permanent/sacrifice-fodder.md).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Tokens usually do not return from graveyard; plan for exile hate and [Hate-Token](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-token.md).
* Creation effects are inventoried under [Create](/effects/permanent/create/) (hub may be sparse—also search Scryfall and related resource tags like repeatable clues/food).
* Do not explode every token subtype; keep the common resource tokens here.
