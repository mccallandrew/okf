# Definition

**Powerstone** tokens tap for colorless mana that often cannot be spent on nonartifact spells—restricted [colorless mana](/resources/mana/colorless-mana.md) on a token permanent.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Excellent in artifact decks; awkward in spell-heavy decks that cannot spend the mana.
* They persist as rocks do—more like dorks/rocks than Treasure.
