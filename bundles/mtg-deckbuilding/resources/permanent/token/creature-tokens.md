# Definition

**Creature tokens** are disposable combat bodies and the backbone of [go-wide](/resources/permanent/go-wide.md) and [sacrifice fodder](/resources/permanent/sacrifice-fodder.md) strategies.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Anthems and lords turn tokens into real clocks; without payoffs they trade poorly.
* Do not list every token creature type—link [Create](/effects/permanent/create/), [Anthem](/effects/resource/board-presence/anthem.md), [Hate-Token](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-token.md).
