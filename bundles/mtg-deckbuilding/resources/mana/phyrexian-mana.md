# Definition

**Phyrexian mana** symbols can be paid with one mana of the appropriate color **or** two life. They blur the line between [Colored Mana](/resources/mana/colored-mana.md) and [Life Payment](/resources/life/life-payment.md): the same spell can be cast off-color at a life cost.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Phyrexian costs enable **splash and off-color** lines; life totals become part of the mana plan.
* Aggressive decks treat the life as tempo; long games make repeated Phyrexian payments risky.
* Effects tagged [Phyrexian Mana](/effects/resource/life/phyrexian-mana.md) and [Phyrexian Mana Ability](/effects/resource/life/phyrexian-mana-ability.md) sit at the life/mana boundary—link both taxonomies.
* Commander and high-life formats make Phyrexian payments more attractive; Burn and poison races make them dangerous.
