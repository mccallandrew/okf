# Definition

**Colored mana** is mana of white, blue, black, red, or green. Spells and abilities with colored pips require matching colored mana (or appropriate hybrid/Phyrexian substitutions). Deckbuilding pressure comes from *producing* the right colors on the turns you need them—not merely from total mana.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Count **colored sources** and early pips separately from raw land count; see [Colored Sources](/mana/colored-sources.md) and [Pip Distribution](/mana/pip-distribution.md).
* Dual-faced and filter lands, birds/rocks that tap for any color, and Treasure all loosen color constraints.
* Devotion and color-matters payoffs care about colored mana *symbols on permanents*, not about floating mana—see [Devotion](/resources/counter/devotion.md).
* Hybrid and Phyrexian symbols change what “paying the color” means; Phyrexian is covered under [Phyrexian Mana](/resources/mana/phyrexian-mana.md).
