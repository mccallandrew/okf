# Definition

**Snow mana** is mana produced by a snow source (typically a snow permanent). Some costs and abilities require snow mana specifically; other cards care how much snow mana you spent or how many snow permanents you control.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Snow is a **package decision**: basic snow lands are nearly free upgrades in formats that allow them; duals and payoffs define whether the package is real.
* Producing snow mana usually still produces a color (snow-Covered Island taps for blue that is also snow-qualified).
* Payoffs that need “mana from a snow source” reward dense snow manabases over splashy non-snow rocks.
* Do not confuse snow *mana* with snow *permanents* as a board resource—both matter, different axes.
