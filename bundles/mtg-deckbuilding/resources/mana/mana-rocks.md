# Definition

**Mana rocks** are noncreature artifacts that increase available mana (typically by tapping for mana, sometimes with other modes). They are the artifact half of persistent nonland mana, distinct from [Mana Dorks](/resources/mana/mana-dorks.md) and zero-mana [Moxen](/resources/mana/moxen.md).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Rocks shine when land drops are contested, colors are awkward, or the format rewards artifact synergies (affinity, improvise).
* Evaluate **net mana** and **when** the rock turns on: tapped rocks and high-CMC rocks can fail tempo tests.
* [Utility Mana Rocks](/effects/resource/mana/utility-mana-rock.md) buy optionality at the cost of raw efficiency.
* Commander defaults to denser rock packages; many 60-card decks prefer dorks or land ramp instead.
