# Definition

**Land ramp** increases mana by putting lands onto the battlefield (search, put, or sneak) beyond what a normal [land drop](/resources/privilege/land-drop.md) would achieve. It is the most “fair” persistent ramp: harder to remove en masse than dorks, and it synergizes with landfall.

# Deckbuilding notes

* [Multi Land Ramp](/effects/resource/mana/multi-land-ramp.md) and [Extra Land](/effects/resource/mana/extra-land.md) define density; [Sneak-Land](/effects/resource/mana/sneak-land.md) bypasses “play” wording.
* Extra land *drops* ([Play Additional Land](/effects/resource/mana/play-additional-land.md)) are a [privilege](/resources/privilege/land-drop.md) that often plays in the same package.
* Ramp into tapped duals still checks colors—fetch/dual density matters.
* Landfall decks treat ramp spells as both mana and trigger engines ([Landfall](/effects/resource/mana/landfall.md)).
