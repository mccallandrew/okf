# Definition

**Mana dorks** are low-cost creatures that tap (or otherwise activate) for mana repeatedly. They accelerate like rocks but die to creature removal and contribute to creature counts (convoke, devotion, go-wide).

# Deckbuilding notes

* Best when the deck already wants creatures or can protect them; worst under heavy sweepers and pox effects.
* [Mana Dork Egg](/effects/resource/mana/mana-dork-egg.md) variants sacrifice for a burst—closer to a ritual than a rock.
* Green’s identity includes denser dork packages; other colors lean rocks or rituals.
* Summoning sickness and tap timing matter: a turn-one dork is a different resource than a turn-three Elvish Mystic equivalent.
