# Definition

**Rituals** are spells (or similar one-shots) that add mana—classic Dark Ritual lines. They increase mana **this turn** without leaving a land, rock, or dork behind. Closely related: effects that [add multiple mana](/effects/resource/mana/adds-multiple-mana.md) in one activation.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Rituals enable combo turns, storm, and temporary curve-breaks; they do not fix flooded midgames the way land ramp does.
* Color of ritual mana and net gain after the spell’s own cost define whether a card is a true ritual.
* Pair with [Mana Sinks](/resources/mana/mana-sinks.md) or a kill condition the same turn—unused ritual mana is wasted.
* Storm and spell-count decks also value the ritual as a [spell](/resources/spell/) cast.
