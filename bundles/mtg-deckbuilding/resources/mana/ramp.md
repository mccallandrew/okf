# Definition

**Ramp** is any effect that increases available mana for this turn or future turns: extra lands in play, rocks, dorks, rituals, or multipliers. In deckbuilding, “ramp package” usually means **persistent** acceleration that advances your curve, with rituals and Treasure called out separately as burst.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Match ramp type to plan: land ramp for green fair decks, rocks for Commander/artifact, dorks for creature shells, rituals for combo.
* Count ramp toward [Mana Curve Construction](/mana/mana-curve-construction.md): how early you cast your expensive threats.
* Over-ramping without payoffs or sinks strands mana—pair with threats and [Mana Sinks](/resources/mana/mana-sinks.md).
* Oracle-tag umbrella: [Ramp](/effects/resource/mana/ramp.md). Land-specific lines deepen under [Land Ramp](/resources/mana/land-ramp.md).
