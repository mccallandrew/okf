# Definition

**Lands** are the default persistent mana resource: they enter (usually) once per turn via a [land drop](/resources/privilege/land-drop.md) and tap for mana thereafter. As a game resource they are both mana producers and [land permanents](/resources/permanent/land/) that fuel landfall and land-matters strategies.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Construction choices—[Land Counts](/mana/land-counts.md), [Fixing and Utility Lands](/mana/fixing-and-utility-lands.md)—determine how reliable this resource is in play.
* Ramp that puts lands into play ([Land Ramp](/resources/mana/land-ramp.md)) increases persistent mana without spending the land drop on those lands.
* Taplands, painlands, and bounce lands trade rate or life for color; utility lands trade mana efficiency for effects.
* Distinguish **playing** a land (land drop) from **putting** a land onto the battlefield (ramp, sneak-land)—see [Extra Land](/effects/resource/mana/extra-land.md) and [Sneak-Land](/effects/resource/mana/sneak-land.md).
