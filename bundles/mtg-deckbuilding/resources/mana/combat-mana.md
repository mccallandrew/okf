# Definition

**Combat mana** is mana produced because you attacked or dealt combat damage—[combat ramp](/effects/resource/mana/combat-ramp.md), firebending, and similar. It ties the mana resource to the combat step and to having attackers.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Requires a board that can attack safely; pillowfort and fog strategies blunt this resource.
* Often colored and temporary (use it in second main or before damage leaves you exposed).
* Synergizes with go-wide, equip, and “attacks each turn if able” packages.
* Effect inventory: [Combat Ramp](/effects/resource/mana/combat-ramp.md), [Firebend-Like](/effects/resource/mana/firebend-like.md), [Gains Firebending](/effects/resource/mana/gains-firebending.md).
