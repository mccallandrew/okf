# Definition

**Mana multipliers** increase how much mana your existing producers make—classic “mana doublers” and [mana increasers](/effects/resource/mana/mana-increaser.md). They scale with board development rather than adding a new rock.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Multipliers are high-variance: weak on empty boards, broken with many lands or dorks.
* Often expensive enchantments or creatures—protect them or chain them the turn you cast them.
* Overlap with [Adds Multiple Mana](/effects/resource/mana/adds-multiple-mana.md) when a single activation yields multiple mana.
* Best in green ramp and Commander shells that reliably flood the board with producers.
