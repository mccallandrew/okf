# Definition

**Moxen** are (typically) zero-mana artifacts that tap for mana—named for the Alpha Mox cycle. They are the highest-rate persistent mana objects: castable for free, immediately accelerating colored or colorless mana.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Format legality and price gate most true Moxen; functional reprints and “mox-like” rocks inherit the same deckbuilding role at worse rates.
* They enable explosive curves and broken starts; mulligan and sequencing advice differs from fair rocks.
* Artifact and storm decks treat Moxen as both mana and spell/artifact count.
