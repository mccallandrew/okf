# Definition

**Colorless mana** is mana with no color. It pays generic costs and costs that specifically require colorless (for example some Eldrazi and Powerstone restrictions). Most lands that “tap for C” or artifacts that make colorless increase total mana without helping colored pips.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Colorless acceleration (many rocks, Sol lands, Powerstones) raises the curve but can leave you **color-screwed** if over-indexed.
* [Powerstone](/resources/permanent/token/powerstone.md) tokens produce colorless that often cannot be spent on nonartifact spells—restricted colorless.
* Wastes and similar sources matter in mono-colorless or Eldrazi shells; elsewhere they are usually inferior to colored sources.
* Distinguish floating colorless from **generic costs** on spells: generic can be paid with any mana, including colored.
