# Definition

**Alternative costs** (and cost-payment helpers) let you spend creatures (convoke), cards in graveyard (delve), artifacts (improvise), and similar instead of—or in addition to—mana. They convert other [game resources](/resources/) into casting power.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Build the **payment fuel**: creature tokens for convoke, graveyard density for delve, cheap artifacts for improvise.
* These lines interact with [Card](/resources/card/), [Permanent](/resources/permanent/), and [Spell](/resources/spell/) branches as much as with mana producers.
* [Cost Bypass](/effects/spell/cost-bypass/) and cast-without-paying effects are the extreme form—see spell effects.
* Convoke is also inventoried under [Convoke](/effects/resource/mana/convoke.md).
