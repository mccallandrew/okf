# Definition

**Treasure** tokens are artifact tokens that usually sacrifice for one mana of any color. They are **burst, floating-style mana** stored on the board until spent—bridging [Token](/resources/permanent/token/) permanents and mana currency. Related objects (Gold, Junk) play a similar role.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Treasure fixes colors and enables splashy turns without committing to rocks that stay on board.
* Cascading Treasure creation (pirate/dragon/sacrifice engines) becomes a mana engine; single Treasures are often combat or spell bonuses.
* Unlike lands, Treasures rarely produce mana every turn unless you keep making them—plan for **consumption**.
* Hate cards and edicts that hit artifacts interact with Treasure piles; see [Hate-Treasure](/effects/interaction/hate/hate-treasure.md).
* The permanent-side stub lives at [Treasure](/resources/permanent/token/treasure.md).
