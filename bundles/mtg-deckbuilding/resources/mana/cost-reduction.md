# Definition

**Cost reduction** lowers how much mana you must spend (affinity, generic reducers, commander tax mitigations in spirit). It is a mana resource in reverse: the same producers cast more spells. Affinity-style reduction scales with board counts.

# Deckbuilding notes

* Do not enumerate every affinity subtype here—use the [Affinity](/effects/resource/mana/affinity.md) effect hub and specific tags under [Mana Effects](/effects/resource/mana/) when searching.
* Reduction stacks with [Alternative Costs](/resources/mana/alternative-costs.md) differently than with rituals; read card text for “cost” vs “pay.”
* Artifact, typal, and enchantment decks often build boards that *are* the reduction engine.
* Convoke appears under both reduction and alternative payment depending on framing—see also [Convoke](/effects/resource/mana/convoke.md).
