# Game Resources

In-game currencies, board assets, and counts decks produce, spend, or race. Effect roles that *change* these live under [Effects](/effects/).

* [Game Resources](overview.md) - Ontology and boundaries for this taxonomy.
* [Mana](mana/) - Mana forms, producers, rituals, ramp, cost reduction, and sinks.
* [Life](life/) - Life total, lifegain/payoffs, payments, Phyrexian/fixing costs, drain, fog, commander damage.
* [Card](card/) - Card advantage, selection, tutoring; fuel by zone (hand, library, graveyard, exile).
* [Permanent](permanent/) - Board presence, go-wide, sacrifice/ETB value; creatures, lands, artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers, battles, tokens.
* [Counter](counter/) - Counters matter/fuel/proliferate; +1/+1, loyalty, energy, experience, keywords, storage types, devotion.
* [Spell](spell/) - Cast events, spell count/storm, cast payoffs, copies, timing, nonhand/recast, free spells, stack denial.
* [Privilege](privilege/) - Land drops, turns, designations (monarch, initiative, Ring, city’s blessing, day/night), dungeon/venture, commander zone, emblems, rad.
* [Progress](progress/) - Damage (combat/noncombat), poison, commander damage (EDH), mill-to-deck, alternate wins.
* [Social](social/) - Soft multiplayer standing (political capital, deals, threat presence, archenemy, card-driven heat); primarily Commander.
