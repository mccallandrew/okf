# Overview

[Commander Spellbook](https://commanderspellbook.com) catalogs multi-card combos with color-identity and card filters. Powers combo surfaces on EDHREC.

# When to use it

* Find win lines that fit a commander’s identity.
* Audit whether a “value” package accidentally enables infinite loops your table cares about.
* Study combo density when tuning power level.
