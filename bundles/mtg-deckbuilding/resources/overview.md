# Definition

A **game resource** is something a deck produces, spends, counts, or races toward a win—mana, cards in a zone, permanents, counters, spell events, turn privileges, progress markers such as damage or poison, and (in multiplayer) soft [social](/resources/social/) standing.

This taxonomy is distinct from:

* **[Resource Effects](/effects/resource/)** — oracle-tag *roles* that change resources (ramp, draw, lifegain).
* **[Mana](/mana/)** (construction) — curves, land counts, and pip math for building the deck.
* **[Builder Resources](/builder-resources/)** — external tools and sites.

| Goes here | Stays elsewhere |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Mana as a currency / producer / sink | Curve and land-count construction → [Mana](/mana/) |
| Cards by zone as fuel | Draw/search/recursion effects → [Zone Effects](/effects/zone/) |
| Creatures/lands as board assets | Create/destroy/modify effects → [Permanent Effects](/effects/permanent/) |
| Energy, +1/+1, loyalty | Under [Counters](/resources/counter/) (energy is **not** mana) |
| Land drop, extra turns, designations, commander zone | Under [Privileges](/resources/privilege/) |
| Damage / poison / mill / alt-wins (EDH: commander damage) | Under [Progress](/resources/progress/) |
| Political capital, deals, archenemy, card-driven heat (Commander) | Under [Social](/resources/social/) |
