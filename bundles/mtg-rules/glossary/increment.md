# Definition

**Increment** — A keyword ability that lets you put a +1/+1 counter on a creature whenever you cast a spell if the amount of mana you spent on it is greater than that creature’s power or…[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A keyword ability that lets you put a +1/+1 counter on a creature whenever you cast a spell if the amount of mana you spent on it is greater than that creature’s power or toughness.
* See rule 702.191, “Increment.”
