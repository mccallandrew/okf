# Definition

**Blocking Creature** — A creature that has either been declared as part of a legal block during the combat phase (once all costs to block, if any, have been paid), or a creature that has been put…[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A creature that has either been declared as part of a legal block during the combat phase (once all costs to block, if any, have been paid), or a creature that has been put onto the battlefield blocking.
* It remains a blocking creature until it’s removed from combat or the combat phase ends, whichever comes first.
