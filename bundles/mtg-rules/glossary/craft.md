# Definition

**Craft** — Craft is an activated ability that allows a player to exile cards from their graveyard and/or permanents they control to exile the permanent with the craft ability and return…[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* Craft is an activated ability that allows a player to exile cards from their graveyard and/or permanents they control to exile the permanent with the craft ability and return it onto the battlefield transformed.
* See rule 702.167, “Craft.”
