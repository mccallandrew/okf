# Definition

**Backup** — A keyword ability that lets a creature give +1/+1 counters to itself or another when it enters the battlefield.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A keyword ability that lets a creature give +1/+1 counters to itself or another when it enters the battlefield.
* If a different creature is chosen, that creature also temporarily gains one or more abilities.
