# Definition

**Excess Damage** — Damage dealt to a creature greater than what would be lethal damage or damage dealt to a planeswalker greater than its loyalty.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* Damage dealt to a creature greater than what would be lethal damage or damage dealt to a planeswalker greater than its loyalty.
