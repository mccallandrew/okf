# Definition

**Attacks and Isn’t Blocked** — An ability that triggers when a creature “attacks and isn’t blocked” triggers when the creature becomes an unblocked attacking creature.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* An ability that triggers when a creature “attacks and isn’t blocked” triggers when the creature becomes an unblocked attacking creature.
* See rule 509.1h.
