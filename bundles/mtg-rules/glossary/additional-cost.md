# Definition

**Additional Cost** — A cost a spell may have that its controller may pay (or, in some cases, must pay) in addition to its mana cost to cast that spell.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A cost a spell may have that its controller may pay (or, in some cases, must pay) in addition to its mana cost to cast that spell.
* See rule 118, “Costs,” and rule 601, “Casting Spells.”
