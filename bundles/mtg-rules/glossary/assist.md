# Definition

**Assist** — A keyword ability that lets another player help you pay for a spell.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A keyword ability that lets another player help you pay for a spell.
* See rule 702.132, “Assist.” At End of Turn (Obsolete) A trigger condition printed on abilities that triggered at the beginning of the end step (which is not the last thing to happen in the turn).
