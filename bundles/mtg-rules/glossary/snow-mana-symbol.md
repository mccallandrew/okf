# Definition

**Snow Mana Symbol** — The snow mana symbol {S} represents a cost that can be paid with one mana produced by a snow source.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* The snow mana symbol {S} represents a cost that can be paid with one mana produced by a snow source.
* It can also represent mana from a snow source that was spent to pay a cost.
