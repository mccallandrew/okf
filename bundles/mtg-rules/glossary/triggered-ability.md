# Definition

**Triggered Ability** — A kind of ability.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A kind of ability.
* Triggered abilities begin with the word “when,” “whenever,” or “at.” They’re written as “[Trigger condition], [effect].” See rule 113, “Abilities,” and rule 603, “Handling Triggered Abilities.”
