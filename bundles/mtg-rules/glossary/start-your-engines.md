# Definition

**Start Your Engines!** — A keyword ability that gives a player a speed of 1 if they have no speed.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A keyword ability that gives a player a speed of 1 if they have no speed.
* Once a player has speed, their speed increases once on each of their turns when an opponent loses life.
