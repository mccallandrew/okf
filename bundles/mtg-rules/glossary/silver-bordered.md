# Definition

**Silver-Bordered** — Cards in certain sets and certain promotional cards are printed with a silver border.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* Cards in certain sets and certain promotional cards are printed with a silver border.
* Silver-bordered cards are intended for casual play and may have features and text that aren’t covered by these rules.
