# Definition

**Cast** — To take a card from where it is (usually the hand), put it on the stack, and pay its costs, so that it will eventually resolve and have its effect.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* To take a card from where it is (usually the hand), put it on the stack, and pay its costs, so that it will eventually resolve and have its effect.
* See rule 601, “Casting Spells.” Caster (Obsolete) An obsolete term that referred to the player who cast a spell.
