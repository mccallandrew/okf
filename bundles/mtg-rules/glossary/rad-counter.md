# Definition

**Rad Counter** — A type of counter a player can have that causes that player to mill cards at the beginning of their precombat main phase, then lose 1 life and remove one rad counter for each…[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A type of counter a player can have that causes that player to mill cards at the beginning of their precombat main phase, then lose 1 life and remove one rad counter for each nonland card milled this way.
* See rule 122, “Counters,” and rule 728, “Rad Counters.”
