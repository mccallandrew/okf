# Definition

**Shared Life Total** — In the Two-Headed Giant multiplayer variant, each team has a “shared life total” rather than each player having an individual life total.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* In the Two-Headed Giant multiplayer variant, each team has a “shared life total” rather than each player having an individual life total.
* See rule 810, “Two-Headed Giant Variant.”
