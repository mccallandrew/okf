# Definition

**Blocked Creature** — An attacking creature that another creature blocks or that an effect causes to become blocked.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* An attacking creature that another creature blocks or that an effect causes to become blocked.
* It remains a blocked creature until it’s removed from combat, an effect says that it becomes unblocked, or the combat phase ends, whichever comes first.
