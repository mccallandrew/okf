# Definition

**Placed** — (Obsolete) Some spells and abilities previously referred to a counter being “placed” on a permanent.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* (Obsolete) Some spells and abilities previously referred to a counter being “placed” on a permanent.
* These cards have received errata in the Oracle card reference to use the term “put” instead.
