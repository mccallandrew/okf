# Definition

**Earthbend** — A keyword action that causes a land to become a 0/0 creature with haste in addition to its other types and puts a number of +1/+1 counters on it.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A keyword action that causes a land to become a 0/0 creature with haste in addition to its other types and puts a number of +1/+1 counters on it.
* When that land dies or is put into exile, return it to the battlefield tapped under your control.
