# Definition

**Companion** — A keyword ability that allows a player to choose one creature card from outside the game as a companion if the restriction of that card’s companion ability is met.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A keyword ability that allows a player to choose one creature card from outside the game as a companion if the restriction of that card’s companion ability is met.
* Once a player has chosen a companion, that player may pay {3} to put it into their hand once during the game.
