# Definition

**Enters the Battlefield** — A nontoken permanent enters the battlefield when it’s moved onto the battlefield from another zone.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A nontoken permanent enters the battlefield when it’s moved onto the battlefield from another zone.
* A token enters the battlefield as it’s created.
