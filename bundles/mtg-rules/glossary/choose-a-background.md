# Definition

**Choose a Background** — A variant of the partner ability that lets two legendary permanent cards be your commander in the Commander variant rather than one if one of them has the “choose a Background”…[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A variant of the partner ability that lets two legendary permanent cards be your commander in the Commander variant rather than one if one of them has the “choose a Background” ability and the other is a Background enchantment card.
* See rule 702.124, “Partner,” and rule 903, “Commander.”
