# Glossary

Thin stubs for Comprehensive Rules Glossary entries. Prefer linked rule concepts when present; the CR Glossary remains authoritative.

## A

* [Abandon](abandon.md) - To turn a face-up ongoing scheme card face down and put it on the bottom of its owner’s scheme deck.
* [Ability](ability.md) - Text on an object that explains what that object does or can do.
* [Ability Word](ability-word.md) - An italicized word with no rules meaning that ties together abilities on different cards that have similar functionality.
* [Absorb](absorb.md) - A keyword ability that prevents damage.
* [Activate](activate.md) - To put an activated ability onto the stack and pay its costs, so that it will eventually resolve and have its effect.
* [Activated Ability](activated-ability.md) - A kind of ability.
* [Activation Cost](activation-cost.md) - Everything that appears before the colon in an activated ability’s text.
* [Active Player](active-player.md) - The player whose turn it is.
* [Active Player, Nonactive Player Order](active-player-nonactive-player-order.md) - A system that determines the order by which players make choices if multiple players are instructed to make choices at the same time.
* [Active Team](active-team.md) - The team whose turn it is in a game using the shared team turns option.
* [Adapt](adapt.md) - A keyword action that puts +1/+1 counters on a creature that doesn’t have any yet.
* [Additional Cost](additional-cost.md) - A cost a spell may have that its controller may pay (or, in some cases, must pay) in addition to its mana cost to cast that spell.
* [Adventurer Card](adventurer-card.md) - Cards with a two-part card frame (one part of which is inset on the left) on a single card where the alternative characteristics include the Adventure spell type.
* [Affinity](affinity.md) - A keyword ability that reduces how much mana you need to spend to cast a spell.
* [Afflict](afflict.md) - A keyword ability that makes the defending player lose life for blocking.
* [Afterlife](afterlife.md) - A keyword ability that leaves behind Spirit creature tokens when certain creatures die.
* [Aftermath](aftermath.md) - A keyword ability that lets a player cast one half of a split card only from their graveyard.
* [Airbend](airbend.md) - A keyword action that exiles one or more permanents and/or spells.
* [Alternate Name](alternate-name.md) - A different name used on promotional or alternate-art versions of some cards.
* [Alternating Teams Variant](alternating-teams-variant.md) - A multiplayer variant played among two or more teams of equal size.
* [Alternative Cost](alternative-cost.md) - A cost a spell may have that its controller can pay rather than paying its mana cost.
* [Amass](amass.md) - A keyword action that gives you a Zombie Army creature token or grows an Army you already have.
* [Amplify](amplify.md) - A keyword ability than can have a creature enter the battlefield with +1/+1 counters on it.
* [Anchor Word](anchor-word.md) - A word that precedes one of two abilities a permanent may enter the battlefield with.
* [Annihilator](annihilator.md) - A keyword ability that can make a creature particularly brutal when it attacks.
* [Ante](ante.md) - A zone used only when playing “for keeps.” 2.
* [APNAP Order](apnap-order.md) - See Active Player, Nonactive Player Order.
* [Archenemy](archenemy.md) - A casual variant in which a team of players faces off against a single opponent strengthened with powerful scheme cards.
* [Archenemy Commander](archenemy-commander.md) - A Commander game that follows a modified version of the Archenemy rules.
* [Artifact](artifact.md) - A card type.
* [Artifact Creature](artifact-creature.md) - A combination of artifact and creature that’s subject to the rules for both.
* [Artifact Land](artifact-land.md) - A combination of artifact and land that’s subject to the rules for both.
* [Artifact Type](artifact-type.md) - A subtype that’s correlated to the artifact card type.
* [As Though](as-though.md) - Text used to indicate that the game, for some specific purpose, treats a condition as true even though it’s not.
* [Ascend](ascend.md) - A keyword causing a player to get the designation of the city’s blessing once they control ten permanents.
* [Assemble](assemble.md) - Assemble is a keyword action in the Unstable set that puts Contraptions onto the battlefield.
* [Assign Combat Damage](assign-combat-damage.md) - To determine how an attacking or blocking creature will deal its combat damage.
* [Assist](assist.md) - A keyword ability that lets another player help you pay for a spell.
* [Attach](attach.md) - To take an Aura, Equipment, or Fortification from where it currently is and put it onto a specified object or player.
* [Attack](attack.md) - To send a creature into combat offensively.
* [Attack Alone](attack-alone.md) - A creature “attacks alone” if it’s the only creature declared as an attacker during the declare attackers step.
* [Attack Left Option](attack-left-option.md) - An option that may be used in certain multiplayer variants.
* [Attack Multiple Players Option](attack-multiple-players-option.md) - An option that may be used in certain multiplayer variants.
* [Attack Right Option](attack-right-option.md) - An option that may be used in certain multiplayer variants.
* [Attacking Creature](attacking-creature.md) - A creature that has either been declared as part of a legal attack during the combat phase (once all costs to attack, if any, have been paid), or a creature that has been put…
* [Attacking Team](attacking-team.md) - The team who can attack during the combat phase of a multiplayer game using the shared team turns option.
* [Attacks and Isn’t Blocked](attacks-and-isn-t-blocked.md) - An ability that triggers when a creature “attacks and isn’t blocked” triggers when the creature becomes an unblocked attacking creature.
* [Attraction](attraction.md) - An artifact type seen only on nontraditional Magic cards in the Unfinity expansion.
* [Attraction Deck](attraction-deck.md) - An optional deck of at least three (in limited play) or ten (in constructed play) Attraction cards that can be used to support play with some cards from the Unfinity expansion.
* [Aura](aura.md) - An enchantment subtype.
* [Aura Swap](aura-swap.md) - A keyword ability that lets you exchange an Aura on the battlefield with one in your hand.
* [Awaken](awaken.md) - A keyword ability that lets you turn a land you control into a creature.

* [Beginning Phase](beginning-phase.md) - Part of the turn.

* [Blight](blight.md) - A keyword action.
* [Blitz](blitz.md) - A keyword ability found on creature cards that allows them to be cast for an alternative cost.
* [Block](block.md) - To send a creature into combat defensively.
* [Block Alone](block-alone.md) - A creature “blocks alone” if it’s the only creature declared as a blocker during the declare blockers step.
* [Blocked Creature](blocked-creature.md) - An attacking creature that another creature blocks or that an effect causes to become blocked.
* [Blocking Creature](blocking-creature.md) - A creature that has either been declared as part of a legal block during the combat phase (once all costs to block, if any, have been paid), or a creature that has been put…

* [Bloodthirst](bloodthirst.md) - A keyword ability that can have a creature enter the battlefield with +1/+1 counters on it.
* [Boast](boast.md) - A special kind of activated ability that can be activated only once each turn if the creature with the boast ability attacked that turn.
* [Bolster](bolster.md) - A keyword action that puts +1/+1 counters on the weakest creature a player controls.
* [Booster Pack](booster-pack.md) - A group of unopened Magic cards from a particular expansion.
* [Brawl](brawl.md) - An option for the Commander casual variant.
* [Bushido](bushido.md) - A keyword ability that can make a creature better in combat.
* [Buyback](buyback.md) - A keyword ability of instants and sorceries that can let the spell return to its owner’s hand as it resolves.
