# Definition

**Leaves the Battlefield** — A permanent “leaves the battlefield” when it’s moved from the battlefield to another zone, or (if it’s phased in) when it leaves the game because its owner leaves the game.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* A permanent “leaves the battlefield” when it’s moved from the battlefield to another zone, or (if it’s phased in) when it leaves the game because its owner leaves the game.
* See rules 603.6c and 603.10.
