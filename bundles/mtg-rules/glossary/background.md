# Definition

**Background** — An enchantment type that, in combination with the “choose a Background” ability, may allow a legendary enchantment card to be your commander.[^cr-glossary]

# Rules

* An enchantment type that, in combination with the “choose a Background” ability, may allow a legendary enchantment card to be your commander.
* See rule 702.124, “Partner,” and rule 903, “Commander.”
