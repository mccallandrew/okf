# Definition

**Extort** — Extort is a triggered ability.[^cr-702-101]

# Rules

* Extort is a triggered ability.
* “Extort” means “Whenever you cast a spell, you may pay {W/B}.
* If you do, each opponent loses 1 life and you gain life equal to the total life lost this way.”
