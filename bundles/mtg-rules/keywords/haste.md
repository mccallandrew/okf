# Definition

**Haste** lets a creature ignore [summoning sickness](/foundations/summoning-sickness.md) for attacking and for activating {T} or {Q} abilities.[^cr-702-10]

# Rules

* Without haste, a creature cannot attack or use tap/untap activated abilities unless it has been under your continuous control since your most recent turn began.
* Haste does not untap the creature or grant additional attacks.
