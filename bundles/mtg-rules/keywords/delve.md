# Definition

**Delve** — Delve is a static ability that functions while the spell with delve is on the stack.[^cr-702-66]

# Rules

* Delve is a static ability that functions while the spell with delve is on the stack.
* “Delve” means “For each generic mana in this spell’s total cost, you may exile a card from your graveyard rather than pay that mana.”
