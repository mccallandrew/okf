# Definition

**Hideaway** — Hideaway is a triggered ability.[^cr-702-75]

# Rules

* Hideaway is a triggered ability.
* “Hideaway N” means “When this permanent enters, look at the top N cards of your library.
* Exile one of them face down and put the rest on the bottom of your library in a random order.
