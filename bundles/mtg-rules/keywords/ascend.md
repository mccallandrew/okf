# Definition

**Ascend** — Ascend on an instant or sorcery spell represents a spell ability.[^cr-702-131]

# Rules

* Ascend on an instant or sorcery spell represents a spell ability.
* It means “If you control ten or more permanents and you don’t have the city’s blessing, you get the city’s blessing for the rest of the game.”
