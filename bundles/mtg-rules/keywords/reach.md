# Definition

**Reach** allows a creature to block creatures with [flying](/keywords/flying.md).[^cr-702-17]

# Rules

* Reach does not grant flying; a reach creature can still be blocked by nonflying creatures.
* Reach only matters when declaring blockers against flying attackers.
