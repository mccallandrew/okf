# Definition

**Foretell** — Foretell is a keyword that functions while the card with foretell is in a player’s hand.[^cr-702-143]

# Rules

* Foretell is a keyword that functions while the card with foretell is in a player’s hand.
* Any time a player has priority during their turn, that player may pay {2} and exile a card with foretell from their hand face down.
* That player may look at that card as long as it remains in exile.
