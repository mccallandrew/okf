# Definition

**Lifelink** means damage dealt by this source also causes its controller to gain that much life.[^cr-702-15]

# Rules

* Life gain happens as the damage is dealt; it is not a separate trigger.
* Multiple instances of lifelink are redundant.
* Prevention that stops damage also stops the associated life gain.
