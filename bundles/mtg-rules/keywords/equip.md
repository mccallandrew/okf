# Definition

**Equip** is an activated ability of Equipment. "Equip [cost]" means "[Cost]: Attach this permanent to target creature you control. Activate only as a sorcery."[^cr-702-6]

# Rules

* You may activate equip only during your main phase when the stack is empty (as a sorcery).
* Variants such as "Equip [quality] creature" further restrict legal targets but do not prevent the Equipment from remaining attached to other creatures if already attached illegally by other effects.
* A permanent may have multiple equip abilities; any of them may be activated.
* "Equip planeswalker" attaches to a planeswalker you control as though it were a creature.
