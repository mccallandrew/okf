# Definition

**Indestructible** means the permanent cannot be destroyed. Lethal damage and effects that say "destroy" do not destroy it.[^cr-702-12]

# Rules

* Indestructible permanents can still be exiled, returned to hand, sacrificed, or have toughness reduced to 0 (0 toughness is not "destroy").
* Damage is still marked; it just does not cause destruction via lethal damage.
* Regeneration is irrelevant if the permanent is never destroyed.
