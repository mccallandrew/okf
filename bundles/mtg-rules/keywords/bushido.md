# Definition

**Bushido** — Bushido is a triggered ability.[^cr-702-45]

# Rules

* Bushido is a triggered ability.
* “Bushido N” means “Whenever this creature blocks or becomes blocked, it gets +N/+N until end of turn.” (See rule 509, “Declare Blockers Step.”)
