# Definition

**Improvise** — Improvise is a static ability that functions while the spell with improvise is on the stack.[^cr-702-126]

# Rules

* Improvise is a static ability that functions while the spell with improvise is on the stack.
* “Improvise” means “For each generic mana in this spell’s total cost, you may tap an untapped artifact you control rather than pay that mana.”
