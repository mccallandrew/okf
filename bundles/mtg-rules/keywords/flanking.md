# Definition

**Flanking** — Flanking is a triggered ability that triggers during the declare blockers step.[^cr-702-25]

# Rules

* Flanking is a triggered ability that triggers during the declare blockers step.
* (See rule 509, “Declare Blockers Step.”) “Flanking” means “Whenever this creature becomes blocked by a creature without flanking, the blocking creature gets -1/-1 until end of turn.”
