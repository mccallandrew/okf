# Definition

**Kicker** — Kicker is a static ability that functions while the spell with kicker is on the stack.[^cr-702-33]

# Rules

* Kicker is a static ability that functions while the spell with kicker is on the stack.
* “Kicker [cost]” means “You may pay an additional [cost] as you cast this spell.” Paying a spell’s kicker cost(s) follows the rules for paying additional costs in rules 601.2b and 601.2f–h.
