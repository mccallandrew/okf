# Definition

**First strike** creates an extra combat damage step before the normal one. Only creatures with first strike or double strike assign combat damage in that step.[^cr-702-7]

# Rules

* Creatures destroyed in the first-strike step do not deal damage in the normal step.
* Creatures with only first strike do not deal damage again in the normal combat damage step.
* Double strike deals damage in both steps.
