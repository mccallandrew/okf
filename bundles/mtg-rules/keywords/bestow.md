# Definition

**Bestow** — Bestow represents a static ability that functions in any zone from which you could play the card it’s on.[^cr-702-103]

# Rules

* Bestow represents a static ability that functions in any zone from which you could play the card it’s on.
* “Bestow [cost]” means “As you cast this spell, you may choose to cast it bestowed.
* If you do, you pay [cost] rather than its mana cost.” Casting a spell using its bestow ability follows the rules for paying alternative costs (see 601.2b and 601.2f–h).
