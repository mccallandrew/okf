# Definition

**Deathtouch** means any nonzero amount of damage this source deals to a creature is considered lethal for destruction (state-based actions) and for combat damage assignment with trample.[^cr-702-2]

# Rules

* Applies to combat and noncombat damage from a source with deathtouch.
* Indestructible creatures are not destroyed by lethal damage.
* With trample, assigning 1 damage to each blocker with deathtouch can be enough before excess goes through.
