# Definition

**Flash** means you may cast this spell any time you could cast an [instant](/card-types/instant.md).[^cr-702-8]

# Rules

* Flash overrides the normal timing for permanents and sorceries that have it.
* You still need priority; flash does not let you cast without priority.
* Granting flash to a card in another zone allows casting it at instant speed if you cast it.
