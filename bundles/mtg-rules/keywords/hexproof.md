# Definition

**Hexproof** on a permanent or player means it cannot be the target of spells or abilities opponents control.[^cr-702-11]

# Rules

* You can still target your own hexproof permanents.
* Hexproof does not stop nontargeted effects ("destroy all creatures").
* Hexproof from [quality] restricts only sources with that quality.
