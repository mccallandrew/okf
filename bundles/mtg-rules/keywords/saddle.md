**Saddle** — Saddle is an activated ability.[^cr-702-171]

# Rules

* Saddle is an activated ability.
* “Saddle N” means “Tap any number of other untapped creatures you control with total power N or greater: This permanent becomes saddled until end of turn.
* Activate only as a sorcery.”
