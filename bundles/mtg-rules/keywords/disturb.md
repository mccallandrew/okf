# Definition

**Disturb** — Disturb is an ability found on the front face of some double-faced cards (see rule 712, “Double-Faced Cards”).[^cr-702-146]

# Rules

* Disturb is an ability found on the front face of some double-faced cards (see rule 712, “Double-Faced Cards”).
* “Disturb [cost]” means “You may cast this card transformed from your graveyard by paying [cost] rather than its mana cost.” See rule 712.8c.
