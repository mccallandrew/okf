# Definition

**Defender** is a static ability. A creature with defender can't attack.[^cr-702-3]

# Rules

* Defender does not stop blocking or activating abilities.
* Multiple instances of defender on the same creature are redundant.
* Effects that allow a creature to attack "as though it didn't have defender" override this restriction.
