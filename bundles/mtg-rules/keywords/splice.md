# Definition

**Splice** — Splice is a static ability that functions while a card is in your hand.[^cr-702-47]

# Rules

* Splice is a static ability that functions while a card is in your hand.
* “Splice onto [quality] [cost]” means “You may reveal this card from your hand as you cast a [quality] spell.
* If you do, that spell gains the text of this card’s rules text and you pay [cost] as an additional cost to cast that spell.” Paying a card’s splice cost follows the rules for paying additional costs in rules 601.2b…
