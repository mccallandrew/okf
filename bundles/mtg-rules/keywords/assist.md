# Definition

**Assist** — Assist is a static ability that modifies the rules of paying for the spell with assist (see rules 601.2g-h).[^cr-702-132]

# Rules

* Assist is a static ability that modifies the rules of paying for the spell with assist (see rules 601.2g-h).
* If the total cost to cast a spell with assist includes a generic mana component, before you activate mana abilities while casting it, you may choose another player.
* That player has a chance to activate mana abilities.
