**Unearth** — Unearth is an activated ability that functions while the card with unearth is in a graveyard.[^cr-702-84]

# Rules

* Unearth is an activated ability that functions while the card with unearth is in a graveyard.
* “Unearth [cost]” means “[Cost]: Return this card from your graveyard to the battlefield.
