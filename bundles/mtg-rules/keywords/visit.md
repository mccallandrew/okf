**Visit** — Visit is a keyword ability found on Attraction cards (see rule 717).[^cr-702-159]

# Rules

* Visit is a keyword ability found on Attraction cards (see rule 717).
* “Visit — [Effect]” means “Whenever you roll to visit your Attractions, if the result is equal to a number that is lit up on this Attraction, [effect].” See rule 701.52, “Roll to Visit Your Attractions.”
