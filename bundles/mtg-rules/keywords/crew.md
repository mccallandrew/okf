# Definition

**Crew** is an activated ability of [Vehicle](/card-types/vehicle.md) cards. “Crew N” means: tap any number of other untapped creatures you control with total power N or greater — this permanent becomes an artifact creature until end of turn.[^cr-702-122]

# Rules

* A creature “crews a Vehicle” when it is tapped to pay that Vehicle’s crew cost.
* Effects that say a creature “can’t crew Vehicles” stop it from being tapped to pay a crew cost.
* “Whenever [this Vehicle] becomes crewed” means whenever a crew ability of that Vehicle resolves.
* While a Vehicle is a creature (via crew or another effect), it uses its printed power and toughness, subject to continuous effects, and can attack and block like other creatures.
* Crewing does not use the stack beyond the normal activated-ability rules; the cost is paid by tapping creatures as the ability is activated.
