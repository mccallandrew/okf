# Definition

**Double strike** means the creature deals combat damage in both the first-strike combat damage step and the normal combat damage step.[^cr-702-4]

# Rules

* A creature with double strike also has first strike for rules purposes of the early damage step.
* Multiple instances of double strike are redundant.
* If the creature is removed after dealing first-strike damage, it will not deal normal combat damage.
