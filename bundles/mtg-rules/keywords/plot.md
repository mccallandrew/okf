# Definition

**Plot** — Plot is a keyword ability that functions while the card with plot is in a player’s hand.[^cr-702-170]

# Rules

* Plot is a keyword ability that functions while the card with plot is in a player’s hand.
* “Plot [cost]” means “Any time you have priority during your main phase while the stack is empty, you may exile this card from your hand and pay [cost].
* It becomes a plotted card.”
