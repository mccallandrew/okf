# Rules

* Cumulative upkeep is a triggered ability that imposes an increasing cost on a permanent.
* “Cumulative upkeep [cost]” means “At the beginning of your upkeep, if this permanent is on the battlefield, put an age counter on this permanent.
* Then you may pay [cost] for each age counter on it.
