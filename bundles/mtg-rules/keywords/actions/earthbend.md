# Definition

**Earthbend** — “Earthbend N” means “Target land you control becomes a 0/0 land creature with haste in addition to its other types.[^cr-701-66]

# Rules

* “Earthbend N” means “Target land you control becomes a 0/0 land creature with haste in addition to its other types.
* Put N +1/+1 counters on it.
* When that land dies or is put into exile, return it to the battlefield tapped under your control.”
