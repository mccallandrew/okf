# Definition

**Detain** — Certain spells and abilities can detain a permanent.[^cr-701-35]

# Rules

* Certain spells and abilities can detain a permanent.
* Until the next turn of the controller of that spell or ability, that permanent can’t attack or block and its activated abilities can’t be activated.
