# Definition

To **surveil N** means look at the top N cards of your library, then put any number of them into your graveyard and the rest on top of your library in any order.[^cr-701-25]

# Rules

* Unlike [scry](/keywords/actions/scry.md), surplus cards go to the **graveyard**, not the bottom of the library.
* Surveil 0 is not a surveil event; abilities that trigger on surveilling do not trigger.
* If an effect lets you look at additional cards while you surveil, those cards are included among the cards you may put into the graveyard or on top.
* Surveil does not draw cards; cards put on top stay in the library.
