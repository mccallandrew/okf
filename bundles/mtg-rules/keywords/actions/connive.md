# Definition

**Connive** — Certain spells and abilities instruct a permanent to connive.[^cr-701-50]

# Rules

* Certain spells and abilities instruct a permanent to connive.
* To do so, that permanent’s controller draws a card, then discards a card.
* If a nonland card is discarded this way, that player puts a +1/+1 counter on the conniving permanent.
