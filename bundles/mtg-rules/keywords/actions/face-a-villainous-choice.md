# Definition

**Face a Villainous Choice** — “[A player] faces a villainous choice — [option A], or [option B]” means “[A player] chooses [option A] or [option B].[^cr-701-55]

# Rules

* “[A player] faces a villainous choice — [option A], or [option B]” means “[A player] chooses [option A] or [option B].
* Then all actions in the chosen option are performed.”
