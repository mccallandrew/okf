# Definition

**Vote** — Some spells and abilities instruct players to vote for one choice from a list of options to determine some aspect of the effect of that spell or ability.[^cr-701-38]

# Rules

* Some spells and abilities instruct players to vote for one choice from a list of options to determine some aspect of the effect of that spell or ability.
* To vote, each player, starting with a specified player and proceeding in turn order, chooses one of those choices.
