# Definition

**Activate** — To activate an activated ability is to put it onto the stack and pay its costs, so that it will eventually resolve and have its effect.[^cr-701-2]

# Rules

* To activate an activated ability is to put it onto the stack and pay its costs, so that it will eventually resolve and have its effect.
* Only an object’s controller (or its owner, if it doesn’t have a controller) can activate its activated ability unless the object specifically says otherwise.
* A player may activate an ability if they have priority.
