# Definition

**Venture into the Dungeon** — If a player is instructed to venture into the dungeon while they don’t own a dungeon card in the command zone, they choose a dungeon card they own from outside the game and put…[^cr-701-49]

# Rules

* If a player is instructed to venture into the dungeon while they don’t own a dungeon card in the command zone, they choose a dungeon card they own from outside the game and put it into the command zone.
* They put their venture marker on the topmost room.
* See rule 309, “Dungeons.”
