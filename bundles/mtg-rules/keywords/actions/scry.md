# Definition

To **scry N** means look at the top N cards of your library, then put any number of them on the bottom of your library in any order and the rest on top in any order.[^cr-701-22]

# Rules

* Scry 0 is not a scry event; abilities that trigger on scrying do not trigger.
* When multiple players scry at once, they look simultaneously and decide order in APNAP order before cards move.
* Scry does not draw cards; the looked-at cards stay in the library.
