# Definition

**Convert** — To convert a permanent, turn it so that its other face is up.[^cr-701-28]

# Rules

* To convert a permanent, turn it so that its other face is up.
* This follows rules 701.27a–f, 712.9–10, and 712.18.
* Those rules apply to converting a permanent just as they apply to transforming a permanent.
