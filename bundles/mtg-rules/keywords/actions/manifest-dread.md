# Definition

**Manifest Dread** — “Manifest dread” means “Look at the top two cards of your library.[^cr-701-62]

# Rules

* “Manifest dread” means “Look at the top two cards of your library.
* Manifest one of them, then put the cards you looked at that were not manifested this way into your graveyard.” See rule 701.40, “Manifest.”
