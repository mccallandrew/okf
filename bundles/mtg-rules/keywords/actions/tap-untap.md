# Definition

To **tap** a permanent is to turn it sideways from upright; only untapped permanents can be tapped. To **untap** is to rotate it upright again; only tapped permanents can be untapped.[^cr-701-26]

# Rules

* Attempting to tap an already tapped permanent (or untap an untapped one) does nothing.
* Tapping is often part of costs (`{T}`) or combat (attacking, unless the creature has [vigilance](/keywords/vigilance.md)).
* The [untap step](/turn/untap-step.md) untaps permanents you control as a turn-based action (with exceptions such as phasing and "doesn't untap" effects).
