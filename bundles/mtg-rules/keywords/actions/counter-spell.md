# Definition

To **counter** a spell or ability means cancel it, removing it from the [stack](/zones/stack.md). It does not resolve, and none of its effects occur. A countered spell is put into its owner's graveyard.[^cr-701-6]

# Rules

* Costs already paid for a countered spell or ability are not refunded.
* This keyword action is unrelated to +1/+1 counters or other counter markers on permanents.
* [Ward](/keywords/ward.md) and many "counter target spell" effects use this action.
