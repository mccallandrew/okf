# Definition

**Roll to Visit Your Attractions** — To roll to visit your Attractions, roll a six-sided die.[^cr-701-52]

# Rules

* To roll to visit your Attractions, roll a six-sided die.
* Then if you control one or more Attractions with a number lit up that is equal to that result, each of those Attractions has been “visited” and its visit ability triggers.
* See rule 717, “Attraction Cards,” and rule 702.159, “Visit.”
