# Definition

**Cloak** — To cloak a card, turn it face down.[^cr-701-58]

# Rules

* To cloak a card, turn it face down.
* It becomes a 2/2 face-down creature card with ward {2}, no name, no subtypes, and no mana cost.
* Put that card onto the battlefield face down.
