# Definition

**Endure** — Certain abilities instruct a permanent to endure N.[^cr-701-63]

# Rules

* Certain abilities instruct a permanent to endure N.
* To do so, that permanent’s controller creates an N/N white Spirit creature token unless they put N +1/+1 counters on that permanent.
