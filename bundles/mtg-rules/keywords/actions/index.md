# Keyword Actions

Full Comprehensive Rules keyword actions (CR 701). Thin stubs cite the CR; evergreen and high-frequency actions may be longer.


## A

* [Abandon](abandon.md) - Only a face-up ongoing scheme card may be abandoned, and only during an Archenemy game.
* [Activate](activate.md) - To activate an activated ability is to put it onto the stack and pay its costs, so that it will eventually resolve and have its effect.
* [Adapt](adapt.md) - “Adapt N” means “If this permanent has no +1/+1 counters on it, put N +1/+1 counters on it.”
* [Airbend](airbend.md) - Certain spells and abilities instruct a player to airbend one or more permanents and/or spells.
* [Amass](amass.md) - To amass [subtype] N means “If you don’t control an Army creature, create a 0/0 black [subtype] Army creature token.
* [Assemble](assemble.md) - Assemble is a keyword action in the Unstable set that puts Contraptions onto the battlefield.
* [Attach](attach.md) - To attach an Aura, Equipment, or Fortification to an object or player means to take it from where it currently is and put it onto that object or player.

## B

* [Behold](behold.md) - “Behold a [quality]” means “Reveal a [quality] card from your hand or choose a [quality] permanent you control on the battlefield.”
* [Blight](blight.md) - To “blight N” means to put N -1/-1 counters on a creature you control.
* [Bolster](bolster.md) - “Bolster N” means “Choose a creature you control with the least toughness or tied for least toughness among creatures you control.

## C

* [Cast](cast.md) - To cast a spell is to take it from the zone it’s in (usually the hand), put it on the stack, and pay its costs, so that it will eventually resolve and have its effect.
* [Clash](clash.md) - To clash, a player reveals the top card of their library.
* [Cloak](cloak.md) - To cloak a card, turn it face down.
* [Collect Evidence](collect-evidence.md) - To “collect evidence N” means to exile any number of cards from your graveyard with total mana value N or greater.
* [Connive](connive.md) - Certain spells and abilities instruct a permanent to connive.
* [Convert](convert.md) - To convert a permanent, turn it so that its other face is up.
* [Counter](counter-spell.md) - Cancel a spell or ability on the stack so it does not resolve.
* [Create](create.md) - Put one or more tokens with specified characteristics onto the battlefield.

## D

* [Destroy](destroy.md) - Move a permanent from the battlefield to its owner's graveyard via destroy effects or lethal damage SBAs.
* [Detain](detain.md) - Certain spells and abilities can detain a permanent.
* [Discard](discard.md) - Move a card from a player's hand to that player's graveyard.
* [Discover](discover.md) - “Discover N” means “Exile cards from the top of your library until you exile a nonland card with mana value N or less.
* [Double](double.md) - Doubling a creature’s power and/or toughness creates a continuous effect.

## E

* [Earthbend](earthbend.md) - “Earthbend N” means “Target land you control becomes a 0/0 land creature with haste in addition to its other types.
* [Endure](endure.md) - Certain abilities instruct a permanent to endure N.
* [Exchange](exchange.md) - A spell or ability may instruct players to exchange something (for example, life totals or control of two permanents) as part of its resolution.
* [Exert](exert.md) - To exert a permanent, you choose to have it not untap during your next untap step.
* [Exile](exile.md) - Move an object to the exile zone.
* [Explore](explore.md) - Certain spells and abilities instruct a permanent to explore.

## F

* [Face a Villainous Choice](face-a-villainous-choice.md) - “[A player] faces a villainous choice — [option A], or [option B]” means “[A player] chooses [option A] or [option B].
* [Fateseal](fateseal.md) - To “fateseal N” means to look at the top N cards of an opponent’s library, then put any number of them on the bottom of that library in any order and the rest on top of that…
* [Fight](fight.md) - Two creatures deal damage equal to their power to each other (not combat damage).
* [Forage](forage.md) - To forage means “Exile three cards from your graveyard or sacrifice a Food.”

## G

* [Goad](goad.md) - Multiplayer-evergreen action — goaded creatures must attack each combat if able, and attack someone other than the goader if able.

## H

* [Harness](harness.md) - “Harness [this permanent]” means “If this permanent isn’t harnessed, it becomes harnessed.”

## I

* [Incubate](incubate.md) - To incubate N, create an Incubator token that enters the battlefield with N +1/+1 counters on it.
* [Investigate](investigate.md) - “Investigate” means “Create a Clue token.

## L

* [Learn](learn.md) - “Learn” means “You may discard a card.

## M

* [Manifest](manifest.md) - To manifest a card, turn it face down.
* [Manifest Dread](manifest-dread.md) - “Manifest dread” means “Look at the top two cards of your library.
* [Meld](meld.md) - Meld is a keyword action that appears in an ability on one card in a meld pair.
* [Mill](mill.md) - Put cards from the top of a library into that player's graveyard.
* [Monstrosity](monstrosity.md) - “Monstrosity N” means “If this permanent isn’t monstrous, put N +1/+1 counters on it and it becomes monstrous.”

## O

* [Open an Attraction](open-an-attraction.md) - A player may open an Attraction only during a game in which that player is playing with an Attraction deck (see rule 717, “Attraction Cards”).

## P

* [Planeswalk](planeswalk.md) - A player may planeswalk only during a Planechase game.
* [Play](play.md) - To play a land means to put it onto the battlefield from the zone it’s in (usually the hand).
* [Populate](populate.md) - To populate means to choose a creature token you control and create a token that’s a copy of that creature token.
* [Proliferate](proliferate.md) - To proliferate means to choose any number of permanents and/or players that have a counter, then give each one additional counter of each kind that permanent or player already has.

## R

* [Regenerate](regenerate.md) - If the effect of a resolving spell or ability regenerates a permanent, it creates a replacement effect that protects the permanent the next time it would be destroyed this turn.
* [Reveal](reveal.md) - To reveal a card, show that card to all players for a brief time.
* [Roll to Visit Your Attractions](roll-to-visit-your-attractions.md) - To roll to visit your Attractions, roll a six-sided die.

## S

* [Sacrifice](sacrifice.md) - Controller moves one of their permanents from the battlefield to its owner's graveyard (not destroy).
* [Scry](scry.md) - Look at the top N cards of your library and reorder them on top and/or bottom.
* [Search](search.md) - To search for a card in a zone, look at all cards in that zone (even if it’s a hidden zone) and find a card that matches the given description.
* [Set in Motion](set-in-motion.md) - Only a scheme card may be set in motion, and only during an Archenemy game.
* [Shuffle](shuffle.md) - To shuffle a library or a face-down pile of cards, randomize the cards within it so that no player knows their order.
* [Support](support.md) - “Support N” on a permanent means “Put a +1/+1 counter on each of up to N other target creatures.
* [Surveil](surveil.md) - Look at the top N cards of your library, put any number into your graveyard, and the rest on top in any order.
* [Suspect](suspect.md) - Certain spells and abilities instruct a player to suspect a creature.

## T

* [Tap and Untap](tap-untap.md) - Turn a permanent sideways (tap) or upright (untap); only the opposite status can receive each action.
* [The Ring Tempts You](the-ring-tempts-you.md) - Certain spells and abilities have the text “the Ring tempts you.” Each time the Ring tempts you, choose a creature you control.
* [Time Travel](time-travel.md) - To time travel means to choose any number of permanents you control with one or more time counters on them and/or suspended cards you own in exile with one or more time…
* [Transform](transform.md) - To transform a permanent, turn it over so that its other face is up.
* [Triple](triple.md) - Tripling a creature’s power and/or toughness creates a continuous effect.

## V

* [Venture into the Dungeon](venture-into-the-dungeon.md) - If a player is instructed to venture into the dungeon while they don’t own a dungeon card in the command zone, they choose a dungeon card they own from outside the game and put…
* [Vote](vote.md) - Some spells and abilities instruct players to vote for one choice from a list of options to determine some aspect of the effect of that spell or ability.

## W

* [Waterbend](waterbend.md) - “Waterbend [cost]” means “Pay [cost].
