# Definition

**Discover** — “Discover N” means “Exile cards from the top of your library until you exile a nonland card with mana value N or less.[^cr-701-57]

# Rules

* “Discover N” means “Exile cards from the top of your library until you exile a nonland card with mana value N or less.
* You may cast that card without paying its mana cost if the resulting spell’s mana value is less than or equal to N.
* If you don’t cast it, put that card into your hand.
