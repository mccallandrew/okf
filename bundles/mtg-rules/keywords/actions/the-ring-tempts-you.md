# Definition

**The Ring Tempts You** — Certain spells and abilities have the text “the Ring tempts you.” Each time the Ring tempts you, choose a creature you control.[^cr-701-54]

# Rules

* Certain spells and abilities have the text “the Ring tempts you.” Each time the Ring tempts you, choose a creature you control.
* That creature becomes your Ring-bearer until another creature becomes your Ring-bearer or another player gains control of it.
