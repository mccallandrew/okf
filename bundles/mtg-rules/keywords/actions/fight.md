# Definition

When creatures **fight**, each deals damage equal to its power to the other. That damage is not combat damage.[^cr-701-14]

# Rules

* If either creature is no longer on the battlefield or is no longer a creature, neither fights.
* If either is an illegal target for a resolving fight effect, neither fights.
* A creature that fights itself deals damage to itself equal to twice its power.
* Abilities that care about combat damage (such as many lifelink/combat triggers) do not see fight damage as combat damage.
