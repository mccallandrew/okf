# Definition

To **sacrifice** a permanent, its controller moves it from the battlefield directly to its owner's graveyard. A player can sacrifice only permanents they control.[^cr-701-21]

# Rules

* Sacrifice is not destruction; regeneration and [indestructible](/keywords/indestructible.md) do not prevent sacrifice.
* You cannot sacrifice something that isn't a permanent, or that you don't control.
* Sacrifice often appears as a cost; paying it requires a legal choice at the time the cost is paid.
