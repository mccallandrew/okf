# Definition

**Suspect** — Certain spells and abilities instruct a player to suspect a creature.[^cr-701-60]

# Rules

* Certain spells and abilities instruct a player to suspect a creature.
* That creature becomes suspected until it leaves the battlefield or until a spell or ability causes it to no longer be suspected.
