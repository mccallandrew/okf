# Definition

To **mill** a number of cards, a player puts that many cards from the top of their library into their graveyard.[^cr-701-17]

# Rules

* A player cannot mill more cards than are in their library; if instructed, they mill as many as possible.
* A player cannot choose to mill more cards than their library holds, and cannot pay a mill cost that exceeds the library size.
* Effects that refer to a milled card can find it in the public zone it moved to.
