# Definition

To **discard** a card, move it from its owner's hand to that player's graveyard.[^cr-701-9]

# Rules

* By default, the affected player chooses which card to discard; some effects require a random discard or let another player choose.
* If a discarded card is put into a hidden zone instead without being revealed, its characteristics are considered undefined for related cost checks.
* Discard is distinct from milling (library → graveyard) and from putting cards into the graveyard from other zones.
