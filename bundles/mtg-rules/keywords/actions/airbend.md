# Definition

**Airbend** — Certain spells and abilities instruct a player to airbend one or more permanents and/or spells.[^cr-701-65]

# Rules

* Certain spells and abilities instruct a player to airbend one or more permanents and/or spells.
* To do so, that player exiles those objects.
* For each card exiled this way, for as long as it remains exiled, its owner may cast it by paying {2} rather than paying its mana cost.
