# Definition

**Explore** — Certain spells and abilities instruct a permanent to explore.[^cr-701-44]

# Rules

* Certain spells and abilities instruct a permanent to explore.
* To do so, that permanent’s controller reveals the top card of their library.
* If a land card is revealed this way, that player puts that card into their hand.
