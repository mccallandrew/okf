# Definition

To **destroy** a permanent is to move it from the battlefield to its owner's graveyard. Destruction happens only from effects that use the word "destroy" or from state-based actions for lethal damage / deathtouch damage.[^cr-701-8]

# Rules

* Putting a permanent into a graveyard for any other reason (sacrifice, toughness 0, leave-play effects) is not destroying it.
* [Indestructible](/keywords/indestructible.md) prevents destruction; regeneration can replace a destruction event.
* Destroy is distinct from [exile](/keywords/actions/exile.md) and [sacrifice](/keywords/actions/sacrifice.md).
