# Definition

**Learn** — “Learn” means “You may discard a card.[^cr-701-48]

# Rules

* “Learn” means “You may discard a card.
* If you do, draw a card.
* If you didn’t discard a card, you may reveal a Lesson card you own from outside the game and put it into your hand.”
