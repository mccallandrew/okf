# Definition

**Protection** is a static ability written “protection from [quality].” A permanent or player with protection from a quality is shielded from sources that have that quality.[^cr-702-16]

Protection is **deciduous** (not current evergreen): still common on older and Commander cards, but not expected in every premier set.

# Rules

Memory aid **DEBT** — a permanent or player with protection from a quality:

* **D**amage — any damage that would be dealt to it by sources with that quality is prevented.
* **E**nchanted / **E**quipped — can't be enchanted by Auras, or equipped/fortified by Equipment/Fortifications, that have that quality (illegal attachments fall off via SBAs).
* **B**locked — attacking creatures with protection can't be blocked by creatures that have that quality.
* **T**argeted — can't be the target of spells with that quality or abilities from sources with that quality.

Further notes:

* The quality is usually a color, but can be any characteristic value or other stated information.
* “Protection from [A] and from [B]” and “protection from each [characteristic]” are shorthand for multiple separate protection abilities.
* Protection does not stop nontargeted effects that don't deal damage (for example “destroy all creatures”).
