# Definition

**Trample** lets a blocked attacking creature assign excess combat damage to the player, planeswalker, or battle it is attacking after assigning lethal damage to blockers.[^cr-702-19]

# Rules

* Lethal damage for assignment considers deathtouch (any nonzero damage is considered lethal for assignment purposes).
* Unblocked attackers with trample simply damage the attack destination as usual.
* Damage must be assigned to blockers in damage assignment order before excess goes through.
