# Definition

**Flying** is an evasion ability. A creature with flying can be blocked only by creatures with flying or [reach](/keywords/reach.md).[^cr-702-9]

# Rules

* Flying does not affect attacking; it only restricts blockers.
* Creatures without flying or reach cannot block a flying attacker.
* A flying creature can block both flying and nonflying attackers.
