# Definition

**Mutate** — Mutate appears on some creature cards.[^cr-702-140]

# Rules

* Mutate appears on some creature cards.
* It represents a static ability that functions while the spell with mutate is on the stack.
* “Mutate [cost]” means “You may pay [cost] rather than pay this spell’s mana cost.
