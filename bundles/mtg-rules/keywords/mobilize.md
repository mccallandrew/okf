**Mobilize** — Mobilize is a triggered ability.[^cr-702-181]

# Rules

* Mobilize is a triggered ability.
* “Mobilize N” means “Whenever this creature attacks, create N 1/1 red Warrior creature tokens.
* Those tokens enter tapped and attacking.
