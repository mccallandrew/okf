# Definition

**Recover** — Recover is a triggered ability that functions only while the card with recover is in a player’s graveyard.[^cr-702-59]

# Rules

* Recover is a triggered ability that functions only while the card with recover is in a player’s graveyard.
* “Recover [cost]” means “When a creature is put into your graveyard from the battlefield, you may pay [cost].
* If you do, return this card from your graveyard to your hand.
