# Definition

An **enchantment** is a permanent type. Auras are enchantments that enchant another object or player.[^cr-303]

# Rules

* Enchantment spells resolve onto the battlefield as enchantments.
* Auras target what they will enchant as they are cast; if the target is illegal on resolution, the Aura spell is countered.
* If an Aura is attached to an illegal object, it is put into its owner's graveyard.
