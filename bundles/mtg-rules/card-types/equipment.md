# Definition

**Equipment** is an artifact subtype that can be attached to a creature. The **equip** keyword attaches it to a creature you control; Equipment does not enter the battlefield already attached.[^cr-301-5]

# Rules

* “Equip [cost]” means attach this permanent to target creature you control; activate only as a sorcery.[^cr-702-6]
* Control of the Equipment and the equipped creature are separate; changing control of one does not change control of the other.
* If the equipped creature leaves the battlefield, stops being a creature, or otherwise becomes an illegal attachment, the Equipment becomes unattached and remains on the battlefield (state-based action).[^cr-704-5n]
* If the Equipment itself leaves the battlefield, it is no longer attached to anything.
* An Equipment that’s also a creature generally can’t equip another creature unless it has reconfigure.
