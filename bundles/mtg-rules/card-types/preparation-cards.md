# Definition

**Preparation Cards** — Cards that can be prepared (set aside) and cast later under special rules.[^cr-722]

# Rules

* Preparing is a special action or instructed action that exiles/sets aside the card.
* Casting a prepared card follows CR 722 timing and cost rules.
