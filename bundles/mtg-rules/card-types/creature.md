# Definition

A **creature** is a permanent with power and toughness that can attack and block (subject to summoning sickness and other restrictions).[^cr-302]

# Rules

* Creatures enter with [summoning sickness](/foundations/summoning-sickness.md): they cannot attack or use {T}/{Q} activated abilities unless they have haste or have been under your control continuously since your most recent turn began.
* Damage marked on a creature remains until cleanup; lethal damage destroys it (unless regeneration or indestructible applies).
* Creature spells become creatures on the battlefield when they resolve.
