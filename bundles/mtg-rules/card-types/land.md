# Definition

A **land** is a permanent type. Lands are not cast as spells under normal rules; they are played as a [special action](/turn/special-actions.md).[^cr-305]

# Rules

* Normally one land play per turn, during a main phase of your turn when the stack is empty.
* Basic lands have intrinsic mana abilities; nonbasic lands have printed abilities.
* Lands are colorless unless an effect gives them a color.
