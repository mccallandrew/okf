# Definition

A **sorcery** is a nonpermanent card type. Sorcery spells may normally be cast only during a main phase of their controller's turn when the stack is empty.[^cr-307]

# Rules

* Sorceries go to the graveyard after they resolve (unless otherwise directed).
* Effects (including flash on other cards, or "cast as though it had flash") can change timing.
* Same stack interaction as other spells once cast.
