# Definition

A **Vehicle** is an artifact subtype. Most Vehicles have **crew**, an activated ability that makes the Vehicle become an artifact creature until end of turn.[^cr-301-7]

# Rules

* A Vehicle has printed power and toughness, but those characteristics apply only while it is also a creature.
* “Crew N” means tap any number of other untapped creatures you control with total power N or greater: this permanent becomes an artifact creature until end of turn.[^cr-702-122]
* While crewed (or otherwise a creature), a Vehicle can attack and block like other creatures, subject to summoning sickness and other restrictions.
* Effects other than crew can also make a Vehicle a creature; when it becomes a creature, it immediately has its printed power and toughness (subject to continuous effects).
