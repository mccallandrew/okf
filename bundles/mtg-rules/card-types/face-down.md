# Definition

Some effects and abilities let you cast a spell or put a permanent onto the battlefield **face down**. While face down, the object has defined generic characteristics rather than its usual printed face; turning it face up later reveals its identity.[^cr-708]

This hub covers the shared face-down rules. Keyword abilities that create face-down objects (Morph, Megamorph, Disguise, Cloak, Manifest, and similar) are not each given their own page here.

# Rules

* A face-down spell on the stack or face-down permanent on the battlefield is a 2/2 creature with no name, mana cost, or creature types by default (unless the effect that made it face down says otherwise).
* You may look at face-down spells and permanents you control; you generally may not look at an opponent’s face-down objects.
* If you control multiple face-down objects, you must keep them distinguishable (order cast/entered, what ability made them face down, and so on).
* Turning a permanent face up (paying a morph/disguise cost, or another instruction) is typically a special action that doesn’t use the stack; the permanent’s full characteristics apply immediately afterward.
* Casting a card face down still follows casting rules, using the face-down casting characteristics for legality and costs.
