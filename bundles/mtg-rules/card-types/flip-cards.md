# Definition

**Flip Cards** — Cards with two sets of characteristics stacked vertically; flipping turns the card 180°.[^cr-710]

# Rules

* Only the upright half's characteristics are used while on the battlefield/stack as appropriate.
* Flipping is distinct from transforming a DFC.
