# Definition

An **instant** is a nonpermanent card type. Instant spells can be cast any time their controller has priority.[^cr-304]

# Rules

* Instants go to the graveyard after they resolve (unless otherwise directed).
* Timing is limited only by priority and any additional restrictions on the card.
* Instant speed is the baseline for reacting on the stack.
