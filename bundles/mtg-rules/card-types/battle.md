# Definition

A **battle** is a permanent with defense. Battles can be attacked; damage to a battle removes defense counters. Siege battles have additional rules about who protects them and transforming when defeated.[^cr-310]

# Rules

* Battles enter with defense counters equal to their defense.
* A battle with no defense counters is put into its owner's graveyard (sieges may exile and transform instead).
* Attacking battles follows combat rules similar to attacking planeswalkers, with siege-specific controller rules.
