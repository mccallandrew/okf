# Definition

A **planeswalker** is a permanent with loyalty. Loyalty abilities add or remove loyalty counters; a planeswalker with 0 loyalty is put into its owner's graveyard.[^cr-306]

# Rules

* Planeswalkers enter with loyalty counters equal to their printed loyalty.
* Normally one loyalty ability per planeswalker per turn, any time you could cast a sorcery.
* Damage dealt to a planeswalker causes that many loyalty counters to be removed.
* Planeswalkers can be attacked in combat.
