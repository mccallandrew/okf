# Definition

An **adventurer card** has two sets of characteristics on one card: a permanent (usually a creature) and an **Adventure** — an instant or sorcery you may cast instead. After the Adventure resolves, the card is exiled "on an adventure" and may later be cast as the permanent.[^cr-715]

# Rules

* You choose whether to cast the card as its Adventure or as its non-Adventure characteristics when casting it from your hand (and from other zones if permitted).
* While resolving, an Adventure spell uses only the Adventure's characteristics; the permanent half is ignored for that cast.
* As an Adventure spell resolves, its controller exiles it rather than putting it into their graveyard; it is "exiled on an adventure."
* While exiled on an adventure, the owner may cast the card as its permanent half (paying that half's cost) if rules and effects allow casting from exile that way.
* Effects that refer to "casting a spell" see whichever set of characteristics was chosen for that cast.
