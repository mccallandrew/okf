# Definition

An **artifact** is a permanent type. Artifacts are colorless by default (unless they have a color indicator or colored mana cost that gives them color).[^cr-301]

# Rules

* Artifact spells are cast like other permanent spells and become artifacts on the battlefield when they resolve.
* Artifact creatures are both artifacts and creatures.
* Equipment and Vehicles are artifact subtypes with additional rules.
