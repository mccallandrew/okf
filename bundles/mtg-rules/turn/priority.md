# Definition

**Priority** is permission to cast a spell, activate an ability, or take a special action. Only one player has priority at a time.[^cr-117]

# Rules

* Active player receives priority first in a step or phase after turn-based actions and triggers are handled.
* After a spell or ability is put on the stack, the player who put it there receives priority again.
* When a player passes, the next player in turn order receives priority.
* When all players pass in succession, the top object of the stack resolves — or if the stack is empty, the step or phase ends.
