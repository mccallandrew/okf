# Turn Structure

* [Turn](turn.md) - The structure of a Magic turn — phases and steps in order.
* [Beginning Phase](beginning-phase.md) - First phase of the turn — untap, upkeep, and draw steps.
* [Untap Step](untap-step.md) - Step where the active player untaps permanents; no priority.
* [Upkeep Step](upkeep-step.md) - Step after untap where upkeep triggers occur and players get priority.
* [Draw Step](draw-step.md) - Step where the active player draws a card, then players get priority.
* [Main Phase](main-phase.md) - Phase where the active player may cast most spells and play a land.
* [Ending Phase](ending-phase.md) - Final phase of the turn — end step then cleanup step.
* [End Step](end-step.md) - Step where "until end of turn" effects are still active and end-of-turn triggers fire.
* [Cleanup Step](cleanup-step.md) - Step where hand size is enforced, damage is removed, and "until end of turn" effects end.
* [Priority](priority.md) - The system that determines who may cast spells or activate abilities.
* [Special Actions](special-actions.md) - Actions that do not use the stack, such as playing a land.
