# Definition

The **upkeep step** follows untap. "At the beginning of your upkeep" triggers go on the stack, then players receive priority.[^cr-503]

# Rules

* Triggered abilities waiting from the start of upkeep are put on the stack.
* Players may cast instants and activate abilities while they have priority.
* When the stack empties and all players pass, the game proceeds to the draw step.
