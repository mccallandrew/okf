# Definition

**Special actions** are actions a player may take that do not use the stack (for example, playing a land). They require priority but cannot be responded to with the stack.[^cr-116]

# Common examples

* Playing a land (once per turn under normal rules, during a main phase of your turn with empty stack).
* Turning a face-down creature face up (morph/manifest-style costs).
* Suspending a card / other effect-defined special actions.
