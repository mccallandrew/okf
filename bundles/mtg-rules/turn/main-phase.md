# Definition

A **main phase** is when the active player may cast sorceries and most permanent spells, and may play a land. Each turn has a precombat and a postcombat main phase.[^cr-505]

# Rules

* Sorceries and most non-instant spells can be cast only during a main phase of the active player's turn when the stack is empty (unless an effect says otherwise, e.g. flash).
* Playing a land is a special action; one land per turn under normal rules.
* Players receive priority during main phases.
