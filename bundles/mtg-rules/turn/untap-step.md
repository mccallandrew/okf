# Definition

In the **untap step**, the active player untaps all permanents they control (unless an effect prevents untapping). Players do not receive priority during this step.[^cr-502]

# Rules

* Phasing happens at the start of the untap step (if relevant).
* No one gets priority; the game moves to upkeep after untapping.
* Effects that say "does not untap during its controller's untap step" apply here.
