# Definition

Each player has a **life total**. Starting life is normally 20 in Constructed (40 in Commander). A player with 0 or less life loses the game as a state-based action.[^cr-119]

# Rules

* Damage to a player causes loss of life equal to the damage.
* Effects can cause a player to gain or lose life without dealing damage.
* Paying life is a cost; it is not the same as being dealt damage.
* Lifelink causes its controller to gain life equal to damage dealt.
