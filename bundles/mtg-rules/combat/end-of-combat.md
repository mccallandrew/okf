# Definition

The **end of combat step** is the last step of the combat phase. “At end of combat” triggers fire as it begins, players get priority, and when the step ends all attacking and blocking creatures (and attacked planeswalkers and battles) leave combat.[^cr-511]

# Rules

* This step has no turn-based actions; once it begins, the active player gets priority.
* Abilities that trigger “at end of combat” trigger as the step begins.
* Effects that last “until end of combat” expire at the end of the combat phase (after this step finishes).
* As soon as the step ends, all creatures, battles, and planeswalkers are removed from combat; the postcombat main phase then begins.
