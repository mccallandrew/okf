# Definition

In the **declare attackers step**, the active player chooses which creatures attack and declares what each attacks (a player, planeswalker, or battle). Attacking creatures become tapped unless they have vigilance.[^cr-508]

# Rules

* Only untapped creatures that could attack (no summoning sickness, etc.) may be declared as attackers.
* Attacking is optional unless an effect requires it.
* After attackers are declared, "when this creature attacks" triggers go on the stack; then players get priority.
* If no attackers are declared, the declare blockers and combat damage steps are skipped.
