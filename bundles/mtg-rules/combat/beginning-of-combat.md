# Definition

The **beginning of combat step** opens the combat phase. “At the beginning of combat” triggers go on the stack, then players get priority — the last window to act before attackers are declared.[^cr-507]

# Rules

* In some multiplayer games, the active player chooses a defending player as a turn-based action that does not use the stack.
* Abilities that trigger at the beginning of combat trigger as this step begins and are put on the stack before priority.
* After the stack empties and all players pass, the game proceeds to the declare attackers step.
* Common uses of priority here include tapping potential attackers, removing creatures from combat readiness, or otherwise changing who can attack.
