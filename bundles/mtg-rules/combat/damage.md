# Definition

**Damage** is a quantity dealt to a creature, planeswalker, battle, or player. Results depend on what receives it.[^cr-120]

# Results

| Recipient | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Creature | Mark damage; lethal toughness → destroy (state-based) |
| Planeswalker | Remove that many loyalty counters |
| Battle | Remove that many defense counters |
| Player | Lose that much life |

# Rules

* Damage can be combat or noncombat; both use the same damage rules unless an effect says otherwise.
* Prevention and replacement effects can modify damage; see [Prevention Effects](/effects/prevention-effects.md).
* Marked damage on creatures clears in cleanup.
