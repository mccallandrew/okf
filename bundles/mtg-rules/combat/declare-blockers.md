# Definition

In the **declare blockers step**, the defending player chooses which untapped creatures they control block which attacking creatures.[^cr-509]

# Rules

* A creature can normally block only one attacker; multiple blockers may block one attacker.
* Evasion abilities (flying, etc.) restrict which creatures can block.
* After blockers are declared, triggers go on the stack; then players get priority.
* How damage is divided among multiple blockers is decided in the combat damage step — see [Multi-Blocker Damage](/combat/multi-blocker-damage.md).
