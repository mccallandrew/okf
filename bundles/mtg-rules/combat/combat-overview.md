# Definition

The **combat phase** is when the active player may attack with creatures. It has steps: beginning of combat, declare attackers, declare blockers, combat damage, and end of combat.[^cr-506]

# Steps

1. [Beginning of Combat](/combat/beginning-of-combat.md) — triggers; priority
2. [Declare Attackers](/combat/declare-attackers.md)
3. [Declare Blockers](/combat/declare-blockers.md)
4. [Combat Damage](/combat/combat-damage.md) (first/double strike may create extra damage steps; see [Multi-Blocker Damage](/combat/multi-blocker-damage.md))
5. [End of Combat](/combat/end-of-combat.md) — triggers; priority
