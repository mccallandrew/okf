# Definition

**Summoning sickness** is the informal name for the rule that a creature cannot attack or activate abilities with {T} or {Q} in their cost unless its controller has controlled it continuously since the beginning of their most recent turn.[^cr-302-6]

# Rules

* The restriction applies to creatures (and to Vehicles/other permanents while they are creatures).
* [Haste](/keywords/haste.md) lets a creature ignore this restriction.
* The creature can still block, and can still activate abilities that do not require {T} or {Q}.
* Changing controllers restarts the clock: the new controller must wait until they have controlled it since the start of their most recent turn (unless it has haste).
* "Summoning sickness" is not a keyword or status marker; it is simply whether the continuous-control condition is met.
