# Definition

The Golden Rules sit above other rules text and decide how conflicts resolve.[^cr-101]

# Rules

1. **Card text wins over CR** when they conflict (except when a card specifically says otherwise).
2. **Can't beats can** — if one effect says something can't happen and another says it can, the "can't" wins.
3. **Specific beats general** — more specific instructions override broader ones.
4. **Action legality is checked when the action is taken** — illegality later does not rewind a legal action.
