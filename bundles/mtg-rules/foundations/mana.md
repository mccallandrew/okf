# Definition

**Mana** is the resource used to pay costs. Players add mana to their mana pool and spend it; unspent mana empties as steps and phases end (with limited exceptions).[^cr-106]

# Rules

* Mana has colors (white, blue, black, red, green) or is colorless.
* Mana abilities produce mana and usually do not use the stack.
* Lands typically produce mana via activated mana abilities (e.g., tapping a basic land).
* Paying a mana cost requires spending mana of the appropriate types from the pool.
