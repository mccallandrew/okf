# Definition

**Ending Turns and Phases** — Effects that end the turn or phase, skipping remaining steps.[^cr-724]

# Rules

* Ending the turn exiles objects on the stack and skips to cleanup as specified.
* Ending a phase skips remaining steps of that phase.
