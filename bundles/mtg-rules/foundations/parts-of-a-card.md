# Definition

**Parts of a card** are the printed elements of a Magic card — name, mana cost, illustration, color indicator, type line, expansion symbol, text box, power/toughness, loyalty, defense, and information below the text box.[^cr-200]

# Rules

* Some parts are also characteristics of the object; others (illustration, expansion symbol, collector info) generally have no rules meaning.
* Tokens and copies have only the parts that are also characteristics.
* See also mana symbols and numbers under Numbers and Symbols (CR 107).
