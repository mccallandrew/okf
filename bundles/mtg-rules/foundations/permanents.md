# Definition

A **permanent** is an object on the [battlefield](/zones/battlefield.md). Permanents include artifacts, creatures, enchantments, lands, planeswalkers, and battles (among others).[^cr-110]

# Rules

* A permanent enters the battlefield under a player's control (usually the player who put it there).
* Permanents can be tapped or untapped; many costs and abilities require tapping.
* When a permanent leaves the battlefield, it becomes a different object if it moves to another zone (except for certain replacement effects).
* Tokens are permanents while on the battlefield but cease to exist if they leave.
