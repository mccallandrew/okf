# Definition

Effects may instruct a player to **roll a die** (including the planar die in Planechase). The CR defines faces, results, and modifiers.[^cr-706]

# Rules

* Unless specified, a die roll uses a fair die of the stated type.
* Results can be modified by replacement effects.
* The planar die has special Planeswalk and Chaos faces.
