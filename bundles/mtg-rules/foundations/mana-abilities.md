# Definition

A **mana ability** is an activated or triggered ability that could produce [mana](/foundations/mana.md), is not a loyalty ability, and (for activated abilities) does not target. Mana abilities do **not** use the [stack](/zones/stack.md).[^cr-605]

# Rules

* When you activate a mana ability, you pay its cost and it resolves immediately — opponents cannot respond to the activation itself.
* Triggered mana abilities also skip the stack; they resolve the next time a player would receive priority without being put on the stack.
* You can activate mana abilities while casting a spell or activating another ability, during the process of paying costs.
* An ability that produces mana but targets (or is a loyalty ability) is **not** a mana ability and uses the stack like a normal activated or triggered ability.

# Contrast with other activated abilities

| | Mana ability | Typical activated ability |
|-|--------------|---------------------------|
| Uses the stack? | No | Yes |
| Can be responded to? | Not the ability itself | Yes, while on the stack |
| Timing while paying costs | Allowed | Generally not |
