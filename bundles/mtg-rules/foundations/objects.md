# Definition

An **object** is an ability on the stack, a card, a copy of a card, a token, a spell, a permanent, or an emblem.[^cr-109]

# Rules

* Objects exist in [zones](/zones/zone.md).
* Characteristics of an object (name, mana cost, type, etc.) depend on its current zone and any continuous effects.
* Cards in a library, hand, or closed-faced zone are usually face down and have no characteristics visible to opponents unless an effect reveals them.
