# Definition

Magic has five **colors**: white, blue, black, red, and green. An object's color is a [characteristic](/foundations/characteristics.md) used by costs, effects, and deck-building rules such as Commander color identity.[^cr-105]

# Rules

* An object is the color(s) of the colored mana symbols in its mana cost, unless a color indicator or effect says otherwise.[^cr-202]
* An object with no colored mana symbols in its mana cost (and no color indicator or color-setting effect) is **colorless**.
* Multicolored objects have more than one color; effects that care about "a color" usually mean one chosen color unless they say otherwise.
* Lands are typically colorless even when they produce colored mana; producing a color does not make the land that color.
* Effects can change, add, or remove color; color is applied in the color layer of the [layer system](/effects/layers.md).
