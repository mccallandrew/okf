# Definition

A **player** is a participant in a Magic game. In a two-player game each player is the other's opponent; multiplayer games may define teams and additional relationships.[^cr-102]

# Rules

* Each player has zones (library, hand, graveyard, and so on) and a life total.
* The **active player** is the player whose turn it is.
* Players take turns in order; priority and APNAP order matter for simultaneous choices.
* A player who cannot make a required choice or who loses all life (or meets other lose conditions) may leave the game.
