# Definition

Some spells and abilities instruct a player to **flip a coin**. The CR defines how outcomes are chosen and what happens if a flip is replaced or rerolled.[^cr-705]

# Rules

* A coin flip has two outcomes unless an effect says otherwise.
* Effects may replace or modify coin flips.
* Related randomizing instructions include rolling dice.
