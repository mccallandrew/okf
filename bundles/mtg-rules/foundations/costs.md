# Definition

A **cost** is what a player must pay to take an action such as casting a spell or activating an ability.[^cr-118]

# Rules

* Costs may include mana, tapping, sacrificing, paying life, discarding, and other actions.
* A player cannot pay a cost they cannot afford; partial payment is not allowed.
* Alternative and additional costs can modify what is paid to cast a spell.
* Mana costs are paid with mana from the mana pool; see [Mana](/foundations/mana.md).
