# Definition

A **spell** is a card on the [stack](/zones/stack.md), or a copy of a spell. Instant and sorcery cards are always spells while on the stack; permanent cards are spells while on the stack and become permanents when they resolve.[^cr-112]

# Rules

* Casting puts a spell on the stack; see [Casting Spells](/stack-and-priority/casting-spells.md).
* Spells have targets if their text uses "target."
* When a spell resolves, its instructions happen; a permanent spell becomes a permanent on the battlefield.
* If a spell is countered or otherwise leaves the stack without resolving, it usually goes to the graveyard (unless it is a copy or a token spell).
