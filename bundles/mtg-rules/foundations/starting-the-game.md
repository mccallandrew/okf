# Definition

**Starting the game** covers setup before the first turn: determining who goes first, setting life totals, drawing opening hands, and taking mulligans.[^cr-103]

# Steps

1. Players shuffle their decks; each player's deck becomes their [library](/zones/library.md).
2. Players determine a randomly selected starting player (or as the format specifies).
3. Each player's starting [life](/combat/life.md) total is set (normally 20 in Constructed; 40 in Commander and many multiplayer variants).
4. Each player draws an opening hand (normally seven cards).
5. In turn order beginning with the starting player, each player may take a **mulligan**.
6. After mulligans, the starting player's first [turn](/turn/turn.md) begins (that player skips the draw step of their first turn in a two-player game).

# Mulligan summary

* Players declare mulligans in turn order; those who mulligan do so simultaneously.
* To mulligan: shuffle the hand into the library, draw a new hand of starting-hand size (normally seven), then put N of those cards on the bottom of the library, where N is the number of mulligans taken so far (including this one).
* Once a player declines to mulligan, their remaining cards are their opening hand.
* In multiplayer (and Brawl), the first mulligan does not count toward that put-on-bottom number.
