# Definition

An **attachment** is an Aura or Equipment (or similar) that is attached to another object or player. Attachment is a game relationship on the [battlefield](/zones/battlefield.md); it is not the same as being stacked under or representing the same object.[^cr-303][^cr-301]

# Auras

* An [Aura](/card-types/aura.md) spell targets what it will enchant as it is cast.
* When the Aura spell resolves, it enters attached to that object or player; if the target is illegal, the spell is countered on resolution.
* If an Aura is attached to something illegal for it, a [state-based action](/foundations/state-based-actions.md) puts it into its owner's [graveyard](/zones/graveyard.md).
* Moving an Aura usually requires an effect that attaches it or returns it to the battlefield attached to something.

# Equipment

* [Equipment](/card-types/equipment.md) enters unattached unless an effect says otherwise.
* The equip keyword is an activated ability: pay the equip cost to attach the Equipment to a creature you control (sorcery timing).
* Equipment that is attached to an illegal permanent (or whose creature leaves) becomes unattached; it stays on the battlefield.
* Reconfigure and similar abilities are alternate ways some Equipment attach or unattach.
