# Definition

**Numbers and symbols** cover how Magic represents quantities and mana in costs and rules text, including colored mana symbols, generic mana, hybrid, Phyrexian, snow, and the variable **X**.[^cr-107]

# Rules

* Mana symbols in a mana cost contribute to color and mana value as defined in CR 202.
* X is a placeholder determined as the spell or ability is cast/activated (or as otherwise specified).
* Symbol meanings are fixed by the CR; reminder text may summarize but does not redefine them.
