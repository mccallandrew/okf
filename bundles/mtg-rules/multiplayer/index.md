# Multiplayer

CR 8xx multiplayer options and variants:

* [Multiplayer Overview](multiplayer-overview.md) - Hub for CR 8xx multiplayer options and variants.
* [APNAP](apnap.md) - Active Player, Nonactive Player order for simultaneous choices and triggers.
* [Attacking Multiple Players](attacking-multiple-players.md) - Declaring attackers against different opponents, planeswalkers, or battles.
* [Limited Range of Influence](limited-range-of-influence.md) - Optional rule limiting how far away a player can affect others by seat.
* [Attack Left and Attack Right](attack-left-and-right.md) - Options restricting whom you may attack by seating direction.
* [Deploy Creatures](deploy-creatures.md) - Option allowing creatures to move to teammates' control under shared rules.
* [Shared Team Turns](shared-team-turns.md) - Option where teammates take turns simultaneously as a team.
* [Free-for-All](free-for-all.md) - Default multiplayer variant: each player for themselves.
* [Grand Melee](grand-melee.md) - Large multiplayer variant with range of influence and attack left/right.
* [Team vs. Team](team-vs-team.md) - Multiplayer variant with two or more teams.
* [Emperor](emperor.md) - Team variant with emperors and generals and range of influence.
* [Two-Headed Giant](two-headed-giant.md) - Team multiplayer variant (CR 810): shared turns and shared life.
* [Alternating Teams](alternating-teams.md) - Team variant with alternating team turns.
* [Commander Overview](commander-overview.md) - Casual-but-ubiquitous Commander rules (CR 903).
