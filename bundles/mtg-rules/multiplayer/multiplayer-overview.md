# Definition

A **multiplayer** game begins with more than two players. CR 8xx adds optional rules and named variants on top of the normal two-player rules.[^cr-800]

# Rules

* Options (for example limited range of influence, attack multiple players, shared team turns) may combine; a game uses at most one multiplayer *variant*.[^cr-800]
* Games can continue after a player leaves; objects and effects clean up per CR 800.4.[^cr-800]
* **Range of influence** (optional, CR 801) limits who and what a player can affect by seat distance — common in large games and required in Emperor.[^cr-801]
* **Attack multiple players** (optional, CR 802) lets the active player assign attackers to different opponents (or their planeswalkers/battles) in one combat.[^cr-802]
* Simultaneous choices and triggers among players use [APNAP](/multiplayer/apnap.md) order.
* Default Free-for-All multiplayer often pairs with attack-multiple-players and no limited range of influence (see individual variants).
