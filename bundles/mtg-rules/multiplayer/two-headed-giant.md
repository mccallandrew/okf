# Definition

**Two-Headed Giant** (THG) is a multiplayer *variant* under CR 810: two teams of two players each, using shared team turns and a shared team life total.[^cr-810]

# Rules

* Each team sits together; teammates choose their seating order within the team.[^cr-810]
* The variant always uses the shared team turns option (CR 805): teams take turns, not individual players.[^cr-810][^cr-805]
* Each team starts at **30** life (shared). Damage, life loss, and life gain apply to players individually but change the shared total.[^cr-810]
* Hands, mana, libraries, and other resources are not shared; teammates may review each other's hands and discuss strategy, but may not manipulate each other's cards or permanents.[^cr-810]
* The team that plays first skips the draw step of its first turn.[^cr-810]
* Players win or lose only as a team.[^cr-810]
* Combat follows shared-team-turns combat rules (CR 805.10).[^cr-810]
