# Definition

The **attack multiple players** option (CR 802) lets the active player attack more than one opponent during a single combat phase. Each attacking creature is still assigned one attack target: a player, a planeswalker that player controls, or a battle that player protects.[^cr-802]

# Rules

* When the option is in use, all of the attacking player's opponents are defending players for that combat; the attacker need not pick a single defending player up front.[^cr-802]
* As each attacker is declared, its controller chooses which defending player, planeswalker, or battle it attacks.[^cr-802][^cr-508]
* Attacking only one opponent in a given combat is still legal under the option.[^cr-802]
* If more than one player (or their planeswalkers/battles) is being attacked, defending players declare blockers in [APNAP](/multiplayer/apnap.md) order; each may block only creatures attacking them (or their PW/battle).[^cr-802]
* Combat damage assignment among defending sides also follows APNAP.[^cr-802]
* Without this option, a multiplayer combat has a single chosen defending player (creatures may still attack that player's planeswalkers or battles).[^cr-508]
* Limited range of influence further restricts legal attack targets by seat distance. See [Multiplayer Overview](/multiplayer/multiplayer-overview.md).
