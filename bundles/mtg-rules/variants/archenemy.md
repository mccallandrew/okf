# Definition

**Archenemy** (CR 904) pits a single **archenemy** player — strengthened by scheme cards — against a team of opposing players.[^cr-904]

# Rules

* Default setup is Team vs. Team (CR 808) with exactly two teams, using attack multiple players and shared team turns; no other multiplayer options.[^cr-904]
* One team is a single player designated the archenemy; the other team has one or more players.[^cr-904]
* The archenemy needs a scheme deck of at least twenty scheme cards (no more than two of any English name). Schemes stay in the [command zone](/zones/command.md).[^cr-904]
* Archenemy starting life is **40**; each other player starts at **20**.[^cr-904]
* The archenemy takes the first turn (not a random starting player).[^cr-904]
* Face-up scheme abilities function from the command zone; the scheme's owner controls it.[^cr-904]
* Shared team turns (CR 805) apply to how the opposing team acts together.[^cr-805]
