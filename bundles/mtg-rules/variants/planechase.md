# Definition

**Planechase** (CR 901) adds plane and phenomenon cards that modify the game from the [command zone](/zones/command.md), plus a planar die used to chaos or planeswalk.[^cr-901]

# Rules

* Playable as two-player or multiplayer; default multiplayer is Free-for-All with attack multiple players and without limited range of influence.[^cr-901]
* Each player needs a planar deck of at least ten plane and/or phenomenon cards (at most two phenomena; unique English names). The game needs one planar die.[^cr-901]
* Plane and phenomenon cards remain in the command zone (in the planar deck or face up).[^cr-901]
* After opening hands are kept, the starting player reveals planes until a plane card is face up; that is the starting plane (phenomena revealed this way go to the bottom without triggering).[^cr-901]
* The **planar controller** is normally the active player; face-up plane/phenomenon abilities function from the command zone.[^cr-901]
* Planar die faces include the Planeswalker symbol and the chaos symbol; blank faces do nothing. (Full roll timing and planeswalk procedure are in CR 901 — not fully ingested here.)
