# Definition

**Draft** and **Sealed** are Limited formats: players build decks from cards opened for that event rather than from a preconstructed collection. Detailed event structure lives primarily in the Magic Tournament Rules, not the Comprehensive Rules.[^mtr][^cr]

# Rules

* **Sealed Deck**: each player opens a fixed product pool (for example six boosters), builds a minimum-size deck from those cards plus basic lands as allowed by the event, and plays with that deck.
* **Booster Draft**: players open packs in sequence, pick cards into a private pool, pass the remainder, then build decks from their drafted cards (plus basic lands as allowed).
* Minimum deck size and sideboard rules for tournament Limited are defined by the Tournament Rules / format announcements, not fully restated here.[^mtr]
* The CR casual section includes **Conspiracy Draft** (CR 905) as a draft-adjacent variant with conspiracy cards; general Booster Draft/Sealed procedure is tournament documentation.[^cr-905][^cr-900]
* Once play begins, games use normal Magic rules (and any multiplayer options if the event is multiplayer).
