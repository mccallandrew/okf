# Effects

* [One-Shot Effects](one-shot-effects.md) - Effects that do something once when they apply, then are done.
* [Continuous Effects](continuous-effects.md) - Effects that modify characteristics or rules for a duration.
* [Layers](layers.md) - Ordered system for applying continuous effects to characteristics.
* [Replacement Effects](replacement-effects.md) - Effects that replace one event with a modified event.
* [Prevention Effects](prevention-effects.md) - Effects that prevent some or all damage that would be dealt.
* [Text-Changing Effects](text-changing-effects.md) - Continuous effects that change the text of an object.
* [Replacement and Prevention Interaction](replacement-prevention-interaction.md) - How multiple replacement and/or prevention effects apply to the same event.
