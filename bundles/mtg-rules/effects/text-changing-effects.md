# Definition

**Text-changing effects** modify an object's rules text (for example changing a color word or creature type word in text).[^cr-612]

# Rules

* Text-changing effects are applied in the text-changing layer before type-changing effects that depend on the new text where applicable.
* They change the text that is considered to be on the card for rules purposes.
* They do not usually change the name printed in the name line unless they say so.
