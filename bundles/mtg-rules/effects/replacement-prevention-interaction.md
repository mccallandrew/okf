# Definition

When multiple **replacement and/or prevention effects** could apply to the same event, CR 616 determines which applies first and how the event is reprocessed.[^cr-616]

# Rules

* Affected players or controllers often choose the order when more than one effect could apply.
* Self-replacement effects may take precedence as specified in CR 616.
* After one effect applies, the event may be modified and other effects may apply to the new event.
