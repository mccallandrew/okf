# Definition

A **prevention effect** stops some or all damage that would be dealt. Prevention is a form of replacement applied to damage events.[^cr-615]

# Rules

* Prevention does not use the stack; it applies when damage would be dealt.
* "Prevent the next N damage" effects track remaining prevention.
* Prevention can be partial; unprevented damage is still dealt.
* Interaction with other replacements follows replacement-effect ordering rules.
