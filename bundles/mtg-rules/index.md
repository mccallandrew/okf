# Magic: The Gathering Rules

Concept graph for Magic rules. Concepts are organized by idea, not by Comprehensive Rules number; CR citations live in frontmatter `sources`.

This bundle aims for Comprehensive Rules topic coverage: foundations and section hubs, full CR 701 keyword actions and CR 702 keyword abilities (thin stubs), a CR Glossary mirror, effects, multiplayer options, and casual variants. It cites the CR rather than reproducing it. Intentionally out of scope: tournament policy / MTR.
