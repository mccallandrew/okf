# Definition

The **battlefield** is the shared zone where [permanents](/foundations/permanents.md) exist and interact.[^cr-403]

# Rules

* Permanents enter the battlefield (ETB) and leave the battlefield (LTB).
* Tokens exist only on the battlefield; if a token would leave, it ceases to exist.
* Combat and most permanent abilities apply while objects are on the battlefield.
* Permanents have controllers; control can change without changing ownership.
