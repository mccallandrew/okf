# Definition

The **command** zone holds certain objects that are part of the game but not in a player's deck — notably emblems, and in casual formats commanders, planes, schemes, and similar cards.[^cr-408]

# Rules

* [Emblems](/foundations/emblems.md) exist in the command zone and have abilities but are not permanents or cards.
* Command-zone cards may have special cast or play rules defined by their format.
* Moving between command and other zones is format- and effect-specific.
