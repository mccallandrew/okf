# Definition

Each player has a **hand** — a hidden zone holding cards that player has drawn (or otherwise put there) and may cast or play from.[^cr-402]

# Rules

* Cards in hand are face down to opponents; the owner may look at their own hand.
* Casting usually moves a card from hand to the stack.
* Maximum hand size is normally seven; discard to hand size happens in the cleanup step.
* Effects can force discards or reveal cards in hand.
