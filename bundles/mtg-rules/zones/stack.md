# Definition

The **stack** is the shared zone that holds spells and abilities waiting to resolve. Last in, first out (LIFO).[^cr-405]

# Rules

* Newly cast spells and put abilities go on top of the stack.
* Players receive priority and may respond before the top object resolves.
* When all players pass in succession, the top object of the stack resolves.
* Mana abilities generally do not use the stack.
