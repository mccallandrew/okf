# Definition

A **zone** is a place where objects can be during a game. Moving between zones is a zone-change; many abilities trigger on entering or leaving a zone.[^cr-400]

# Public vs. hidden

* Public zones (battlefield, stack, graveyard, exile, command) are generally visible.
* Hidden zones (library, hand) keep card faces private unless revealed.
