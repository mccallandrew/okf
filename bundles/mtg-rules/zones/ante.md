# Definition

The **ante** zone is used only in ante games. Each player may put the top card of their library into the ante zone as a wager.[^cr-407]

# Rules

* Ante is obsolete in mainstream play and banned under current tournament policy; the CR still defines the zone.
* Cards in ante are owned by their owners but are not in any player's deck during the game.
