# Definition

Each player has a **graveyard** — an ordered, public zone for cards that have been discarded, destroyed, milled, or otherwise put there.[^cr-404]

# Rules

* Cards in a graveyard are face up and public.
* Order matters for some effects; cards are usually put on top in the order they arrive.
* Many abilities care about cards in graveyards (flashback, reanimation, etc.).
* Tokens that would go to a graveyard cease to exist instead.
