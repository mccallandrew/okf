# Definition

When all players pass with objects on the stack, the **top object resolves**: its instructions are followed (or a permanent spell becomes a permanent).[^cr-608]

# Rules

* Check legality of targets on resolution; illegal targets can cause the spell/ability to be countered or do nothing as appropriate.
* Instant and sorcery spells go to the graveyard after resolving (unless otherwise directed).
* Permanent spells enter the battlefield under the control of the spell's controller.
* After resolution, the active player receives priority.
