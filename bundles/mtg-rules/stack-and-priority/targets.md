# Definition

A **target** is an object, player, or zone chosen when casting a spell or putting an ability on the stack, when the text uses the word "target."[^cr-115]

# Rules

* Targets are chosen as the spell is cast or the ability is put on the stack — not on resolution (except for delayed choices the effect creates).
* A spell or ability is illegal to cast/activate if it requires targets and none are legal.
* On resolution, if all targets are illegal, the spell/ability does not resolve (is countered for spells); if some remain legal, it affects only remaining legal targets.
* Hexproof, shroud, and protection can make objects illegal targets.
