# Definition

A spell or ability is **modal** if it instructs a player to choose among two or more options (modes), typically a bulleted list after "Choose one —" or similar. Each chosen mode is part of what the spell or ability does.[^cr-700-2]

# Rules

* Modes for a modal spell or activated ability are chosen while casting or activating, before targets for those modes are chosen.[^cr-700-2]
* Modes for a modal triggered ability are chosen when the ability is put on the stack.
* A mode that would be illegal (for example, no legal targets) cannot be chosen.
* You normally cannot choose the same mode more than once unless the card says you may.
* Changing targets later cannot change the chosen mode(s); a copy of a modal spell or ability copies the chosen modes and its controller cannot pick different ones.
* Targeting requirements can differ per mode; only targets for the chosen mode(s) are required.
