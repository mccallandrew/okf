# Definition

**Activating** an activated ability announces it, makes required choices (including targets), and pays its cost. Most activated abilities use the stack; mana abilities usually do not.[^cr-602]

# Outline

1. Announce the ability; put it on the stack (unless it is a mana ability).
2. Choose modes, targets, and other required choices.
3. Determine and pay the activation cost.
4. Ability becomes activated; activating player receives priority.
