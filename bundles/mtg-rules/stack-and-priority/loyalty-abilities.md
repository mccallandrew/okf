# Definition

**Loyalty abilities** are activated abilities with a loyalty symbol in the cost. They add or remove loyalty counters and follow special activation restrictions.[^cr-606]

# Rules

* You may activate a loyalty ability of a permanent you control only any time you could cast a sorcery, and only if you haven't activated a loyalty ability of that permanent this turn.
* The cost's loyalty symbol adds or removes the indicated number of loyalty counters as a cost.
* Loyalty abilities use the stack like other activated abilities.
