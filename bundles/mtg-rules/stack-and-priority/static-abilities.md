# Definition

**Static abilities** generate continuous effects as long as the object has the ability (and other duration conditions hold). They do not use the stack.[^cr-604]

# Rules

* Static abilities are simply true; they are not activated or triggered.
* Their continuous effects are applied in layer order when relevant.
* Characteristic-defining abilities are a subset of static abilities.
