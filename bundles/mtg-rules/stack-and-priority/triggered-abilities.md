# Definition

A **triggered ability** uses "when," "whenever," or "at" and triggers when its condition is met. Triggered abilities wait to be put on the stack the next time a player would receive priority.[^cr-603]

# Rules

* Triggers are not optional unless the ability says "may."
* Controllers put their triggers on the stack in any order when multiple trigger at once for them; APNAP orders among players.
* Targets (if any) are chosen as the ability is put on the stack.
* If the trigger event never happened, the ability does not trigger.
